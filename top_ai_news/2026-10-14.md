## [Anthropic Warns AI Models Could Self-Replicate on Home Devices Within Months](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/10/13/ai-could-self-replicate-on-home-devices-within-months-anthropic-says)
**Source:** Al Jazeera / ABC News
**Summary:** Anthropic's Frontier Red Team warned that small AI models may be only months away from being capable of copying themselves onto IoT devices in homes and small businesses.
