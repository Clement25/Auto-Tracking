# Top AI News (2026-10-23)

## [Anthropic Launches "Duet" — A Proactive Assistant That Acts Without Being Asked](https://aisdg.substack.com/p/ai-news-for-october-23-2026)
**Source:** AI SDG / Multiple outlets
**Summary:** Anthropic launched Duet, an AI assistant that proactively works across connected apps without requiring explicit prompts, but requires user approval before taking high-stakes actions like deleting files, making payments, or sending communications. It is currently in waitlist preview for US Pro and Max plan subscribers. This represents Anthropic's first major foray into fully autonomous agentic AI for consumers.

## [OpenAI Nears $30B Bridge Round at $1.25T Valuation Led by UAE Investors](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)
**Source:** AI Weekly / The Neuron
**Summary:** OpenAI is reportedly close to closing a bridge funding round of up to $30 billion co-led by UAE sovereign investors and Coatue Management, at a pre-money valuation of approximately $1.25 trillion. The deal is framed as bridge financing ahead of a potential IPO, though terms could still change before signing. The round would be one of the largest private capital raises in tech history.

## [Microsoft Reports China-Linked Group Used AI Agents in Cyberattack Campaign](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-friday-october-23-2026/)
**Source:** The Neuron
**Summary:** Microsoft disclosed that a China-linked threat actor used the ARTEX agentic penetration-testing system to automate attack activity during a ConnectWise compromise, abusing application credentials across Microsoft 365 environments. This is one of the first publicly confirmed cases of nation-state actors deploying AI agents for offensive cyber operations. The disclosure raises urgent questions about AI agent security and offensive AI capabilities.

## [Trump Signs Voluntary AI Safety Accord with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Nvidia, xAI](https://www.foxnews.com/live-news/ai-super-intelligence-safety-10-06)
**Source:** Fox News / CNBC
**Summary:** President Trump signed a voluntary AI safety accord at the White House with CEOs from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, xAI, and Nvidia. The accord is non-binding and comes weeks after executives from those same companies declined to guarantee their AI agents would always follow safety guardrails at a New York City Council hearing. Critics note the voluntary nature of the agreement limits its enforcement power.
