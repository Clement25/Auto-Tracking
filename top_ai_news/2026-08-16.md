## [Google Unveils HEIR: Open-Source Homomorphic Encryption Compiler for AI](https://imfounder.com/science-tech/ai/ai-updates-august-2026-openai-astra-deepmind/)
**Source:** AI Updates August 2026 / VentureBeat
**Summary:** Google released HEIR (Homomorphic Encryption Intermediate Representation), an open-source compiler toolchain that converts pretrained AI models to run inference on fully encrypted inputs — meaning servers process data without ever seeing it in plaintext. ## [White House Plans AI Safety Framework Meeting with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-03/openai-anthropic-google-to-join-white-house-ai-safety-meeting)
**Source:** Bloomberg
**Summary:** The Trump administration is convening a meeting with major AI labs — including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google — to finalize a voluntary AI safety testing framework stemming from a June executive order on AI cybersecurity.
