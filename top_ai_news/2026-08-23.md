## [Cloudflare Launches Kitesurf, a Browser Built for AI Agents](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/07/cloudflare-launches-kitesurf-a-browser-built-for-ai-agents/)
**Source:** TechCrunch
**Summary:** Cloudflare released Kitesurf, a purpose-built browser for AI agents that runs entirely in V8 isolates on Cloudflare Workers, using 3–7× less CPU and memory than Chromium and just 1/7th the memory during HTML extraction. It supports the Chrome DevTools Protocol so existing Puppeteer/Playwright agent code can switch with a single parameter change. This marks a major infrastructure shift for agentic web automation, bringing browser-use capabilities to serverless-scale deployments at a fraction of the cost.

## [EU AI Act Article 50 Transparency Obligations Now Enforceable](https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2026/2026-08-03-eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-take-effect-2-august-2026)
**Source:** Cooley / European Commission
**Summary:** As of August 2, 2026, the EU AI Act's Article 50 transparency obligations are fully enforceable, requiring providers and deployers to disclose AI involvement in user interactions, AI-generated content, emotion recognition, biometrics, and deepfakes. Non-compliance can trigger fines of up to €15 million or 3% of global annual turnover. This is the most sweeping AI transparency regulation yet to take effect, affecting virtually every consumer-facing AI product operating in the EU.
