## [Mistral Releases "le Chonk" — 1 Trillion Parameter Open-Weight Model](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news)
**Source:** VentureBeat / LLM Stats
**Summary:** Mistral released Mistral Large 4, dubbed "le Chonk," a 1-trillion-parameter open-weight model trained on 4,000 Grace Blackwell GPUs and optimized for general agentic capabilities.
