# Top AI News (2026-09-20)

## [Plugin4Shell: Zero-Click RCE Hits Claude Code, Codex, Copilot and Gemini CLI](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/plugin4shell-lets-repository-owners.html)
**Source:** The Hacker News / Help Net Security
**Summary:** AIR researchers disclosed Plugin4Shell on September 18, a zero-click remote code execution vulnerability that lets attackers bypass SHA-pinning checks in Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, GitHub Copilot, and Gemini CLI, silently swapping in malicious plugin code. Anthropic patched Claude Code in v2.1.179 and OpenAI patched Codex in v0.146.0, but Microsoft has not yet shipped a fix for Copilot. The flaw is especially severe because affected plugins inherit full developer permissions — source code, credentials, SSH keys, and production secrets.

## [StepFun Ships Step 5 Preview: 600B MoE with 1M-Token Context](https://pandaily.com/stepfun-step-5-preview-600b-moe-1m-context)
**Source:** Pandaily / AI Weekly
**Summary:** Chinese AI lab StepFun officially announced Step 5 Preview on September 20 and opened API access the same day. The model is a 600B-parameter sparse MoE with 27B active parameters per token and a one-million-token context window, targeting long-horizon agent workloads such as AI coding, financial analysis, and professional knowledge work. Priced at $1/M input and $2.70/M output tokens — roughly one-seventh the cost of GPT-5.6 Sol — with open weights scheduled for October 15.

## [Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei Calls for Slower AI Development](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/09/14/anthropics-dario-amodei-calls-for-slower-pace-of-ai-development.html)
**Source:** CNBC / Axios
**Summary:** Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published an essay calling on AI companies and governments to deliberately slow frontier-model capability advances, citing recursive self-improvement risks and a recent AI agent incident as tipping points. His "pacing the frontier" plan calls for third-party embedded evaluators, shared safety benchmarks among democratic-nation AI labs, and broader international coordination. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman and xAI's Elon Musk both publicly backed the call, marking an unusual moment of cross-industry safety consensus.

## [Anthropic Selects Accenture as First Embedded AI Safety Evaluator](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/anthropic-accenture-ai-safety.html)
**Source:** CNBC / TechCrunch
**Summary:** Anthropic announced on September 18 that it has chosen Accenture (via its Faculty AI unit) as its first embedded third-party evaluator, granting employee-level access to red-team models, test safeguards, and assess alignment with human values. Both companies will invest at least $1 billion over five years, with Anthropic funding Accenture's work in the near term; the agreement is non-exclusive and additional evaluation partners are expected. The move is the first concrete implementation of Amodei's "pacing the frontier" safety plan.

## [Clinicians Push Back on Medical AI Beyond Diagnostics](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/ft-clinicians-push-back-on-medical-ai-beyond-diagnostics)
**Source:** Financial Times (via AI Weekly)
**Summary:** A Financial Times investigation found widespread clinician skepticism about deploying AI beyond radiology and diagnostics — the one domain where peer-reviewed clinical trial evidence exists. A Wolters Kluwer 2026 survey of 355 US doctors and nurses flagged deskilling (74%), hallucinations (74%), and advertiser-driven bias (72%) as top concerns, while formal AI governance awareness rose only from 21% to 27% year-over-year despite AI use tripling. The reporting highlights a growing evidentiary gap between diagnostic AI (well-validated) and newer triage, documentation, and patient-interaction tools (minimally validated).

## [China's CXMT Starts Mass Production of G5 DRAM at 11.95nm](https://easternherald.com/2026/09/20/china-cxmt-g5-dram-mass-production-samsung-micron/)
**Source:** Eastern Herald / Seoul Economic Daily
**Summary:** Chinese memory chipmaker CXMT announced at the 2026 World Manufacturing Convention in Hefei that its fifth-generation G5 DRAM platform — built without ASML's banned EUV machines via quadruple patterning — is in mass production at an 11.95nm active-area half-pitch. Two 24Gb LPDDR5X products are already integrated into mainstream Chinese flagship smartphones, and die yield per wafer is up more than 50% versus gen-4. CXMT's global DRAM market share has reached 10%, intensifying competition with Samsung and SK Hynix at a time when AI hardware demand is surging.
