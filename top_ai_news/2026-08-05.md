## [rust-lang/rust Publishes Official LLM Usage Policy](https://www.cryptointegrat.com/p/ai-news-august-5-2026)
**Source:** AI Weekly
**Summary:** The rust-lang/rust maintainers published a formal LLM policy on August 5, 2026, summarized as: LLMs may "answer questions, analyze, distill, refine, check, suggest, review" but not "create." The policy is among the first by a major open-source project to explicitly define the acceptable scope of AI assistance in contributions and code review. It is expected to influence other large open-source communities grappling with AI-generated code quality and attribution.

## [SK Hynix and SanDisk Publish First OCP Spec for High Bandwidth Flash (HBF)](https://www.cryptointegrat.com/p/ai-news-august-5-2026)
**Source:** AI Weekly / Flash Memory Summit 2026
**Summary:** SK Hynix and SanDisk jointly published the first Open Compute Project technical specification for High Bandwidth Flash (HBF) at Flash Memory Summit 2026, defining a new NAND-based memory tier that sits between HBM and SSDs in the memory hierarchy. HBF targets AI inference workloads that need large working sets at lower cost than HBM while maintaining higher bandwidth than traditional SSDs. The spec is expected to catalyze a new class of AI accelerators and memory-centric computing architectures.
