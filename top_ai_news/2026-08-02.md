# Top AI News (2026-08-02)

## [California SB 942 AI Transparency Act Takes Effect](https://www.ailawsbystate.com/blog/california-ai-transparency-act-sb-942)
**Source:** TechTimes / National Law Review
**Summary:** California SB 942 became operative on August 2, 2026, requiring generative-AI providers with 1 million or more California monthly users to embed C2PA-compatible provenance metadata in AI-generated images, video, and audio, offer a free public AI-detection tool, and allow users to add visible AI labels to content. Violations carry fines of $5,000 per day per instance enforced by the state Attorney General. This is the first major U.S. state-level AI content provenance mandate and sets a compliance precedent likely to ripple across the industry.

**Source:** AI Weekly / Palo Alto Networks Unit 42

## [Munich Court Rules AI Music Generator Suno Violated Copyrights](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)
**Source:** AI Weekly
**Summary:** A Munich court ruled that Suno, the AI music generation startup, infringed copyrights through both its training data practices and the outputs it generated, marking one of the first European court rulings to hold an AI company liable at both stages of the AI pipeline.
