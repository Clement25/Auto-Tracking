# Top AI News (2026-08-04)

## [OpenAI's Astra Solves 10 Decades-Old Math Problems](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/openai-teases-astra-its-next-major-ai-model-after-it-solves-10-long-standing-math-problems/)
**Source:** BleepingComputer / The Next Web / Forbes
**Summary:** OpenAI unveiled its next major model, "Astra," by announcing it solved ten long-standing open problems across eight fields including group theory, high-dimensional geometry, lattice cryptography, and quantum complexity — each unsolved for at least a decade. The headline result is the first-ever explicit construction of a non-sofic group, resolving a central question since Mikhail Gromov introduced the concept of soficity in 1999. OpenAI published a 249-page manuscript with machine-checkable Lean 4 certificates, achieved at a cost of roughly $2,000 in compute, signaling AI is becoming genuine scientific research infrastructure.

## [Alibaba Launches Qwen3.8-Max: A 2.4 Trillion Parameter Open-Weight Model](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/03/alibaba-debuts-qwen3-8-max-model-2-4t-parameters/)
**Source:** SiliconANGLE / TNGlobal / MLQ.ai
**Summary:** Alibaba officially launched Qwen3.8-Max on August 3, a 2.4-trillion-parameter Sparse Mixture-of-Experts model with a 1M token context window that activates only 95B parameters at inference time. The model ranks second in Vision Arena and supports multimodal inputs (text, images, video, documents) — the first Qwen model over 1T parameters to do so. Open weights are planned for release next week, making it the largest open-weight model Alibaba has ever released; it scores just 37 points behind Claude Opus 5 on Frontend Code Arena.

**Summary:** The embodied intelligence market hit $4.44 billion in 2025 and is growing at 39% annually toward $23 billion by 2030, driven by vision-language-action (VLA) models that let robots learn by interacting with the real world rather than from scripted programs.
