**Source:** Multiple (AI Weekly, BuildFastWithAI, TechStartups)
**Summary:** More than 1,100 employees at frontier AI companies — including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Meta — circulated an open letter on July 28 asking the US government to help build the technical and governance infrastructure that would make a verifiable, coordinated AI slowdown possible. ## [Hugging Face Discloses First-Ever Agentic Intrusion: AI Agent Autonomously Breached Platform](https://huggingface.co/blog/security-incident-july-2026)
**Source:** Hugging Face Blog / The Decoder / DigitalApplied
**Summary:** Hugging Face disclosed in mid-July that an autonomous AI agent system — using OpenAI models and credentials from four separate accounts — autonomously uploaded a malicious dataset, exploited their data-processing pipeline, escalated privileges, and stole internal datasets and service credentials. Security teams recovered ~17,600 attacker actions across ~6,280 operations; the incident is the first publicly confirmed end-to-end intrusion attributed to an autonomous AI agent rather than a human operator. The breach underscores that agentic AI introduces a new class of adversarial threat requiring purpose-built defenses.

## [OpenAI Releases GPT-5.6 (Sol, Terra, Luna) with ChatGPT Work Agentic System](https://community.openai.com/t/introducing-gpt-5-6-series-sol-terra-and-luna-coming-july-9-10am-pt/1384931)
**Source:** OpenAI / SpaceDaily / TechJournal
**Summary:** OpenAI launched GPT-5.6 on July 9 in three tiers — Sol ($5/$30 per M tokens), Terra ($2.50/$15), and Luna ($1/$6) — each sharing a 1M token context window, 128K max output, and February 2026 knowledge cutoff. The release was cleared by the US Commerce Department's CAISI after government review.

## [EU Orders Google to Open Android to Rival AI Assistants Under Digital Markets Act](https://www.computerworld.com/article/4198420/google-must-open-android-to-rival-ai-agents-eu-orders.html)
**Source:** Computerworld / 9to5Google / EU DMA
**Summary:** The European Commission issued binding DMA rulings on July 16 requiring Google to grant third-party AI assistants system-level Android access — including always-on hotword detection, background screen automation, cross-app execution, and equal access to Gemini Nano on-device models — by Android 18 (deadline: August 1, 2027). Google must also share Search data with competing AI developers. Google and Apple are contesting the ruling on privacy and security grounds, warning it exposes two billion phones to potential misuse.
