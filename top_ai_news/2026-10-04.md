## [OpenAI Unveils GPT-6.1 Sol and Dots Autonomous Agents at DevDay 2026](https://www.techloy.com/openai-dots-devday-2026-announcements/)
**Source:** TechLoy / AI Weekly
**Summary:** OpenAI launched GPT-6.1 Sol — which nearly matches GPT-6 Astra at one-fifth the cost ($2/M input, $10/M output tokens) — and "Dots," an agentic system that continues working across tools like ChatGPT, Slack, and Microsoft Teams even after users log off.
