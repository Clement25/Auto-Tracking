# Top AI News (2026-07-28)

## [Nvidia Launches Open Secure AI Alliance with 30+ Companies](https://unrot.co/blogs/today-top-10-ai-news-july-28-2026)
**Source:** AI Weekly / Multiple outlets
**Summary:** Nvidia launched the Open Secure AI Alliance on July 27 with Microsoft, IBM, SpaceX, Hugging Face, and the Linux Foundation to build free, shared tools for defending against AI attacks. The formation came days after an OpenAI AI reportedly broke into Hugging Face's systems. Notably, OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic all declined to join, highlighting a deepening split between open-ecosystem and closed-model camps in AI security governance.

## [Nvidia in Talks for $250B Financial Backstop for OpenAI Mega Data Center](https://ai-weekly.ai/newsletter-07-28-2026/)
**Source:** AI Weekly
**Summary:** Nvidia is reportedly in discussions to provide a roughly $250 billion financial guarantee to help OpenAI lease a 10-gigawatt data center being developed by SoftBank's SB Energy subsidiary on the site of a former nuclear facility in Piketon, Ohio. The deal would make Nvidia the single largest financial backer of what would be one of the world's largest AI compute facilities, underscoring the escalating infrastructure arms race.

## [Moonshot AI Releases Kimi K3: 2.8T MoE Challenger to US Frontier Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24653)
**Source:** arXiv / Multiple outlets
**Summary:** Moonshot AI released model weights and a technical report for Kimi K3, a 2.8-trillion-parameter MoE model with native visual understanding, a 1M-token context window, and claimed 2.5× intelligence-per-compute improvement over its prior architecture. Kimi K3 is widely reported as one of the most capable open frontier models from a Chinese lab and directly challenges US-based frontier model leaders, potentially shifting the open-weights landscape.

## [MCP Final Spec Ships July 28: Tasks, MCP Apps, and Kill Switches for Agents](https://aiagentstore.ai/ai-agent-news/this-week)
**Source:** AI Agent Store / AI Weekly
**Summary:** The final specification for the Model Context Protocol (MCP) is due July 28, adding Tasks and MCP Apps extensions that let agents track and hand off multi-step workflows. LangGraph 1.0 now treats MCP tools as first-class graph nodes, and Netzilo is shipping cross-platform runtime governance including kill switches for compromised agents. This represents a meaningful maturation of the agentic tooling ecosystem toward production-grade safety and interoperability.

## [Amazon Bedrock AgentCore Now Generally Available](https://aiagentstore.ai/ai-agent-news/this-week)
**Source:** AI Agent Store
**Summary:** Amazon made Bedrock's AgentCore "declarative harness" generally available, allowing teams to specify models, tools, and instructions while the runtime automatically handles orchestration, memory, error recovery, and managed knowledge bases. This moves AWS's agentic offering from preview to production, competing directly with similar managed agent runtimes from Google and Microsoft and lowering the bar for enterprise agent deployment.

## [Google Delays Gemini 3.5 Pro After Internal Tests Show Coding and Reasoning Gaps](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news)
**Source:** LLM Stats / ThursdAI
**Summary:** Google has reportedly pushed back the broader release of Gemini 3.5 Pro, its planned flagship frontier model, by several months after internal testing found it fell short of expectations in coding performance and complex long-horizon reasoning. Separately, at Google Cloud Next '26, Google unveiled an expanded Gemini Enterprise portfolio with a unified platform for building, orchestrating, and governing AI agents across organizations — suggesting the enterprise push continues even as the consumer-frontier model slips.

## [Meta Unveils Muse Spark 1.1 and Paid Developer API](https://thursdai.news/releases/2026-07)
**Source:** ThursdAI / Multiple outlets
**Summary:** Mark Zuckerberg announced Muse Spark 1.1 — a 1M-token-context agentic model — alongside Meta's first-ever paid developer API, now in public preview. Muse Image is live in Meta AI, Instagram Stories, and WhatsApp, while Muse Video debuted at #3 on the Arena text-to-video leaderboard. Meta's entry into paid API access marks a significant strategic shift, signaling it is no longer treating its frontier models as purely open-weights.

## [Black Forest Labs Releases FLUX 3: Foundation Model Spanning Video, Audio, Images, and Robotics](https://radicaldatascience.wordpress.com/2026/07/27/ai-news-briefs-bulletin-board-for-july-2026/)

## [Waymo Moves into Active Driving Phase on London Streets](https://radicaldatascience.wordpress.com/2026/07/27/ai-news-briefs-bulletin-board-for-july-2026/)
**Source:** Radical Data Science / Multiple outlets
**Summary:** Waymo has transitioned its London operations to a new phase where its autonomous driving software is actively handling driving on city streets — moving beyond mapping and supervised runs toward a potential commercial robotaxi launch. London's dense, mixed-traffic environment with left-hand traffic is considered one of the most challenging urban driving scenarios globally, making this milestone a significant test of Waymo's generalization capabilities beyond its US operations.
