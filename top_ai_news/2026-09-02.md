## [OpenAI's Astra Becomes First LLM to Breach 'Critical' Cybersecurity Threshold](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)
**Source:** AI Weekly / Anthropic Newsroom
**Summary:** OpenAI confirmed that its upcoming Astra model is the first LLM to exceed its Preparedness Framework's 'Critical' cybersecurity capability threshold, scoring a perfect ExploitBench and autonomously finding and exploiting two zero-days in controlled tests. ## [EU Designates ChatGPT a Very Large Online Search Engine Under Digital Services Act](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)
**Source:** AI Weekly / European Commission
**Summary:** The European Commission formally designated ChatGPT a Very Large Online Search Engine (VLOSE) under the Digital Services Act on September 1, 2026 — the first time a generative AI chatbot has been placed in that regulatory category — triggered by roughly 159 million average monthly EU users exceeding the 45-million-user threshold. The designation imposes algorithmic transparency, audit, and risk-mitigation obligations on OpenAI, and sets a precedent for how Europe may regulate other frontier AI products with large-scale public reach.
