## [China's MIIT Targets 9,800 Eflops of AI Compute by 2030, Plans $532B Infrastructure Investment](https://www.scmp.com/tech/policy/article/3366733/china-targets-fourfold-boost-in-ai-computing-capacity-2030-major-tech-push)
**Source:** South China Morning Post / Unite.AI
**Summary:** China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology published its 15th Five-Year Plan for information and communications on September 7, targeting 9,800 eflops of intelligent computing capacity by 2030—more than four times the 2025 baseline of 1,590 eflops—alongside 1,700 exabytes of advanced storage. The plan calls for 3.8 trillion yuan (~$532 billion) in cumulative information infrastructure investment from 2026–2030, with orderly deployment of 10,000- and 100,000-GPU clusters and a nationwide integrated computing-power network. This is one of the most ambitious state-level AI infrastructure commitments ever announced.

## [Repo-To-Skill: 5,000 GitHub-Distilled Skills Push Research Agent +134% on MLE-Bench](https://aiweekly.co/editors-blog/found-first-repo-to-skill-5-000-github-distilled-skills-push-a-gpt-5-5-agent-134)
