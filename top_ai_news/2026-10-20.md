# Top AI News (2026-10-20)

## [AI Agents Used in Large-Scale Cyberattack on 100+ Companies](https://www.reuters.com/technology/)
**Source:** Reuters
**Summary:** A Chinese-speaking hacker reportedly breached more than 100 company websites by delegating much of the work to AI agents, marking one of the first confirmed large-scale uses of agentic AI in offensive cyber operations. The incident is prompting urgent calls for new security frameworks that account for autonomous agent-driven attacks, which can operate far faster and at greater scale than human-led intrusions.

## [Microsoft Introduces Agent Risk Controls in Microsoft 365 E7 and Project Perception](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/)
**Source:** VentureBeat
**Summary:** Microsoft rolled out new security controls that allow administrators to assess individual AI agents for risk level—restricting them from making changes, requiring human approval for actions, or shutting them down entirely. The controls are part of Microsoft 365 E7 and Project Perception, signaling that enterprise AI governance is becoming a product feature rather than a policy afterthought.

## [GPT-6.1 Sol Achieves 97.9% on OpenAI's COBOL Migration Benchmark](https://aiweekly.co/)
**Source:** AI Weekly
**Summary:** OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Sol model reportedly hit 97.9% accuracy on OpenAI's COBOL migration evaluation at maximum reasoning effort, compared to just 5.4% for the prior GPT-5.6 Sol. The leap highlights the rapid pace of progress in applying large language models to legacy code modernization, a multi-billion dollar enterprise IT challenge.

## [Google Releases TorchTPU: Open-Source PyTorch for Tensor Processing Units](https://blog.google/)
**Source:** Google / Tech News
**Summary:** Google released TorchTPU, an open-source PyTorch variant that enables AI applications to run on its Tensor Processing Units with minimal code changes. The release lowers the barrier for researchers and developers to leverage Google's custom AI hardware, potentially shifting more AI workloads toward TPU-based infrastructure.

## [MIT CSAIL Study: Isolated Claude Opus 4.7 Agents Invented Their Own Games](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-tuesday-october-20-2026/)
**Source:** The Neuron / MIT CSAIL
**Summary:** MIT CSAIL researcher Antonio Norelli and collaborators placed isolated Claude Opus 4.7 agent instances on a virtual island for 30 hours with no external instructions, and the agents reportedly invented their own cooperative and competitive games. The study raises intriguing questions about emergent social behavior in large language models and the conditions under which AI systems develop goal-like structures spontaneously.

## [AI Outpaces Security Teams in Finding Software Vulnerabilities](https://aiweekly.co/)
**Source:** AI Weekly
**Summary:** A new report highlights that AI systems are now discovering software vulnerabilities faster than security teams can triage and patch them, creating a widening remediation gap. Security researchers warn that this asymmetry could give attackers—especially those pairing AI tools with automated exploit generation—a structural advantage over defenders.
