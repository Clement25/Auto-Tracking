## [UK Court of Appeal Hears Getty vs. Stability AI Test Case on AI Training and Copyright](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)
**Source:** AI Weekly
**Summary:** The UK Court of Appeal heard arguments in the landmark Getty Images v. Stability AI case, which asks whether a trained generative model constitutes an infringing copy of copyrighted training data. The ruling will set a significant precedent for the entire generative AI industry's legal exposure in the UK and potentially influence EU and US interpretations. Both sides presented technical arguments about how diffusion models store and reproduce training data.
