## [EU AI Act Transparency Rules Take Effect, Requiring AI Disclosure](https://www.euronews.com/tag/google?p=8)
**Source:** European Commission / AI Herald
**Summary:** As of August 2, 2026, the EU AI Act's transparency obligations came into force, requiring providers of AI systems such as chatbots and virtual assistants to clearly disclose when users are interacting with AI. EU member states were also required to establish at least one national AI regulatory sandbox by this date, marking a major milestone in the world's most comprehensive AI governance framework.
