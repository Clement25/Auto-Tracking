## [Nvidia to Acquire Hugging Face for $12.9 Billion in Major Open AI Push](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/nvidia-buy-hugging-face-nearly-13-billion-big-bet-open-ai-models-rcna595868)
**Source:** NBC News / CNN / Fortune / The Verge
**Summary:** Nvidia is acquiring open-model hub Hugging Face for $12.93 billion — $11.9B to shareholders plus $1B in equity to retain employees — in a major bet that open AI models will drive future GPU demand. The platform hosts 3 million open models and is relied on by 200,000+ companies; Nvidia has committed to keeping it an open platform. This matters because as Big Tech customers (OpenAI, Microsoft, Amazon) develop their own chips to reduce Nvidia dependence, this acquisition lets Nvidia deepen its stake in the open-model ecosystem that still needs its hardware.

## [OpenAI Launches GPT-6 Astra — First Model to Cross Critical Cybersecurity Threshold](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/openai-launches-astra-its-powerful-and-controversial-new-model/)
**Source:** TechCrunch / CNBC / Bloomberg
**Summary:** OpenAI began rolling out GPT-6 Astra to a limited set of partners, marking the first model to trigger the company's Preparedness Framework's "Critical" cybersecurity tier after scoring a perfect result on ExploitBench and autonomously finding and exploiting two zero-days in modified tests. Access to the strongest cyber capabilities is restricted to selected application-based cybersecurity program (Daybreak) partners, with chain-of-thought monitoring and jailbreak detection in place. This is a landmark moment in AI safety governance — the first real-world trigger of a "Critical" capability threshold — and sets a precedent for how advanced AI models will be deployed going forward.

## [ChatGPT Health Integrates with Epic's 325 Million-Patient Record System](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-09-03)
**Source:** AI Weekly / OpenAI
**Summary:** OpenAI has connected ChatGPT for Healthcare to Epic EHRs, enabling the system to pull authorized patient context directly into the ChatGPT interface or embed it inside the clinical chart. ## [DOJ Files Statement of Interest Backing OpenAI in NYT Copyright Case](https://thenextweb.com/news/doj-statement-of-interest-openai-copyright-fair-use-eu-ai-act-recital-106-tdm-opt-out)
**Source:** The Next Web / US News / CelebrityAccess
**Summary:** The U.S. Justice Department filed a statement of interest supporting OpenAI and Microsoft's argument that training AI models on copyrighted text qualifies as fair use, calling it "extraordinarily transformative." The brief arrives days before summary-judgment motions in The New York Times lawsuit and marks the first time the federal government has directly taken a position in the wave of AI copyright litigation from publishers, authors, and musicians. This could have a decisive effect on the legal landscape for how AI companies are allowed to train their models.
