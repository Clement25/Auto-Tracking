# Top AI News (2026-10-11)

## [Satya Nadella Wants an Emergency Brake on AI](https://artificiallyintimidating.com/p/ai-brief-october-11-2026)
**Source:** AI Brief (Artificially Intimidating)
**Summary:** Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella published an essay arguing that frontier AI models should be treated as insider risks, with control, auditing, and override mechanisms kept outside the model's own decision-making loop. He calls for independent "emergency brake" infrastructure at the national and industry level, framing unmonitored frontier deployments as a systemic risk comparable to financial systemic risk.

## [Can AI Data Centers Win Over Opponents by Dropping NDAs?](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/11/can-the-ai-industry-persuade-data-center-opponents-by-getting-rid-of-ndas)
**Source:** TechCrunch
**Summary:** Amazon announced it will follow Microsoft in eliminating nondisclosure agreements (NDAs) when negotiating with local governments over new data center sites. The move is a bid to reduce community opposition by increasing transparency around power use, water consumption, and land impact — a growing point of friction as AI infrastructure buildout accelerates worldwide.

## [Nvidia in Talks to Acquire or Invest in Reflection AI](https://riorundown.substack.com/p/trending-ai-news-oct-11-2026)
**Source:** Rio Rundown (citing Financial Times)
