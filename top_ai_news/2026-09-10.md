## [California Becomes First U.S. State to Enact Independent AI Audit Laws](https://qz.com/california-ai-independent-audit-laws-newsom-091026)
**Source:** Quartz / Gizmodo / KION
**Summary:** Governor Newsom signed Assembly Bill 1405 and Senate Bill 813 into law, establishing the first U.S. framework requiring independent third-party audits and assessments of AI systems. AB 1405 creates a state AI Auditor Registry — with mandatory registration required from January 1, 2029 — and sets independence and transparency standards for auditors, while SB 813 creates a framework for independent verification organizations to assess AI compliance. Both Anthropic and OpenAI backed the legislation, marking an unusual alignment between the AI industry and regulatory oversight.
