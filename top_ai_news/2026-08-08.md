# Top AI News (2026-08-08)

## [EU AI Act Enforcement Begins: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Under Scrutiny](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/eu-ai-act-enforcement-powers.html)
**Source:** CNBC / Euronews / QZ
**Summary:** The EU Artificial Intelligence Act entered full enforcement on August 2, 2026, with the European Commission's AI Office gaining direct powers to demand pre-release model evaluations, restrict EU market access, and fine providers up to €15M or 3% of global turnover. Anthropic agreed to give EU cybersecurity agency ENISA access to its Mythos model, while OpenAI offered access to GPT-5.5-Cyber. The enforcement phase also activates transparency rules requiring chatbots, AI agents, and digital human systems to clearly identify themselves to users.

## [Google Shifts AI Leadership to California, Restructures DeepMind](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-06/google-shifts-ai-power-to-california-in-race-against-anthropic-openai)
**Source:** Bloomberg / Yahoo Finance
**Summary:** Google is consolidating its AI leadership at Mountain View headquarters, appointing Koray Kavukcuoglu to run AI research and operations as Demis Hassabis steps back from day-to-day operations to become Chairman of Google DeepMind and Alphabet's Chief Scientist. The reorganization comes amid an accelerating race against Anthropic and OpenAI and follows the high-profile departures of Jeff Dean, Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le, and Oriol Vinyals, who are launching a new AI company called Discovery Loop. The move signals a significant strategic bet on centralized model development over distributed research.

## [White House Finalizes Voluntary Cyber Risk Framework for Frontier AI Models](https://spectrumlocalnews.com/nys/central-ny/politics/2026/08/04/source--voluntary-federal-regulatory-framework-for-advanced-ai-models-finalized)
**Source:** Spectrum News / Multiple
**Summary:** The White House finalized a voluntary framework for cyber risk assessment of frontier AI models on August 1, briefing Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Meta, and NVIDIA on its contents in closed-door sessions. The framework encourages but does not mandate pre-deployment red-teaming, threat modeling, and incident reporting, positioning the US approach in deliberate contrast to the EU's binding enforcement regime. The announcement came just one day before the EU AI Act enforcement provisions kicked in, highlighting the diverging transatlantic regulatory philosophies.

## [OpenAI Makes Text Chat Unlimited for Free Users](https://aitoolsrecap.com/daily-ai-news.aspx)
**Source:** AIToolsRecap / Multiple
**Summary:** OpenAI announced it is removing usage caps on text-based chat for all free-tier users, making unlimited access to its latest models available without a subscription for the first time. The move is widely seen as a strategic response to growing competition from open-source and lower-cost alternatives that have eroded OpenAI's market share among casual users. Paid tiers now differentiate primarily through advanced features such as image generation, voice mode, deep research, and higher API rate limits.

## [China Fines 12 AI Companies Under New Companion AI Rules](https://cubbbix.com/blog/ai-regulation-august-2026-global-update/)
**Source:** Cubbbix / Multiple
**Summary:** Chinese regulators issued their first enforcement actions under new companion AI regulations, fining 12 companies a combined 4.2M RMB in the first week of August for violations including failing to disclose AI identity to users, generating inappropriate emotional dependency content, and inadequate age-gating. The fines cover products ranging from AI girlfriend and boyfriend apps to AI tutoring and mental wellness companions. The enforcement action is being closely watched globally as a template for how governments may regulate the rapidly growing sector of emotionally intelligent AI products.

**Source:** ETC Journal / Multiple
**Summary:** The tech industry has recorded over 100,000 job cuts in 2026, with companies citing AI-driven productivity improvements as a primary factor — one major firm reassigned 7,000 employees to AI-focused teams and cancelled 6,000 open roles. ## [EU Brussels Opens Direct Talks with OpenAI and Anthropic Over AI Cybersecurity Incidents](https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-08-03-eu-talks-openai-anthropic-breaches/)
**Source:** ResultSense / CNBC
**Summary:** The European Commission opened formal discussions with OpenAI and Anthropic following cyberattacks reportedly linked to misuse of their models, with senior Commission officials traveling to San Francisco to press company leadership directly. Both companies agreed to give EU cybersecurity agency ENISA access to specific model variants for auditing — Anthropic's Mythos and OpenAI's GPT-5.5-Cyber. The episode marks the first significant test of the EU AI Act's enforcement mechanism and suggests Brussels is prepared to use its new powers aggressively.
