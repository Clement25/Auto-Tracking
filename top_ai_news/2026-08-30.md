## [Google's AI Mode Becomes a Full-Fledged Travel Agent](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/27/googles-ai-mode-can-now-track-flight-prices-help-book-hotels-and-more/)
**Source:** TechCrunch
**Summary:** Google announced new agentic travel capabilities in AI Mode on August 27, 2026, letting users describe a trip and have the AI track flight prices across 300+ airlines, receive price-drop notifications, and book hotels entirely within the conversational interface. This marks a major shift from search-as-information to search-as-action, with Google now executing booking workflows—a capability nine months in the making since it was first teased. The move signals Google's push to make AI Mode the default interface for high-intent consumer tasks, directly threatening the traditional OTA (online travel agency) market.

## [EU AI Act Article 50 Transparency Obligations Now in Effect](https://local-ai-zone.github.io/blog/ai-updates-august-2026.html)
**Source:** Local AI Zone / EU AI Act Updates
**Summary:** Article 50 of the EU AI Act took effect on August 2, 2026, requiring companies to disclose when users are interacting with AI systems—including chatbots, synthetic media, and AI-generated content. All major AI providers serving EU users have updated their interfaces with mandatory disclosure banners and interaction labels, while compliance teams scramble to audit downstream deployments. This is the first enforceable consumer-facing obligation under the Act, and regulators have indicated enforcement actions could begin as early as Q4 2026 for non-compliant deployments.
