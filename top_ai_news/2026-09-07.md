## [Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell Sue Anthropic in Multi-Billion Dollar Copyright Case](https://variety.com/2026/music/news/sony-music-publishing-warner-chappell-anthropic-lawsuit-1236847442/)
**Source:** Variety / TechCrunch / Music Business Worldwide
**Summary:** Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell filed a 48-page federal complaint alleging Anthropic conducted a "brazen campaign" of IP theft, training Claude on tens of thousands of copyrighted songs — including The Beatles, Taylor Swift, and Michael Jackson — via pirate repositories and scraped lyric services. Damages of up to $150,000 per song make this a potential multi-billion dollar case; Anthropic says it will defend on fair use grounds. This is the largest copyright action yet targeting AI training data, escalating an industry-wide legal battle.
