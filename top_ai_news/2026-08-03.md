## [Five Eyes Agencies Publish Joint Agentic AI Security Guidance](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/05/04/five-eyes-warn-agentic-ai-is-too-dangerous-for-rapid-rollout/5229103)
**Source:** The Register / Let's Data Science
**Summary:** The cybersecurity agencies of the US (CISA, NSA), UK (NCSC), Australia (ASD/ACSC), Canada (CCCS), and New Zealand (NCSC-NZ) jointly released "Careful Adoption of Agentic AI Services"—the first joint Five Eyes guidance on agentic AI security. The document identifies five risk categories (privilege escalation, design/configuration, behavioral, structural, and supply-chain) and recommends strict least-privilege access, per-request authentication, human approval for high-impact actions, and treating AI security within existing cybersecurity frameworks rather than as a separate discipline.
