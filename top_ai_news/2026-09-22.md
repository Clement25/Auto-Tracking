# Top AI News (2026-09-22)

## [OpenAI Launches GPT-6 Sol and Luna, Slashing API Costs 50%+](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/openai-launches-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/)
**Source:** TechCrunch / VentureBeat
**Summary:** OpenAI expanded its GPT-6 family with two new models on September 22: GPT-6 Sol ($2/$10 per million tokens) and GPT-6 Luna ($0.10/$0.50 per million tokens) — each priced at roughly half or less than their predecessors. Both models feature a 1.05M-token context window, multimodal input, 128K max output, and the full GPT-6 agent tool suite including computer use and web search. This positions OpenAI aggressively against Anthropic's same-day Claude Opus 5.5 launch, and signals a wider industry trend of dramatic cost compression at the frontier.

## [Anthropic Releases Claude Opus 5.5 with Fable-Level Performance at Lower Cost](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/anthropic-releases-opus-5-5-with-lower-prices-and-fable-level-performance/)
**Source:** TechCrunch / 9to5Mac
**Summary:** Anthropic launched Claude Opus 5.5 on September 22, pricing it at $4/$20 per million tokens (20% below Opus 5) while delivering performance matching Claude Fable 5.1 on most tasks and leading it on agentic coding, computer use, and knowledge work. Anthropic also removed 5-hour usage caps for all paid tiers and added a rate-limit reset feature. External evaluators including METR and Frontier Design confirmed safety improvements over Opus 5, including better resistance to prompt injection and reduced propensity for irreversible actions.

**Source:** SiliconAngle / The Robot Report / Forbes
**Summary:** Announced at ROSCon 2026 in Toronto, Alphabet's robotics software unit Intrinsic released Intrinsic Core — a ROS-compatible open-source platform for intelligent robots covering real-time control, pose estimation, motion/grasp planning, simulation, camera calibration, and preconfigured drivers. The Apache 2.0 license and compatibility with hardware from Fanuc and Universal Robots makes it a significant bid to become an "Android of Robotics." Intrinsic was reincorporated into Google in February 2026 to accelerate physical AI development.

## [AstroForge Puts AI "Solo" in Autonomous Command of Its Asteroid-Mining Spacecraft](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/astroforge-is-putting-ai-in-command-of-its-next-spacecraft/)
**Source:** TechCrunch
**Summary:** AstroForge revealed that its in-house Transformer-based AI system "Solo" will take autonomous control of the Autonomy-1 probe, integrating with traditional control algorithms through a three-layer stack monitoring ~2,500 onboard sensors. Solo will first run in shadow mode aboard Intuitive Machines' DeepSpace-2 lunar mission (launching late 2026), with a fully autonomous deep-space mission targeted for 2027 with NASA backing. This is one of the most ambitious real-world deployments of an LLM-scale AI agent in safety-critical infrastructure.

## [France Convenes UN Security Council AI Session with OpenAI, Anthropic CEOs](https://www.securitycouncilreport.org/whatsinblue/2026/09/artificial-intelligence-high-level-briefing-2.php)
**Source:** Security Council Report / Bloomberg
**Summary:** As September's Security Council president, France convened a September 23 high-level briefing on AI and international security — the first UNSC session to directly address AI safety at the heads-of-government level. Scheduled briefers include OpenAI CEO Sam Altman (in person), Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei (remote), Hugging Face CEO Clément Delangue, and AI safety scientist Yoshua Bengio; DeepSeek and Moonshot AI were also invited. France's concept note centers on AI's potential for malicious use and the risk of humans losing control of highly capable systems.

## [OpenAI to Allow Third-Party Safety Evaluations During Model Training](https://openai.com/index/strengthening-safety-with-external-testing/)
**Source:** OpenAI / Bloomberg
**Summary:** OpenAI announced it will allow external organizations to conduct technical safety evaluations of models during training and evaluation phases — not only at the pre-deployment stage. The company published guiding principles emphasizing strong independence, scientific rigor, and security for participating evaluators, and named METR and Redwood Research as likely partners. The move mirrors Anthropic's simultaneous announcement of embedding Accenture evaluators in a multi-year, $1B-plus evaluation program, suggesting a new industry norm forming around continuous third-party safety scrutiny.

## [AI Helps Mathematicians Solve 25,000 Cases of Inverse Galois Problem](https://hipther.com/news/2026/09/22/148413/ai-dispatch-daily-trends-and-innovations-september-22-2026-ai-mathematics-economic-populism-shopping)
**Source:** HIPTHER / AI Weekly
**Summary:** Researchers announced that AI systems helped complete approximately 25,000 cases of the inverse Galois problem — a long-standing open question in abstract algebra concerning which groups arise as symmetries of field extensions. The work represents one of the largest AI-assisted breakthroughs in pure mathematics and is seen as further evidence that LLM-scale reasoning can meaningfully accelerate formal mathematical research.

## [Banking Groups Warn Regulators of Fraud and Privacy Risks from AI Shopping Bots](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)
**Source:** AI Weekly
**Summary:** A coalition of banking and financial services trade groups filed regulatory comments warning that AI-powered shopping agents acting on behalf of consumers could enable new fraud vectors, bypass security controls, and create privacy risks through excessive data sharing with merchants. The groups are pushing for clear liability frameworks governing autonomous purchasing decisions made by AI agents, particularly in cases where consumers may not fully understand the scope of agent permissions they have granted.
