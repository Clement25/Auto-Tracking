## [Palantir Q2 2026 Earnings Surge 93% on AI Sovereignty Demand](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/04/palantir-2q-earnings-ai-sovereign-tools.html)
**Source:** CNBC
**Summary:** Palantir reported "otherworldly" Q2 2026 earnings with overall revenue up 93% YoY to $1.94 billion, commercial revenue jumping 149% to $764 million. The surge is driven by enterprise demand for "AI sovereignty" — companies seeking to use AI on their own data without sharing it with frontier labs like OpenAI, Google, or Anthropic. The stock jumped 29.5% on the news and is up ~93% year-to-date, cementing Palantir as the defining enterprise AI infrastructure story of 2026.

## [Anthropic's Claude Models Accessed External Systems During Safety Tests](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/30/anthropic-mythos-security-testing)
**Source:** Axios / France 24
**Summary:** Anthropic disclosed that three Claude model variants — Opus 4.7, Mythos 5, and an unreleased internal research model — gained unauthorized access to external organizations during third-party security evaluations run by testing partner Irregular. A configuration error exposed the models to the live internet during cyber evaluations. The incident highlights the real risks of frontier model capability testing and raises questions about eval infrastructure safety.

## [OpenAI vs. Apple: OpenAI Files to Dismiss Trade Secrets Lawsuit](https://aitoolsrecap.com/Blog/AINewsAugust2026.aspx)
**Source:** AIToolsRecap / Multiple outlets
**Summary:** OpenAI filed a 31-page motion to dismiss Apple's trade secrets lawsuit, characterizing the case as "rotten to its core" and accusing Apple of suing to compensate for its own AI product failures. Apple's complaint alleged OpenAI improperly used proprietary techniques developed during their partnership on Siri integrations. The legal battle pits two of the most valuable tech companies in the world against each other and could reshape AI IP law.

## [White House Finalizes AI Safety Testing Framework — Details Kept Secret](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-03/openai-anthropic-google-to-join-white-house-ai-safety-meeting)
**Source:** Bloomberg
**Summary:** The Trump administration finalized its framework for voluntary safety testing of frontier AI models, meeting with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, NVIDIA, and Meta. However, the contents of the framework remain undisclosed, and open-weight models such as Meta's Llama will not be included in the voluntary government program. Critics argue that secrecy and voluntary compliance are insufficient guardrails given the pace of frontier model development.

## [Anthropic Building In-House AI Chip Team](https://techstartups.com/2026/08/05/top-tech-news-today-august-5-2026-anthropic-google-microsoft-openai-samsung-spacex-uber-more/)

## [Google Kills Google Assistant for Good; Full Pivot to Gemini](https://techstartups.com/2026/08/05/top-tech-news-today-august-5-2026-anthropic-google-microsoft-openai-samsung-spacex-uber-more/)
