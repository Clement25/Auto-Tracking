## [OpenAI Claims AI-Generated Proofs of Major Math Problems, Including a Millennium Problem](https://theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-tuesday-october-27-2026)
**Source:** The Neuron
**Summary:** OpenAI announced it is handing three AI-generated mathematical results to the academic community, including a Lean-formalized disproof of the Exponential Time Hypothesis for 3-SAT and a claimed proof that pure Yang-Mills theory exists — one of the seven Clay Millennium Prize Problems.
