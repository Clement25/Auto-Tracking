## [Joe Benton Quits Anthropic Safety Team, Warns AI Race Could Pose 'Extinction-Level Risks'](https://www.freepressjournal.in/world/joe-benton-quits-anthropic-safety-team-warns-ai-race-could-pose-extinction-level-risks)
**Source:** Business Standard / NBC News / Free Press Journal
**Summary:** Joe Benton, former manager of Anthropic's Scalable Oversight team, resigned on September 11, 2026 — the second Anthropic safety departure in two days after Jacob Coxon's exit. Benton warned that AI labs are building systems "much smarter than any human" without sufficient safety investment and accused Anthropic of hiding safety incidents. He will join METR (Model Evaluation and Threat Research) to run independent AI risk assessments, signaling a broader push for external oversight.

## [Cognition Launches SWE-2 Coding Agent at Quarter the Price of Rivals](https://startupfortune.com/cognitions-swe-2-coding-agent-matches-rivals-at-a-quarter-of-the-price/)
**Source:** Startup Fortune / Papers with Code
**Summary:** Cognition released SWE-2 on September 10, 2026, a coding agent post-trained from Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 (2.8 trillion parameters) using reinforcement learning scaled to the multi-trillion-parameter regime for the first time. The agent claims performance within 1 point of Anthropic's Fable 5.1 on real-world coding benchmarks at up to 70% lower cost, and uniquely supports variable reasoning effort levels (medium, high, max) trained in a single RL run. This marks a new price-performance frontier in AI coding assistants.

**Source:** AI Weekly / LLM Stats

## [Russian Threat Actor Uses Hundreds of AI Agents to Compromise 440 Organizations](https://headsupai.io/ai-news-and-updates/today)
**Source:** HeadsUp AI / Malpass
**Summary:** GreyNoise researchers reported that a Russian-speaking threat actor deployed hundreds of AI agents built on OpenAI's Codex and a DeepSeek model to systematically exploit vulnerabilities in PaperCut NG/MF print management software, compromising at least 440 instances across 395 organizations in 48 countries. Security researchers are calling it a watershed moment for AI-enabled offensive operations.
