## [UK Study: 81 of 134 AI Models Fail Safety Tests on Terrorism and Weapons](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)
**Source:** Wired / UK Nonprofit Safety Institute
**Summary:** A UK-based AI safety nonprofit tested 134 leading AI models across 627 red-team prompts on terrorism and weapons topics; 81 failed and only two passed all tests fully. The study underscores persistent safety gaps across commercial and open-source models despite recent advances in alignment techniques, and is being submitted to UK and EU regulators as evidence for stronger mandatory testing requirements.

## [KAIST & Nvidia Cut Robot Training Time 31–68% with Continual Off-Policy RL](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)
**Source:** AI Weekly / KAIST–Nvidia Research
**Summary:** Researchers from KAIST and Nvidia published a method using continual off-policy reinforcement learning to dramatically reduce the amount of real-world robot interaction needed for skill acquisition — cutting training time by 31 to 68% across dexterous tasks. The approach reuses experience from previous tasks to accelerate new skill learning, addressing one of the main barriers to deploying robots in dynamic environments.
