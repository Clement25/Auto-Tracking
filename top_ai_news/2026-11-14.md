## [Nvidia Board Launches Investigation Into CEO Jensen Huang Amid Foreign Targeting Allegations](https://scmp.com/tech/article/3373724/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-admits-affair-board-orders-investigation)
**Source:** South China Morning Post / Tom's Hardware / AP
**Summary:** U.S. intelligence sources report that Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang was targeted by Chinese intelligence in an alleged "honeypot" operation involving a Megaspeed executive. Nvidia's board has opened a formal investigation, and reports indicate the board may be moving toward removing Huang; a company spokesperson confirmed Huang remains committed to leading Nvidia. This marks one of the most significant leadership crises in the AI hardware sector and injects major uncertainty into the chip supply chain underpinning global AI development.

## [FAA's SMART AI System Under Investigation After Nationwide Airport Ground Stop](https://www.click2houston.com/news/local/2026/11/13/faa-investigating-whether-new-ai-technology-contributed-to-nationwide-ground-stop/)
**Source:** Click2Houston / AI Weekly
**Summary:** Federal officials are investigating whether the FAA's new AI-powered air traffic management system, SMART (Scalable Management of Air Resources and Traffic), contributed to a nationwide ground stop at U.S. airports on November 13. The $875M system had been deployed nationally just weeks earlier, and DHS has stated there is no public safety threat. The incident has reignited debate over the pace of AI deployment in critical government infrastructure.

## [White House Urges Easing AI Incident Liability to Encourage Disclosure](https://bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-14/white-house-urges-easing-liability-to-encourage-ai-disclosure)
**Source:** Bloomberg
**Summary:** Officials on President Trump's AI task force proposed loosening cybersecurity liability policies to incentivize AI companies to voluntarily disclose critical incidents and near-misses. The recommendation mirrors the aviation industry's safety reporting model, where voluntary disclosures earn protection from regulatory penalties. The move is seen as a pragmatic step toward building an AI incident database to inform future regulation.

## [OpenAI Disbands Robotics Team, Bets General Models Can Drive Robots](https://aiweekly.co/)
**Source:** AI Weekly
**Summary:** OpenAI has disbanded its dedicated robotics research team, with leadership stating that general-purpose foundation models have matured to the point where specialized robotics teams are no longer needed.
