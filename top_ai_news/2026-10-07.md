## [OpenAI Prices GPT-6.1 Sol at $2/$10 per Million Tokens](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)
**Source:** AI Weekly / Multiple outlets
**Summary:** OpenAI launched GPT-6.1 Sol at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens — one-fifth the price of GPT-6 Astra. Matters because it dramatically lowers the cost barrier for production-grade agentic applications.

## [Google DeepMind Publishes SynthID Bio in Nature: AI-Designed Protein Watermarking](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)
**Source:** AI Weekly / Nature / DeepMind Blog
**Summary:** Google DeepMind published SynthID Bio in Nature, a method to embed imperceptible watermarks into AI-generated protein sequences, enabling provenance tracking of computationally designed biomolecules. The work addresses growing concerns about the unattributed use of AI-designed proteins in research and pharmaceutical development. First major Nature publication applying digital watermarking to AI-generated biological sequences.
