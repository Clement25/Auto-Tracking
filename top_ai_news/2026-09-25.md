## [Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic Form Voluntary Frontier AI Standards Agency](https://aiagentstore.ai/ai-agent-news/this-week)
**Source:** AI Weekly / Multiple outlets
**Summary:** Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic are building a voluntary industry-led safety standards body tentatively called the Frontier AI Standards Agency, planned to launch in late 2026 or 2027 without government oversight. The three companies have approached Sriram Krishnan to serve as chief executive. This signals a major industry effort to self-regulate before potential government mandates arrive.

## [Anthropic's Claude Autonomously Discovers Novel CRISPR-Like Enzyme System](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/9/24/ai-model-claude-discovers-crispr-like-enzyme-system-anthropic-says)
**Source:** Al Jazeera / Anthropic
**Summary:** Anthropic's Claude AI autonomously identified a previously uncharacterized bacteriophage enzyme system called array-associated reverse transcriptases (ART), which may represent a new gene-editing mechanism analogous to CRISPR. This discovery came from Anthropic's newly launched life sciences research group based in San Francisco. It marks one of the first substantive autonomous scientific contributions by a frontier AI model in biology.

## [Claude AI Agent Is Building the Next Version of Itself, Anthropic Says](https://www.yahoo.com/news/videos/ai-agent-claude-building-next-184410112.html)
**Source:** Yahoo News / Anthropic
**Summary:** Anthropic revealed that Claude is being used as an agent to contribute to the development of its own next-generation version, with the AI participating in research, coding, and evaluation loops.
