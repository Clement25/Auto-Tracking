## [Anthropic Files S-1 for IPO, Revealing $16.3B Revenue and Plans to List as 'ANT'](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/anthropic-ipo-live-updates-ai-s-1-filing.html)
**Source:** CNBC / Anthropic
**Summary:** Anthropic officially filed its Form S-1 registration statement with the SEC on October 26, 2026, disclosing $16.3B in first-half 2026 revenue alongside an $80.7B reported loss. The company plans to list on the Nasdaq under the ticker "ANT" in mid-November, with an unusual provision allowing insiders to trade shares from day one. The filing also revealed Anthropic will pay SpaceX approximately $3.1B per month for NVIDIA GPU compute capacity once fully delivered.

## [Alphabet Stock Falls 12.6%, Wiping Out $500 Billion, as AI Mode Earns Less Revenue](https://aiagentstore.ai/ai-agent-news/daily/2026-10-30)
**Source:** Multiple / The Neuron AI Digest
**Summary:** Alphabet shares plunged 12.6% on October 30, erasing roughly $500 billion in market cap, after the company reported that its AI Mode search feature generates significantly less ad revenue per query than traditional search. The company did not raise its capital expenditure forecast for AI infrastructure, disappointing investors expecting continued aggressive spending. The sell-off rippled across the AI sector, with the VanEck Semiconductor ETF (SMH) dropping 7% and Nvidia losing 4%.

## [Big Banks Blocking AI Agents From Customer Accounts](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-october-30-2026/)
**Source:** The Neuron / Axios
**Summary:** Major U.S. banks have begun proactively blocking AI agents from accessing customer accounts after discovering the agents were authenticating using customers' own login credentials rather than authorized API integrations. No funds were reported stolen, but the practice raised significant security and liability concerns. The episode highlights a growing friction between consumer AI agent adoption and financial institution security infrastructure.

## [New York State Limits What AI Chatbots Can Say to Minors](https://riorundown.substack.com/p/ai-news-oct-30-2026-ai-infrastructure)
**Source:** Rio Rundown / State of New York
**Summary:** New York Governor signed legislation on October 28 restricting AI chatbot behavior when interacting with users under 18, prohibiting bots from claiming to be human, promoting self-harm, or generating sexual content for minors. The law marks one of the first U.S. state-level regulations specifically targeting AI conversational systems and their effects on young users. Industry observers expect other states to follow with similar or more expansive legislation.

## [Reddit CEO Says Google's AI Search Is Sending Fewer Visitors to Websites](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-october-30-2026/)
**Source:** The Neuron / Reddit
**Summary:** Reddit's CEO publicly stated that Google's AI Mode is directing less traffic to external websites, with Reddit recording a decline of over one million weekly Google search referrals during October. The company described AI Mode as effectively driving no traffic, as users get answers directly without clicking through. The statement adds a major platform's voice to ongoing publisher concerns about AI-generated search results cannibalizing web traffic.

## [Canada Commits Over $1 Billion to Canadian AI Company Cohere](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-october-30-2026/)

## [Chinese Hackers Used AI Tool with DeepSeek Model to Compromise Nine Organizations](https://riorundown.substack.com/p/ai-news-oct-30-2026-ai-infrastructure)
**Source:** Rio Rundown
**Summary:** A threat intelligence report revealed that a Chinese threat actor used an AI-assisted hacking tool built on a DeepSeek model to successfully compromise nine organizations out of approximately 200 targeted in a recent campaign. The tool automated reconnaissance, phishing content generation, and initial access steps, accelerating the attack lifecycle. The report is among the first public attributions of AI-augmented offensive operations to a nation-state actor.

## [ServiceNow Patches Critical Sandbox Escape in Its AI Platform](https://riorundown.substack.com/p/ai-news-oct-30-2026-ai-infrastructure)
**Source:** Rio Rundown
**Summary:** ServiceNow issued an emergency patch for a critical sandbox escape vulnerability in its Now AI Platform that could allow malicious code executed within an AI workflow to break out and interact with the underlying host system. The flaw was discovered by a third-party security researcher and rated critical due to the platform's widespread enterprise deployment. The patch was released October 29 and administrators are urged to apply it immediately.

## [AI Coding Spend Up 10x Since January; Now ~2% of Engineering Cost](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-october-30-2026/)
**Source:** The Neuron / Jellyfish Analytics
**Summary:** Analytics firm Jellyfish reports that enterprise spending on AI coding tools—including products like Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, and Cursor—has grown tenfold since January 2026, now representing roughly 2% of total engineering cost at large technology companies. The rapid adoption reflects both cost reduction efforts and developer productivity mandates from engineering leadership. Observers note that tool spending at this level is beginning to register as a meaningful line item in engineering budget reviews.
