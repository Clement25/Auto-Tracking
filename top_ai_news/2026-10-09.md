# Top AI News (2026-10-09)

## [OpenAI Launches GPT-6.1 Sol at Dramatically Lower Prices](https://www.microcenter.com/site/mc-news/article/this-week-in-ai-oct-2-2026.aspx)
**Source:** MicroCenter / Multiple Aggregators
**Summary:** OpenAI released GPT-6.1 Sol priced at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens — roughly one-fifth of GPT-6 Astra's pricing — while canceling the separate GPT-6.1 Astra release. The model delivers near-Astra performance for agentic coding, computer use, and professional tasks, making frontier-grade AI significantly more accessible for developers.

## [Google Releases Gemini 4 Argon to Cyber Defenders First](https://www.microcenter.com/site/mc-news/article/this-week-in-ai-oct-2-2026.aspx)
**Source:** MicroCenter / Tech Roundups
**Summary:** Google released Gemini 4 Argon — a security-focused model scoring 68% on CWE-bench v1 — to trusted cyber defenders before broader API and Ultra access. The model can autonomously find, validate, and fix critical vulnerabilities, and it remains in the U.S. government's voluntary pre-release access process, signaling growing AI integration into national cybersecurity infrastructure.

## [Anthropic Releases Claude Sonnet 5.5 with 30% Speed Boost and Cost Reduction](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news)
**Source:** LLM Stats / AI Weekly
**Summary:** Anthropic released Claude Sonnet 5.5, promising 30% faster inference at 30% lower cost compared to its predecessor, alongside a companion release of Claude Haiku 5.5. ## [OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta Decline to Guarantee AI Safety at NYC Council Hearing](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/05/anthropic-openai-google-meta-execs-testify-nyc-council-ai-hearing.html)
**Source:** CNBC
**Summary:** Senior representatives from OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, and Google testified before New York City lawmakers, declining to guarantee their AI agents will always follow safety guardrails, with executives citing the impossibility of eliminating all risk. The hearing reflects escalating pressure on AI labs from local and state governments ahead of anticipated federal regulation.
