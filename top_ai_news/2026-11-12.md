## [Google DeepMind Releases Gemini Robotics 2.5 and AlphaGenome 2](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/11/google-deepmind-releases-new-advanced-robotics-ai-model-and-next-generation-gene-prediction-model/)
**Source:** SiliconANGLE
**Summary:** Google DeepMind unveiled two major models on November 11: Gemini Robotics 2.5, tested across 200+ distinct manipulation and locomotion tasks, and AlphaGenome 2, a DNA foundation model that predicts how genetic variants affect cell-type-specific gene expression. ## [New York's AI Safety Law Takes Effect, Covers Anthropic and OpenAI](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)
**Source:** AI Weekly
**Summary:** New York's AI safety legislation took effect today, imposing new transparency and oversight requirements on frontier AI developers including Anthropic and OpenAI operating in the state. ## [United Airlines Restricts Unauthorized AI Agents from Booking Travel](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)
**Source:** AI Weekly
**Summary:** United Airlines announced it will block unauthorized third-party AI agents from accessing its booking, modification, and purchase systems, citing account security concerns and compliance with FAA and DOT regulations. It may set a precedent for how other travel and financial service providers respond to agentic automation.

**Summary:** Google Research used AI to generate mathematical proofs that formally verify parts of Signal's end-to-end encryption implementation.
