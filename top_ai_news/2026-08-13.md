## [DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 Officially Launches with 1.6 Trillion Parameters and Agentic Focus](https://www.unite.ai/deepseek-ships-v4-pro-as-its-flagship-model-leaves-preview/)
**Source:** Unite.AI / Deccan Chronicle / Global Times
**Summary:** DeepSeek quietly pushed the official `DeepSeek-V4-Pro-0813` model live at ~11 PM Beijing time on Aug 13. Under the hood: 1.6 T total parameters (49 B active per token), pretrained on 32 T+ tokens, with a hybrid attention system supporting a 1 M-token context window and up to 384 K output tokens. The release explicitly targets agentic workloads—tool use, code execution, and multi-step workflows—and is available via API, app, and web. Pricing starts at $0.435/M input tokens (cache miss); a price increase is forthcoming. Signals ongoing Chinese lab pressure on US frontier model providers.

The launch accelerates the competitive race for agent-optimized frontier models.
