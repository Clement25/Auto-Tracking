## [Anthropic's IPO Filing Shows $80B Loss — Mostly Paper](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/ai-news-2026-10-21/)
**Source:** Wall Street Journal / AI News aggregators
**Summary:** Anthropic's draft S-1 prospectus, obtained by the WSJ, reports an $80.7 billion net loss for the first half of 2026. Roughly $72 billion of this is non-cash charges from revaluing financing instruments, making it primarily a paper loss rather than cash burned. The filing also reveals that two customers account for about a fifth of total revenue, highlighting concentration risk as the company prepares to go public.

## [US Bans DeepSeek Across Federal Agencies, Putting AI Supply Chains Under Scrutiny](https://www.cio.com/article/4238132/us-bans-deepseek-across-federal-agencies-putting-ai-supply-chains-under-scrutiny.html)
**Source:** CIO.com
**Summary:** The US government has officially banned the use of DeepSeek AI models across federal agencies, citing national security concerns over data handling and model provenance. The ban has also put broader AI supply chains under scrutiny, as procurement officials re-examine dependencies on foreign-developed models and datasets. The move marks one of the most significant government restrictions on a specific AI model to date.
