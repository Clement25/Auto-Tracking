# Top AI News (2026-10-05)

## [Trump Announces Creation of 'Super Intelligence Force' AI Task Force](https://abcnews.com/Politics/president-donald-trump-announces-creation-super-intelligence-force/story?id=136986122)
**Source:** ABC News
**Summary:** President Trump formally created the "Super Intelligence Force," a federal AI task force led by Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton, designed to promote US AI dominance while managing national security risks. The body will coordinate the administration's AI initiatives, analyze risks and opportunities, and work with the private sector. It signals a major escalation in government involvement in AI policy and competition with China.
