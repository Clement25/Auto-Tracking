## Self-Made Meal Recipes

### Scrambled Eggs with Spinach on Toast *(Mon Breakfast)*
- **Ingredients:** 3 large eggs, 1 cup baby spinach, 1 slice wholegrain bread, 1 tsp olive oil, salt & pepper
- **Prep:** 10 min. Heat oil in pan, add spinach and wilt 2 min, pour in beaten eggs, stir gently on medium-low heat until just set. Serve on toasted bread.

### Overnight Oats with Protein Powder *(Tue Breakfast)*
- **Ingredients:** ½ cup rolled oats, 1 scoop vanilla whey protein, ¾ cup unsweetened almond milk, 1 tbsp chia seeds, handful blueberries
- **Prep:** 5 min (night before). Combine oats, protein powder, almond milk and chia in a jar. Stir, seal, refrigerate overnight. Top with fruit in the morning.

### Greek Yogurt Banana Bowl *(Wed Breakfast)*
- **Ingredients:** 200 g plain Greek yogurt, 1 banana (sliced), 1 tbsp mixed seeds
- **Prep:** 2 min. Spoon yogurt into bowl, top with banana and seeds.

### Avocado Toast with Poached Eggs *(Thu Breakfast)*
- **Ingredients:** 1 slice wholegrain bread, ½ avocado (mashed), 2 eggs, lemon juice, salt & pepper
- **Prep:** 15 min. Poach eggs 3–4 min. Toast bread. Mash avocado with lemon, spread on toast, top with eggs.

### Smoked Salmon Crackers *(Sat Breakfast)*
- **Ingredients:** 80 g smoked salmon, 2 wholegrain crispbreads, 2 tbsp low-fat cream cheese, ½ cucumber (sliced), black pepper, lemon
- **Prep:** 5 min. Spread cream cheese, layer salmon and cucumber, season and squeeze lemon.

### Protein Oatmeal *(Sun Breakfast)*
- **Ingredients:** ½ cup rolled oats, 1 scoop protein powder, ¾ cup almond milk, 1 banana (sliced), handful mixed berries
- **Prep:** 5 min. Cook oats in almond milk on stovetop 3–4 min. Stir in protein powder. Top with fruit.

### Scrambled Eggs with Mushrooms & Tomato *(Fri Breakfast)*
- **Ingredients:** 3 eggs, 80 g mushrooms (sliced), 1 medium tomato (diced), 1 tsp olive oil, salt & pepper
- **Prep:** 12 min. Sauté mushrooms 3 min, add tomato, cook 2 more min. Add beaten eggs, stir until just set.

### Grilled Chicken Breast with Broccoli & Brown Rice *(Mon Dinner)*
- **Ingredients:** 150 g chicken breast, 150 g broccoli florets, ½ cup cooked brown rice, 1 tsp olive oil, 2 garlic cloves, salt & pepper
- **Prep:** 25 min. Marinate chicken with olive oil, garlic, salt, pepper 10 min. Grill or pan-sear 6–7 min per side until cooked through. Steam broccoli 6–7 min. Serve over rice.

### Baked Salmon with Roasted Broccoli & Quinoa *(Tue Dinner)*
- **Ingredients:** 180 g salmon fillet, 150 g broccoli, ½ cup cooked quinoa, 1 tsp olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, mixed dried herbs
- **Prep:** 30 min. Preheat oven 200°C. Season salmon with olive oil, lemon and garlic. Bake 12–15 min. Toss broccoli with olive oil, roast alongside for the last 10 min. Serve over quinoa.

### Chicken & Vegetable Stir-fry with Brown Rice *(Wed Dinner)*
- **Ingredients:** 150 g chicken breast (sliced), 100 g bok choy (halved), ½ capsicum (sliced), 80 g mushrooms, ½ cup cooked brown rice, 1 tbsp light soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil, garlic, ginger
- **Prep:** 20 min. Heat wok, stir-fry garlic and ginger 30 sec. Add chicken, cook 4–5 min. Add vegetables, stir-fry 3–4 min. Add soy sauce and sesame oil. Serve over rice.

### Steamed Seabass with Ginger & Bok Choy *(Thu Dinner)*
- **Ingredients:** 180 g seabass fillet (or red snapper), 100 g bok choy, 3 slices fresh ginger, 2 spring onions (julienned), 1 tbsp light soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil, ½ cup cooked brown rice
- **Prep:** 20 min. Steam seabass with ginger slices 8–10 min until cooked. Steam bok choy alongside. Transfer fish and veg to a plate, drizzle with soy and sesame oil, top with spring onions. Serve with rice.

### Prawn Stir-fry with Brown Rice *(Fri Dinner)*
- **Ingredients:** 150 g medium prawns (peeled, deveined), ½ zucchini (sliced), ½ capsicum (sliced), 3 garlic cloves (minced), 1 tbsp light soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil, ½ cup cooked brown rice
- **Prep:** 15 min. Heat wok with 1 tsp oil, sauté garlic 30 sec, add prawns and cook 2–3 min until pink. Add vegetables, stir-fry 3–4 min. Add soy and sesame oil. Serve over rice.

### Home Chicken Grain Bowl *(Sat Lunch)*
- **Ingredients:** 150 g grilled chicken breast (sliced), ½ cup cooked quinoa, ¼ avocado (diced), handful cherry tomatoes (halved), 2 cups mixed salad greens, 1 tbsp lemon juice, 1 tsp olive oil, salt & pepper
- **Prep:** 25 min (includes chicken grilling). Assemble: quinoa base, greens, sliced chicken, avocado, tomatoes. Drizzle with lemon-olive oil dressing.

### Tuna & Avocado Brown Rice Bowl *(Sun Lunch)*
- **Ingredients:** 120 g canned tuna in spring water (drained), ½ cup cooked brown rice, ¼ avocado (sliced), ½ cucumber (sliced), 50 g shelled edamame, 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 tsp rice vinegar, sesame seeds
- **Prep:** 15 min. Mix tuna with sesame oil, soy sauce and rice vinegar. Assemble bowl: rice base, tuna, avocado, cucumber, edamame. Sprinkle sesame seeds.

### Baked Herb Chicken Thighs with Roasted Vegetables *(Sun Dinner)*
- **Ingredients:** 200 g boneless skinless chicken thighs, ½ zucchini (chunks), ½ capsicum (chunks), 80 g mushrooms, handful cherry tomatoes, 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp dried rosemary, 1 tsp dried thyme, 3 garlic cloves (minced), salt & pepper
- **Prep:** 40 min. Preheat oven 200°C. Rub chicken with olive oil, herbs, garlic, salt, pepper. Toss vegetables with olive oil. Place chicken on a baking tray, roast 15 min, add vegetables, roast 15–20 min more until chicken is cooked through.
