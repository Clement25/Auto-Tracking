## Self-Made Meal Recipes

### Grilled Chicken Breast with Brown Rice & Vegetables
**Serves:** 1 | **Prep time:** 5 min | **Cook time:** 20 min | **~530 kcal | 48g P**
- 150g chicken breast, marinate in 1 tsp soy sauce, garlic, ginger, black pepper
- Grill or pan-sear 6–7 min per side until cooked through
- Steam 200g mixed vegetables (broccoli + carrots); serve with 120g cooked brown rice

### Egg Omelette with Wholegrain Toast
**Serves:** 1 | **Prep time:** 2 min | **Cook time:** 5 min | **~360 kcal | 28g P**
- Whisk 3 egg whites + 1 whole egg; season with salt and pepper
- Spray pan with cooking oil; add 60g fresh spinach and 50g sliced mushrooms; cook 2 min
- Pour egg mix over veg; fold in half; serve with 1 slice toasted wholegrain bread

### Quinoa Chicken Bento (Packed Lunch)
**Serves:** 1 | **Prep time:** 10 min (night before) | **~430 kcal | 40g P**
- Cook 60g dry quinoa (~100g cooked); let cool
- Grill 120g chicken breast strips with light seasoning
- Assemble with sliced cucumber, halved cherry tomatoes, edamame
- Dressing: 1 tsp olive oil, 1 tsp lemon juice, pinch of salt

### Overnight Oats
**Serves:** 1 | **Prep time:** 5 min (night before) | **~300 kcal (without protein powder) | 12g P**
- Combine 50g rolled oats + 200ml low-fat milk + 1 tbsp chia seeds in a jar
- Stir well; refrigerate overnight
- Top with 1 sliced banana; optional: mix in 1 scoop protein powder before eating

### Pan-Seared Salmon with Roasted Vegetables
**Serves:** 1 | **Prep time:** 5 min | **Cook time:** 20 min | **~490 kcal | 40g P**
- Season 150g salmon fillet with salt, pepper, and lemon zest
- Pan-sear skin-side down 4–5 min; flip and cook 3 min
- Toss 200g mixed veg (zucchini, cherry tomatoes, asparagus) with 1 tsp olive oil; roast at 200°C for 15–18 min alongside

### Tofu Stir-Fry with Brown Rice
**Serves:** 1 | **Prep time:** 5 min | **Cook time:** 10 min | **~490 kcal | 26g P**
- Press and cube 200g firm tofu; pan-fry until golden on all sides
- Add 2 cloves garlic (minced), 150g bok choy, 80g shiitake mushrooms
- Season with 1 tbsp light soy sauce + ½ tsp sesame oil; toss well
- Serve with 120g cooked brown rice

### Baked Sea Bass with Sweet Potato
**Serves:** 1 | **Prep time:** 5 min | **Cook time:** 25 min | **~520 kcal | 44g P**
- Marinate 180g sea bass fillet in lemon juice, garlic, herbs (thyme/rosemary), 1 tsp olive oil, salt
- Dice 150g sweet potato; toss with cooking spray and roast at 200°C for 20 min
- Place fish on baking tray; bake at 200°C for 18–20 min until flaky
- Steam 150g broccoli; plate everything together

### Chicken Lettuce Wraps with Quinoa
**Serves:** 1 | **Prep time:** 5 min | **Cook time:** 12 min | **~490 kcal | 42g P**
- Stir-fry 150g minced chicken with 2 cloves garlic, 50g water chestnuts (diced), julienned carrots
- Season with 1 tbsp low-sodium oyster sauce + ½ tbsp light soy sauce; cook until dry
- Serve in iceberg lettuce cups; pair with 80g cooked quinoa

### High-Protein Smoothie
**Serves:** 1 | **Prep time:** 3 min | **~380 kcal | 32g P**
- Blend: 1 scoop vanilla/chocolate protein powder + 200ml low-fat milk + 1 banana + 1 tbsp peanut butter + 1 handful fresh spinach + 5–6 ice cubes

### Chia Seed Pudding
**Serves:** 1 | **Prep time:** 5 min (set overnight) | **~220 kcal | 10g P (before toppings)**
- Mix 3 tbsp chia seeds + 200ml low-fat milk + ½ tsp vanilla extract in a jar or bowl
- Refrigerate overnight; stir once after 15 min to prevent clumping
- Top with 100g mixed berries and 1 tsp honey before eating

## Key Tips for the Week

1. **Meal prep Sunday:** Cook a batch of brown rice and quinoa to last 3–4 days. Pre-grill chicken portions and store in the fridge (keeps 3 days).
2. **At The Soup Spoon Union:** Always choose broth/tomato-based soups over cream-based. Ask for the wholegrain roll only if within your calorie budget.
3. **At the hawker centre:** Always choose steamed over fried. Ask for less rice (½ portion) and request extra vegetables.
4. **Hydration:** Aim for 2–2.5 litres of water daily. A glass of water before each meal naturally reduces portion sizes.
5. **Protein first:** At every meal, eat your protein portion first — it improves satiety and reduces overall calorie intake.
6. **Snack timing:** Eat Snack 1 mid-morning (~10:30am) and Snack 2 mid-afternoon (~3:30pm) to prevent afternoon overeating.
