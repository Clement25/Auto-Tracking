## Self-Made Meal Recipes

### Greek Yogurt Parfait
**Ingredients:** 200 g low-fat Greek yogurt · 80 g mixed berries · 30 g low-sugar granola · 1 tsp honey
**Method:** Layer yogurt, berries, granola in a bowl. Drizzle honey. Ready in 3 min.
**Macros:** ~350 kcal | 25 g P | 45 g C | 8 g F

### Scrambled Eggs on Whole-Wheat Toast with Avocado
**Ingredients:** 3 large eggs · 2 slices whole-wheat bread · ¼ avocado · sliced tomato · salt & pepper
**Method:** Whisk eggs, cook over low heat until just set. Toast bread. Top with eggs, sliced avocado, tomato. 10 min.
**Macros:** ~400 kcal | 28 g P | 32 g C | 20 g F

### Overnight Oats with Protein Powder
**Ingredients:** ½ cup rolled oats · 200 ml unsweetened almond milk · 1 scoop vanilla protein powder · 1 banana · 1 tbsp chia seeds
**Method:** Mix all ingredients in a jar. Refrigerate overnight. Grab and go in the morning. 5 min prep.
**Macros:** ~380 kcal | 30 g P | 52 g C | 8 g F

### Egg-White Omelette with Spinach & Feta
**Ingredients:** 4 egg whites · 1 whole egg · 1 cup baby spinach · 30 g light feta · cherry tomatoes · cooking spray
**Method:** Whisk eggs, pour into non-stick pan. Add spinach and feta. Fold. 8 min.
**Macros:** ~280 kcal | 25 g P | 5 g C | 14 g F

### Poached Eggs on Whole-Grain Toast
**Ingredients:** 2 large eggs · 2 slices whole-grain bread · sliced tomato · baby spinach · salt & pepper
**Method:** Simmer water, add vinegar, swirl, drop eggs in, cook 3–4 min. Toast bread. 12 min.
**Macros:** ~320 kcal | 22 g P | 30 g C | 12 g F

### Protein Smoothie Bowl
**Ingredients:** 1 scoop vanilla protein powder · 150 ml unsweetened almond milk · 100 g frozen mixed berries · ½ banana · 20 g granola (topping)
**Method:** Blend protein, milk, and berries until thick. Pour into bowl. Top with granola and sliced strawberries. 5 min.
**Macros:** ~350 kcal | 30 g P | 42 g C | 7 g F

### Peanut Butter Banana Toast
**Ingredients:** 2 slices whole-grain bread · 2 tbsp natural peanut butter · 1 banana · 1 tsp chia seeds
**Method:** Toast bread. Spread peanut butter. Top with banana slices and chia seeds. 3 min.
**Macros:** ~380 kcal | 14 g P | 52 g C | 16 g F

### Grilled Chicken Bento (Meal Prep)
**Ingredients:** 120 g chicken breast · ½ cup cooked brown rice · 1 cup broccoli · 1 medium carrot · 1 tsp sesame oil · low-sodium soy sauce
**Method:** Season and grill chicken 6 min per side. Steam broccoli and carrot. Serve over rice. 25 min.
**Macros:** ~450 kcal | 40 g P | 42 g C | 10 g F

### Baked Salmon with Roasted Vegetables
**Ingredients:** 150 g salmon fillet · 200 g mixed vegetables (zucchini, capsicum, cherry tomatoes) · 1 tsp olive oil · lemon juice · fresh herbs
**Method:** Preheat oven 200°C. Toss veg in olive oil, season. Bake veg 15 min. Add salmon, bake further 12–15 min. 30 min total.
**Macros:** ~500 kcal | 40 g P | 20 g C | 26 g F

### Stir-Fried Tofu with Mixed Vegetables & Brown Rice
**Ingredients:** 200 g firm tofu (cubed) · 1 cup bok choy · 1 cup mushrooms · 1 cup beansprouts · ½ cup cooked brown rice · 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce · 1 tsp sesame oil · garlic
**Method:** Press and cube tofu, pan-fry until golden. Add garlic, vegetables, stir-fry 5 min. Add soy sauce. Serve over rice. 20 min.
**Macros:** ~480 kcal | 28 g P | 52 g C | 16 g F

### Tuna Whole-Wheat Sandwich
**Ingredients:** 1 can (150 g) tuna in water (drained) · 2 tbsp light mayo · 2 stalks celery (diced) · lettuce · tomato · 2 slices whole-wheat bread
**Method:** Mix tuna, mayo, celery. Spread on bread with lettuce and tomato. 5 min.
**Macros:** ~380 kcal | 35 g P | 32 g C | 10 g F

### Turkey & Veggie Wrap
**Ingredients:** 100 g sliced turkey breast · 1 large whole-wheat tortilla · ½ avocado (sliced) · romaine lettuce · tomato slices · 1 tbsp mustard
**Method:** Lay tortilla flat, add fillings, roll tightly. 5 min.
**Macros:** ~420 kcal | 38 g P | 32 g C | 14 g F

### Pan-Seared Chicken Breast with Sweet Potato
**Ingredients:** 150 g chicken breast · 150 g sweet potato · 150 g green beans · 1 tsp olive oil · rosemary, garlic, salt & pepper
**Method:** Bake sweet potato 35 min at 200°C. Season chicken, sear 6 min/side. Steam green beans 5 min. 40 min total.
**Macros:** ~480 kcal | 38 g P | 38 g C | 14 g F

### Prawn & Vegetable Stir-Fry with Rice Vermicelli
**Ingredients:** 150 g prawns (peeled) · 60 g rice vermicelli (dry) · 1 cup bok choy · 1 cup mushrooms · 1 cup beansprouts · 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce · 1 tsp sesame oil · garlic & ginger
**Method:** Soak vermicelli per packet. Stir-fry garlic, ginger, prawns 3 min. Add veg, cook 3 min. Add drained vermicelli, sauce, toss. 15 min.
**Macros:** ~420 kcal | 32 g P | 52 g C | 8 g F

## Fat-Loss Tips for the Week
1. **Drink 2–3 L of water daily** — reduces hunger and supports metabolism.
2. **Use smaller plates at home** — maintains portion control without measuring every meal.
3. **Order sauces on the side** at restaurants (especially SaladStop!) — saves 80–150 kcal per meal.
4. **Choose steamed over fried** at hawker centres — halves the fat content.
5. **Eat protein first** at every meal — increases satiety and preserves muscle during a deficit.
6. **Meal-prep Sunday evening** — pre-make chicken bento boxes and overnight oats to avoid Monday/Tuesday decision fatigue.
7. **Walk 10–15 min after dinner** — improves insulin sensitivity and helps with digestion.
