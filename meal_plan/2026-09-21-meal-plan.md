## Self-Made Meal Recipes

### Scrambled Eggs with Spinach on Toast
- **Ingredients:** 2 whole eggs, 2 egg whites, 1 handful baby spinach, 1 tsp olive oil, salt & pepper, 1 slice whole grain bread
- **Method:** Heat oil in non-stick pan. Add eggs; scramble on medium-low. Wilt spinach in last 30 s. Serve on toast.
- **Prep time:** 8 min

### Baked Salmon with Broccoli & Brown Rice
- **Ingredients:** 150 g salmon fillet, 200 g broccoli florets, 100 g dry brown rice, lemon juice, garlic powder, pepper, 1 tsp olive oil
- **Method:** Season salmon with lemon juice, garlic powder, pepper. Bake at 200°C for 15–18 min. Steam broccoli 5 min. Cook rice per packet (use ~50 g dry). Serve together.
- **Prep time:** 25 min

### Greek Yogurt Parfait
- **Ingredients:** 200 g plain low-fat Greek yogurt, 100 g mixed berries (frozen ok), 1 tbsp chia seeds, 1 tbsp rolled oats
- **Method:** Layer yogurt, berries, top with chia and oats. Can prep the night before.
- **Prep time:** 3 min

### Grilled Chicken Breast with Stir-Fried Vegetables & Brown Rice
- **Ingredients:** 180 g chicken breast, 150 g kai lan (or chye sim), 100 g sliced mushrooms, 1 medium carrot (julienned), 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 130 g cooked brown rice
- **Method:** Pound chicken to even thickness; season with pepper and garlic. Grill/pan-sear on medium-high 6 min per side. Rest 3 min. Stir-fry veg in sesame oil 3–4 min; add soy sauce. Serve with rice.
- **Prep time:** 20 min

### Overnight Oats
- **Ingredients:** 50 g rolled oats, 200 ml low-fat milk, 1 scoop vanilla protein powder, 1 tbsp almond butter, ½ banana (sliced)
- **Method:** Mix oats, milk, protein powder in a jar. Refrigerate overnight. Top with almond butter and banana in the morning.
- **Prep time:** 3 min (night before)

### Tuna Pita with Side Salad
- **Ingredients:** 150 g canned tuna in water (drained), 1 celery stalk (diced), 1 tbsp light mayo, ½ tsp Dijon mustard, pepper, 1 whole grain pita, cucumber & tomato for salad
- **Method:** Mix tuna, celery, mayo, mustard. Season with pepper. Fill pita. Pack side salad with a squeeze of lemon.
- **Prep time:** 5 min

### Banana Protein Smoothie
- **Ingredients:** 1 ripe banana, 200 ml low-fat milk, 1 scoop protein powder (vanilla or unflavoured), 1 tbsp peanut butter, handful of ice
- **Method:** Blend all until smooth.
- **Prep time:** 3 min

### Steamed Fish with Tofu & Vegetables
- **Ingredients:** 180 g white fish fillet (seabass or cod), 100 g silken tofu, 200 g bok choy, 2 slices ginger, 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil, 100 g cooked brown rice
- **Method:** Place fish on a steaming plate; lay ginger on top. Add tofu alongside. Steam 10–12 min. Pour soy sauce + sesame oil over. Steam bok choy 3 min separately.
- **Prep time:** 20 min

### Quinoa Power Bowl
- **Ingredients:** 100 g cooked quinoa, 100 g grilled chicken (leftover ok), 10 cherry tomatoes (halved), ½ cucumber (sliced), 1 tbsp olive oil, juice of ½ lemon, salt & pepper
- **Method:** Arrange quinoa and chicken in a bowl. Top with vegetables. Drizzle olive oil and lemon juice.
- **Prep time:** 10 min (if chicken pre-cooked)

### Veggie Egg Muffins (Batch Cook for Sunday)
- **Ingredients (makes 6):** 6 eggs, ½ red bell pepper (diced), 100 g mushrooms (sliced), handful baby spinach, 2 tbsp low-fat milk, salt & pepper
- **Method:** Preheat oven 180°C. Grease a 6-cup muffin tin. Divide veg among cups. Whisk eggs with milk, season, pour over veg. Bake 20 min until set. Store remaining in fridge up to 3 days.
- **Prep time:** 30 min

### Salmon & Avocado Brown Rice Bowl
- **Ingredients:** 150 g salmon fillet, 120 g cooked brown rice, ½ avocado (sliced), 50 g edamame (cooked), 1 tsp sesame seeds, 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 tsp rice vinegar
- **Method:** Season salmon; pan-sear on medium-high 3–4 min per side or bake 15 min at 200°C. Assemble bowl: rice, salmon, avocado, edamame. Drizzle soy + vinegar, top with sesame seeds.
- **Prep time:** 20 min

## Customisation Tips for Eating Out

- **Chicken rice:** Always choose steamed over roasted; request half portion of rice; ask for ginger sauce (skip chilli); add extra steamed veg
- **Thunder Tea Rice:** Opt for brown rice; ask for extra tofu; reduce peanut quantity to lower fat
- **Steam fish stalls:** Request ginger and spring onion preparation only; avoid black bean sauce (higher sodium); ask for extra steamed greens
- **SaladStop!:** Choose quinoa or mixed grains as base; use their Nutritional Calculator app to track; avoid creamy dressings; add a boiled egg for extra protein
- **Soup Spoon:** Skip the bread add-on; choose broth-based soups (not chowders) to stay under 300 kcal for the soup
