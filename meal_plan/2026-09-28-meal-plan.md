## Self-Made Meal Recipes

### Breakfasts

**Spinach & Mushroom Omelette** (15 min)
- 2 eggs, beaten + salt + pepper
- 1 tsp olive oil, heat pan over medium-high
- Sauté 80 g sliced mushrooms + large handful spinach (2 min)
- Pour in eggs, fold once set; serve with 2 slices toasted whole-grain bread

**Overnight Oats** (5 min, prep night before)
- 50 g rolled oats + 200 ml low-fat milk + 1 tbsp chia seeds + 1 tsp honey
- Mix in jar, refrigerate overnight; top with 50 g blueberries in the morning

**Protein Smoothie** (5 min)
- 1 scoop whey protein + 1 banana + large handful spinach + 250 ml unsweetened almond milk
- Blend until smooth; add ice for texture

**Avocado Toast with Poached Eggs** (15 min)
- Toast 2 slices whole-grain bread
- Mash ½ avocado with lemon juice + pinch of salt
- Poach 2 eggs (simmer water, vinegar splash, swirl, 3 min); top toast

**Scrambled Eggs with Smoked Salmon** (10 min)
- Whisk 2 eggs + 1 tbsp milk + pinch salt
- Cook in non-stick pan over low heat, stirring gently
- Serve with 60 g smoked salmon on 1 slice whole-grain toast + lemon wedge

**3-Egg Feta Omelette** (10 min)
- Whisk 3 eggs; pour into oiled pan over medium heat
- Add spinach leaves + cherry tomatoes + 30 g crumbled feta
- Fold and serve

**Smoked Salmon Avocado Toast** (10 min)
- Toast 2 slices whole-grain bread
- Spread ¼ avocado + squeeze of lemon
- Layer 80 g smoked salmon on top; season with cracked pepper

### Lunches

**Grilled Chicken Bento (Packed)** (20 min, can prep in batches)
- Season 150 g chicken breast with soy sauce, ginger, garlic, pepper
- Pan-fry or grill 6–7 min each side; slice
- Pack with 100 g cooked brown rice + roasted capsicum, zucchini, onion (15 min at 200°C oven)

**Grilled Chicken Salad** (15 min)
- Grill or pan-fry 150 g chicken breast; slice
- Toss with romaine lettuce, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, red onion
- Dress with 1 tbsp olive oil + juice of ½ lemon + salt + pepper

---

### Dinners

**Grilled Chicken Breast + Brown Rice + Steamed Broccoli** (25 min)
- Marinate 170 g chicken breast with soy sauce + garlic + pepper (10 min)
- Grill or bake at 200°C for 20–22 min
- Steam 200 g broccoli 5 min; serve with 120 g cooked brown rice

**Baked Salmon + Asparagus + Quinoa** (30 min)
- Season 160 g salmon fillet with lemon zest, dill, salt, pepper
- Bake at 200°C for 12–15 min
- Roast 150 g asparagus (1 tsp olive oil) alongside for last 10 min
- Cook quinoa: 80 g dry + 160 ml water, simmer 15 min

**Pan-Fried Chicken + Bok Choy + Brown Rice** (20 min)
- Cut 170 g chicken breast into cubes; season with low-sodium soy sauce + garlic + ginger
- Stir-fry 5–6 min until cooked; remove
- Stir-fry 200 g bok choy + shiitake mushrooms in same pan (2 min) + 1 tsp sesame oil
- Serve over 100 g cooked brown rice

**Pan-Seared Seabass + Cauliflower Mash + Green Beans** (25 min)
- Pat 160 g seabass dry; season with salt, pepper, lemon zest
- Sear in hot pan 3 min each side (skin down first)
- Microwave 200 g cauliflower florets 5 min; mash with 1 tsp olive oil + salt
- Steam 150 g green beans 4 min

**Grilled Chicken Thigh + Sweet Potato + Green Beans** (30 min)
- Season 150 g skinless chicken thigh with garlic powder + herbs + salt
- Grill or bake at 200°C for 25 min
- Roast 100 g diced sweet potato alongside (20 min, 1 tsp olive oil)
- Steam 150 g green beans 4 min
