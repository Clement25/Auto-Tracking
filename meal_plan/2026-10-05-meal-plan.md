Right next door to your address. Open daily.

| Restaurant | Recommended Dish | Est. Calories | Why It Fits |

## Self-Made Meal Recipes

### Breakfasts

#### Scrambled Eggs + Turkey Rashers (Mon)
- **Ingredients:** 3 large eggs, 2 lean turkey rashers, 1 slice whole-wheat bread, 30 g baby spinach, olive oil spray
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Method:** Spray pan, cook turkey rashers 2 min each side. Whisk eggs + salt; scramble on low heat. Wilt spinach in same pan. Toast bread. Plate together.
- **~380 kcal | 30 g P | 20 g C | 18 g F**

#### Overnight Oats (Tue)
- **Ingredients:** 50 g rolled oats, 200 ml low-fat milk, 1 scoop vanilla protein powder, 1 banana (sliced)
- **Prep time:** 5 min (night before)
- **Method:** Mix oats + milk + protein powder in a jar. Refrigerate overnight. Top with banana slices in the morning.
- **~405 kcal | 35 g P | 55 g C | 8 g F**

#### Smoked Salmon Omelette (Wed)
- **Ingredients:** 3 large eggs, 50 g smoked salmon (sliced), 1 tbsp skimmed milk, 1 slice WW toast, olive oil spray
- **Prep time:** 8 min
- **Method:** Whisk eggs + milk. Spray pan, pour egg mix, cook on medium. Lay salmon on one half, fold over. Serve with toast.
- **~360 kcal | 33 g P | 18 g C | 18 g F**

#### Greek Yogurt Protein Bowl (Thu)
- **Ingredients:** 200 g plain Greek yogurt, 1 scoop protein powder, 30 g rolled oats, 80 g mixed berries (fresh or frozen/thawed)
- **Prep time:** 3 min
- **Method:** Mix protein powder into yogurt. Top with oats and berries.
- **~380 kcal | 35 g P | 40 g C | 5 g F**

#### Cottage Cheese Plate (Fri)
- **Ingredients:** 150 g low-fat cottage cheese, 2 plain rice cakes, ½ cucumber (sliced), 1 hard-boiled egg
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Method:** Arrange on plate. Season cottage cheese with black pepper and chives.
- **~320 kcal | 28 g P | 22 g C | 10 g F**

#### Protein Smoothie (Sat)
- **Ingredients:** 200 ml low-fat milk, 1 scoop vanilla/unflavoured protein powder, 1 banana, 1 tbsp almond butter, ice cubes
- **Prep time:** 3 min
- **Method:** Blend all ingredients until smooth. Drink immediately.
- **~400 kcal | 35 g P | 45 g C | 14 g F**

#### Veggie Omelette + Toast (Sun)
- **Ingredients:** 3 large eggs, 50 g button mushrooms, 1 small tomato, 30 g baby spinach, 1 slice WW toast, 200 ml low-fat milk (to drink)
- **Prep time:** 12 min
- **Method:** Sauté mushrooms and tomato 3 min. Add spinach, wilt. Pour whisked eggs over veg, cook until set. Fold and serve with toast.
- **~380 kcal | 30 g P | 28 g C | 15 g F**

### Lunches (Self-Made / Packed)

#### Meal-Prep Grilled Chicken + Quinoa Bowl (Wed)
- **Ingredients:** 150 g chicken breast, 70 g quinoa (dry), 150 g mixed roast veg (courgette, capsicum, red onion), 1 tsp olive oil, lemon juice, salt, pepper
- **Prep time:** 25 min (can prep the night before)
- **Method:** Cook quinoa. Season chicken, grill or pan-sear 5–6 min each side. Toss veg with oil, roast 20 min at 200°C. Pack everything in a container.
- **~480 kcal | 38 g P | 42 g C | 14 g F**

#### Tuna Salad Wrap (Sat)
- **Ingredients:** 120 g tuna in spring water (drained), 1 large whole-wheat wrap, 2 large lettuce leaves, 1 small tomato, 1 tsp yellow mustard
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Method:** Mix tuna with mustard and seasoning. Lay lettuce + tomato on wrap. Spoon tuna mixture on top. Wrap tightly.
- **~400 kcal | 35 g P | 35 g C | 8 g F**

#### Grilled Chicken Salad (Sun)
- **Ingredients:** 150 g chicken breast (grilled), 80 g mixed salad greens, 10 cherry tomatoes, ½ cucumber, 1 tsp extra-virgin olive oil, juice of ½ lemon
- **Prep time:** 15 min
- **Method:** Grill chicken, slice. Toss greens, tomatoes, cucumber with oil and lemon. Top with chicken.
- **~380 kcal | 38 g P | 12 g C | 14 g F**

### Dinners (Self-Made)

#### Baked Salmon + Asparagus + Quinoa (Mon, replicated as needed)
- **Ingredients:** 170 g salmon fillet, 150 g asparagus, 80 g quinoa (dry), 1 tsp olive oil, lemon zest, dill
- **Prep time:** 25 min
- **Method:** Cook quinoa per packet. Toss asparagus with oil + salt, place on baking tray. Season salmon with lemon + dill, place beside veg. Bake at 200°C for 12–15 min until salmon is cooked through.
- **~520 kcal | 43 g P | 38 g C | 18 g F**

#### Grilled Chicken + Bok Choy + Brown Rice (Tue)
- **Ingredients:** 180 g chicken breast, 150 g bok choy, 100 g cooked brown rice (~45 g dry), 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tsp low-sodium soy sauce, garlic (1 clove)
- **Prep time:** 20 min
- **Method:** Cook brown rice. Season chicken with salt, pepper, garlic. Grill/pan-sear 6 min each side. Stir-fry bok choy with sesame oil + soy sauce 3–4 min.
- **~470 kcal | 42 g P | 38 g C | 10 g F**

#### Pan-Seared Sea Bass + Vegetables + Sweet Potato (Thu)
- **Ingredients:** 200 g sea bass fillet, 150 g baked sweet potato, 150 g mixed vegetables (carrot, broccoli, snap peas), 1 tsp olive oil, lemon, salt, pepper
- **Prep time:** 30 min
- **Method:** Bake sweet potato at 200°C for 25 min. Sear sea bass in a hot pan with oil, 3–4 min each side. Stir-fry vegetables with a splash of water and seasoning.
- **~460 kcal | 40 g P | 42 g C | 10 g F**

#### Turkey Mince Zucchini Noodles (Fri)
- **Ingredients:** 200 g lean turkey mince, 2 medium zucchini (spiralised or cut in ribbons), 150 g tomato passata, 10 g parmesan, 1 tsp olive oil, garlic, oregano
- **Prep time:** 20 min
- **Method:** Brown turkey mince in a pan with garlic and oil. Add passata and oregano; simmer 10 min. Spiralise zucchini and briefly sauté 2 min. Plate noodles under sauce, top with parmesan.
- **~430 kcal | 40 g P | 25 g C | 16 g F**

#### Baked Chicken Thighs + Sweet Potato + Green Beans (Sat)
- **Ingredients:** 200 g skinless chicken thighs, 150 g sweet potato (cubed), 100 g green beans, 1 tsp olive oil, rosemary, garlic, salt
- **Prep time:** 35 min
- **Method:** Toss sweet potato cubes and green beans with oil + rosemary. Season chicken with garlic + salt. Arrange on baking tray. Roast at 200°C for 25–30 min until chicken is golden and juices run clear.
- **~480 kcal | 40 g P | 38 g C | 14 g F**
