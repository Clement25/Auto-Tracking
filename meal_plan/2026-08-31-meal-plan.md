## Nearby Restaurant Guide

### Near Home (36 Margaret Drive / Holland Village Area)

- **Margaret Drive Sin Kee Chicken Rice** — 40 Holland Drive, Holland Drive Hawker Centre (Stall 5, #01-39). *~5 min walk.* Michelin Bib Gourmand. Order **steamed chicken breast** (avoid roasted, skip skin), ask for half portion of rice, pair with their ginger dipping sauce. ~430–480 kcal, ~32–35g protein. High protein, low fat when ordered correctly. Open Tue–Sun 11am–8pm.

- **Habit (Holland Village)** — Holland Village area. Build-your-own bowl: choose a grain base (quinoa or brown rice), pick chicken breast as protein, load up on 3 vegetable sides. ~420–500 kcal, ~35g protein. Excellent for customization and portion control.

### Near Office (The Metropolis Tower 1, Buona Vista)

- **SaladStop! The Metropolis** — 9 North Buona Vista Dr #01-16, Singapore 138588. *In the building.* Build-your-own salads, wraps, and warm protein bowls. Best picks: **Warm Protein Bowl** with roasted chicken on wild rice (~550 kcal, 38g protein) or a **custom salad** with seared tuna on spinach base (~480 kcal, 40g protein). Always skip croutons; choose olive oil + lemon over creamy dressings.

- **The Soup Spoon Union** — Near The Metropolis. Hearty, wholesome soups. A large soup bowl runs ~250–350 kcal. Pair with a protein add-on for a lighter lunch day. Low in fat and refined carbs.

- **Dosirak** — Buona Vista area. Korean bibimbap with customizable proteins (chicken, beef, or tofu). ~430–500 kcal. Request brown rice if available; skip the sauce or use sparingly. ~30–35g protein.

## Self-Made Meal Recipes

### Breakfasts

**Greek Yogurt Parfait** (Mon)
- 200g low-fat Greek yogurt · 40g rolled oats · 100g mixed berries (fresh or frozen, thawed)
- Layer and serve cold. Prep: 3 min. ~370 kcal, 22g protein.

**Scrambled Egg Veggie Toast** (Tue)
- 3 eggs · large handful baby spinach · 1 slice wholegrain bread · cooking spray
- Wilt spinach in pan, add beaten eggs, scramble. Toast bread. Prep: 8 min. ~320 kcal, 22g protein.

**Protein Overnight Oats** (Wed)
- 50g rolled oats · 200ml low-fat milk · 1 scoop vanilla whey protein · 10g chia seeds · 80g blueberries
- Mix oats, milk, protein, chia in a jar. Refrigerate overnight. Top with berries. Prep: 5 min (night before). ~420 kcal, 32g protein.

**Egg & Cottage Cheese Omelette** (Thu)
- 2 eggs · 50g mushrooms (sliced) · 1 tomato (diced) · 100g low-fat cottage cheese · cooking spray
- Sauté mushrooms, add beaten eggs, fold omelette. Serve cottage cheese on side. Prep: 10 min. ~330 kcal, 28g protein.

**Greek Yogurt Granola Bowl** (Fri)
- 200g low-fat Greek yogurt · 30g low-sugar granola · 1 banana (sliced)
- Layer and serve. Prep: 2 min. ~380 kcal, 18g protein.

**Protein Pancakes** (Sat)
- 50g rolled oats (blend to flour) · 2 eggs · 1 ripe banana (mashed) · 1 scoop whey protein · pinch of cinnamon
- Mix all, cook on non-stick pan over medium heat (2 min per side). Makes ~4 small pancakes. Prep: 12 min. ~420 kcal, 30g protein.

**Avocado Scrambled Eggs** (Sun)
- 3 eggs · ¼ avocado (sliced) · 1 slice wholegrain toast · salt & pepper
- Scramble eggs gently over low heat. Serve on toast with avocado. Prep: 8 min. ~380 kcal, 22g protein.

### Lunches (Self-Made)

**Tuna Salad Wrap** (Tue)
- 90g canned tuna in spring water (drained) · 1 wholegrain wrap · mixed salad greens · ½ cucumber (sliced) · 1 tsp light mayo · lemon juice
- Mix tuna with mayo and lemon, fill wrap with greens and cucumber. Prep: 5 min. ~380 kcal, 32g protein.

**Chicken & Vegetable Rice Bowl** (Thu)
- 130g chicken breast (sliced) · 100g bok choy · ½ capsicum (sliced) · 80g mushrooms · ½ cup cooked brown rice · 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce · garlic & ginger
- Stir-fry veg in minimal oil, add chicken, season. Serve over rice. Prep: 15 min. ~450 kcal, 38g protein.

**Tuna & Vegetable Stir-fry** (Sat)
- 2 cans tuna in spring water · 1 capsicum · 100g mushrooms · 100g bok choy · ½ cup cooked brown rice · low-sodium soy sauce
- Stir-fry veg, add drained tuna at end to warm through. Serve over rice. Prep: 15 min. ~470 kcal, 35g protein.

**Chicken Caesar Salad** (Sun — no croutons)
- 130g grilled chicken breast (sliced) · 2 large romaine leaves (torn) · 1 tbsp light Caesar dressing · 1 tbsp grated parmesan
- Toss and serve. Prep: 10 min. ~400 kcal, 38g protein.

### Dinners (Self-Made)

**Grilled Chicken + Broccoli + Brown Rice** (Mon)
- 150g chicken breast · 200g broccoli florets · ½ cup cooked brown rice · garlic · olive oil (1 tsp) · lemon · salt & pepper
- Grill chicken 6–7 min per side. Steam broccoli 4 min. Serve with rice. Prep: 20 min. ~450 kcal, 40g protein.

**Baked Salmon + Asparagus + Quinoa** (Wed)
- 150g salmon fillet · 150g asparagus · 80g dry quinoa (cooked per pack) · lemon · dill · olive oil (1 tsp)
- Season salmon, bake at 200°C for 12–14 min. Roast asparagus alongside for 10 min. Serve with cooked quinoa. Prep: 20 min. ~520 kcal, 42g protein.

**Grilled Chicken + Sweet Potato + Green Beans** (Thu)
- 150g chicken breast · 1 medium sweet potato (200g, cubed) · 150g green beans · olive oil (1 tsp) · garlic powder · paprika
- Roast sweet potato at 200°C for 25 min. Grill chicken. Steam beans. Prep: 30 min. ~470 kcal, 38g protein.

**Turkey Meatballs + Zucchini Noodles** (Fri)
- 200g lean turkey mince · 2 zucchini (spiralized or peeled into ribbons) · 150ml tomato passata · garlic · dried oregano · 1 egg (binder for meatballs)
- Mix mince with egg, garlic, oregano. Form 4 balls, bake at 200°C for 18 min. Lightly sauté zucchini noodles 2 min. Heat passata. Plate up. Prep: 30 min. ~450 kcal, 42g protein.

**Baked White Fish + Roasted Veg + Brown Rice** (Sun)
- 150g white fish fillet (dory or cod) · 1 zucchini (sliced) · 100g cherry tomatoes · ½ capsicum · ½ cup cooked brown rice · olive oil (1 tsp) · lemon · herbs
- Arrange veg on tray with olive oil, roast 20 min at 200°C. Place fish on top last 12 min. Serve with rice. Prep: 30 min. ~450 kcal, 38g protein.
