## Self-Made Meal Recipes

### Breakfasts

#### Scrambled Eggs on Wholemeal Toast
**Ingredients:** 3 whole eggs, 1 slice wholemeal bread, 1 cup spinach, 6 cherry tomatoes, 1 tsp olive oil, salt & pepper
**Prep:** 10 min | **Cal: ~350** | **Protein: ~28 g**
1. Heat olive oil in pan over medium heat. Wilt spinach and halve cherry tomatoes (2 min).
2. Whisk eggs with salt & pepper; pour into pan; stir gently until just set.
3. Serve on toasted wholemeal bread with veg on the side.

#### Overnight Oats with Whey Protein
**Ingredients:** 50 g rolled oats, 200 ml unsweetened almond milk, 30 g vanilla whey protein, 80 g mixed berries, 15 g chia seeds
**Prep:** 5 min (night before) | **Cal: ~400** | **Protein: ~32 g**
1. Mix all ingredients in a jar; stir well.
2. Refrigerate overnight. In the morning, top with extra berries. No cooking required.

#### Greek Yogurt Parfait
**Ingredients:** 200 g low-fat Greek yogurt, 40 g rolled oats, 100 g strawberries (sliced), 1 tsp honey
**Prep:** 5 min | **Cal: ~380** | **Protein: ~22 g**
1. Layer Greek yogurt, oats, and sliced strawberries in a bowl.
2. Drizzle with honey. Eat immediately or refrigerate overnight for softer oats.

#### Egg White Veggie Omelette
**Ingredients:** 4 egg whites, 1 whole egg, 1 large handful spinach, 5 cherry tomatoes, 5 mushrooms (sliced), 1 tsp olive oil, salt & pepper
**Prep:** 12 min | **Cal: ~360** | **Protein: ~32 g**
1. Beat egg whites and whole egg together.
2. Heat olive oil over medium heat; sauté mushrooms (3 min), add spinach & halved tomatoes (1 min).
3. Pour egg mixture over veg; cook until set; fold in half. Serve with wholemeal toast.

#### Protein Smoothie
**Ingredients:** 30 g vanilla whey protein, 1 ripe banana, 30 g rolled oats, 200 ml unsweetened soy milk, 1 tbsp almond butter
**Prep:** 5 min | **Cal: ~420** | **Protein: ~33 g**
1. Add all ingredients to a blender; blend on high for 45 seconds.
2. Pour and serve immediately. Add ice cubes for a cooler smoothie.

#### Avocado Egg Toast
**Ingredients:** 2 slices wholemeal bread, 2 eggs, ½ ripe avocado, 6 cherry tomatoes, 1 tsp olive oil, salt & pepper
**Prep:** 15 min | **Cal: ~430** | **Protein: ~24 g**
1. Toast bread. Mash avocado with salt & pepper; spread onto toast.
2. Scramble eggs in olive oil; season. Pile onto toast; top with halved cherry tomatoes.

#### Tamagoyaki (Japanese Rolled Omelette)
**Ingredients:** 3 eggs, 1 tsp mirin, ½ tsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 tsp neutral oil
**Prep:** 20 min | **Cal:** ~200 (paired with edamame 100 g + toast = ~390) | **Protein: ~28 g**
1. Whisk eggs with mirin and soy sauce.
2. Heat an omelette or tamagoyaki pan over medium-low heat; oil lightly.
3. Pour ⅓ of egg mixture; when partially set, roll to one side. Push roll back; pour another ⅓ and roll again. Repeat once more.
4. Shape in a bamboo mat (optional); slice. Serve with steamed edamame and toast.

### Lunches & Dinners

#### Pan-Seared Salmon with Broccoli & Brown Rice
**Ingredients:** 150 g salmon fillet, 200 g broccoli florets, 80 g cooked brown rice, 1 lemon, 1 tsp olive oil, salt & pepper
**Prep:** 25 min | **Cal: ~520** | **Protein: ~42 g**
1. Cook brown rice in rice cooker. Steam broccoli until tender-crisp (5–6 min).
2. Pat salmon dry; season with salt, pepper, lemon zest.
3. Heat olive oil in pan over medium-high; place salmon skin-side down; cook 4 min. Flip; cook 2–3 min more until just cooked through. Squeeze lemon over top.
4. Plate rice, broccoli, and salmon.

#### Tuna Wholemeal Wrap
**Ingredients:** 1 large wholemeal tortilla, 1 can tuna (in spring water, drained), 2 large lettuce leaves, 1 medium tomato (sliced), ½ cucumber (sliced), 1 tbsp low-fat Greek yogurt, pepper
**Prep:** 10 min | **Cal: ~380** | **Protein: ~38 g**
1. Drain tuna thoroughly; mix with Greek yogurt and pepper.
2. Layer lettuce, tuna mixture, tomato, and cucumber onto the wrap.
3. Roll tightly; slice in half. Wrap in foil for a bento.

#### Chicken Bento (Brown Rice + Steamed Chicken + Bok Choy)
**Ingredients:** 150 g chicken breast, 80 g cooked brown rice, 200 g bok choy, 1 clove garlic (minced), 1 tsp sesame oil, low-sodium soy sauce
**Prep:** 20 min | **Cal: ~450** | **Protein: ~42 g**
1. Steam chicken breast over boiling water for 15–18 min until cooked through. Slice.
2. Stir-fry bok choy with garlic in sesame oil (2 min). Season with a splash of soy sauce.
3. Pack rice, chicken, and bok choy into a bento box. Refrigerate if eating later.

#### Lemon Herb Baked Chicken with Quinoa & Roasted Vegetables
**Ingredients:** 150 g chicken breast, 70 g quinoa (dry), 1 zucchini (sliced), 1 bell pepper (sliced), 1 tbsp olive oil, juice of 1 lemon, 1 garlic clove (minced), dried herbs (thyme, rosemary, oregano), salt & pepper
**Prep:** 30 min | **Cal: ~510** | **Protein: ~45 g**
1. Marinate chicken in lemon juice, garlic, herbs, salt & pepper for 20 min.
2. Pre-heat oven to 180°C. Toss zucchini & pepper in half the olive oil; spread on baking tray.
3. Place chicken on same tray (or separate). Bake 22–25 min until chicken is cooked through (internal temp 74°C).
4. Cook quinoa in water (1:2 ratio) for 15 min; fluff with a fork.
5. Serve chicken over quinoa with roasted vegetables.

#### Tuna Niçoise Salad
**Ingredients:** 1 can tuna (spring water, drained), 2 hard-boiled eggs (halved), 150 g green beans (blanched), 100 g mixed salad greens, 8 Kalamata olives, dressing: 1 tbsp olive oil + juice of ½ lemon + 1 tsp Dijon mustard + pepper
**Prep:** 15 min | **Cal: ~450** | **Protein: ~48 g**
1. Blanch green beans in boiling water for 3 min; drain and cool.
2. Hard-boil eggs (8 min); cool and peel.
3. Arrange greens, beans, olives, tuna, and egg halves on a plate.
4. Whisk dressing ingredients; drizzle over. Season to taste.

#### Pan-Seared White Fish with Ginger-Soy Glaze
**Ingredients:** 150 g cod or white fish fillet, 200 g bok choy, 100 g beansprouts, 80 g cooked brown rice, 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 tsp freshly grated ginger, 1 tsp honey, ½ tsp rice vinegar
**Prep:** 25 min | **Cal: ~500** | **Protein: ~38 g**
1. Mix soy sauce, grated ginger, honey, and rice vinegar for the glaze. Set aside.
2. Pat fish dry; heat sesame oil in pan over medium-high; sear fish skin-side down for 3–4 min; flip and cook 2 min more. Spoon glaze over in the last minute.
3. Steam bok choy and beansprouts (3–4 min).
4. Serve fish over rice with steamed greens and drizzle remaining glaze.

#### Grilled Chicken Wrap
**Ingredients:** 150 g chicken breast, 1 large wholemeal tortilla, 4 leaves romaine lettuce, 1 medium tomato (sliced), 2 tbsp light tzatziki (plain yogurt + cucumber + garlic + dill)
**Prep:** 20 min | **Cal: ~460** | **Protein: ~44 g**
1. Grill or pan-sear chicken breast with salt, pepper, garlic powder (6–7 min per side). Slice.
2. Spread tzatziki on tortilla; layer lettuce, tomato, and sliced chicken.
3. Roll tightly and serve.

## Fat-Loss Tips for the Week

1. **Eat protein first** at each meal to trigger satiety hormones and protect muscle mass.
2. **Drink 2–2.5 L water daily** — especially before meals to reduce appetite.
3. **Time your meals** — aim for 4–5 hour gaps between meals to allow insulin to drop.
4. **Walk 8,000–10,000 steps daily** — your home-to-office commute is a great opportunity.
5. **Batch cook on Sunday** — prepare brown rice, grilled chicken, and boiled eggs for the week to reduce weekday prep time.
6. **Hawker customisations** — always request steamed over fried, clear broth over gravy, less rice, and extra vegetables.
