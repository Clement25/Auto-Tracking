## Nearby Restaurant Guide

### Near Home (36 Margaret Drive, Singapore)

- **Margaret Drive Hawker Centre** (38A Margaret Drive, ~2 min walk)
  - **Hai Chun Fish Soup (#01-34):** Sliced fish soup in clear broth (~$5–6). ~350 kcal, ~30 g protein. Clear-broth fish soup is one of Singapore's most macro-friendly hawker dishes — high protein, low fat. Request no fried shallot oil drizzle. Add extra tofu (+$0.50) for more protein.
  - **Hakka Thunder Tea Rice / Lei Cha (#02-34):** Plant-forward bowl of stir-fried greens (cabbage, spinach, long beans), tofu, anchovies, peanuts, and fragrant herbal broth (~$4.50–5.50). ~380–420 kcal, ~18 g protein. Choose brown rice. Skip extra peanuts to keep fat in check. A rare hawker dish that is genuinely nutrient-dense. Open Wed–Sun ~10 am to sell-out (~3 pm).
  - **Economic Rice stall (mixed-vegetable rice):** Choose steamed fish, steamed/braised tofu, and a leafy vegetable. Request half-rice or no rice to cut carbs. ~380–430 kcal, ~28–32 g protein.

### Near Office (The Metropolis Tower 1, Buona Vista)

- **SaladStop! (The Metropolis, #01-09):** Build-your-own salad or grain bowl. ~420–520 kcal per bowl. Pick a protein-rich base (grilled chicken, tuna, or boiled egg) and choose light vinaigrette. Avoid creamy dressings and cheese add-ons to keep calories in range. Weekday hours typically 10 am – 8 pm.
- **The Soup Spoon Union (The Metropolis):** Wide-ranging soup menu. The Moroccan Chicken Soup and Chicken & Vegetable soups are the best fat-loss choices (~380–460 kcal with a roll). High in vegetables, moderate protein; skip the bread roll on higher-calorie days.
- **Grains & Co (Timbre+, 73A Ayer Rajah Crescent, ~10 min by bus/walk from Metropolis):** Grain bowls with customizable proteins. The Bulgogi Bowl and grain-based options provide balanced macros (~450–510 kcal). Good variety for days you want a change from salads.

## Self-Made Meal Recipes

### 1. Greek Yogurt Parfait
**Serves 1 | ~380 kcal | 20 g protein | Prep: 3 min**
- 200 g plain full-fat Greek yogurt
- 30 g low-sugar granola
- 100 g mixed frozen berries (thaw overnight in fridge)
- 1 tsp honey (optional)
Layer yogurt, then granola, then berries. Add honey if needed for sweetness.

### 2. 3-Egg Spinach & Mushroom Omelette
**Serves 1 | ~310 kcal | 24 g protein | Prep: 8 min**
- 3 large eggs, whisked
- Large handful baby spinach
- 6 button mushrooms, sliced
- 1 tsp olive oil, salt, pepper
Heat oil in non-stick pan. Sauté mushrooms 2 min. Add spinach until wilted. Pour in eggs, fold when set.

### 3. Baked Chicken Bento
**Serves 1 | ~490 kcal | 43 g protein | Prep: 15 min active, 20 min oven**
- 150 g chicken breast
- Marinade: 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce + 1 tsp olive oil + garlic powder + lemon juice
- ¾ cup cooked brown rice (cook in bulk Sunday)
- Cucumber sticks + cherry tomatoes
Bake chicken at 200 °C for 18–20 min. Slice. Pack in bento with rice and veg.

### 4. Overnight Oats with Whey Protein
**Serves 1 | ~420 kcal | 35 g protein | Prep: 5 min (night before)**
- 60 g rolled oats
- 1 scoop whey protein powder
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- 1 banana, sliced (or berries)
- 200 ml unsweetened almond milk
Mix all in a jar. Cover and refrigerate overnight. Eat cold in the morning.

### 5. Protein Smoothie
**Serves 1 | ~360 kcal | 32 g protein | Prep: 3 min**
- 1 scoop whey protein powder
- 1 small banana
- Large handful baby spinach
- 30 g rolled oats
- 250 ml unsweetened almond milk
Blend until smooth. Consume immediately.

### 6. Pan-Seared Cod / Salmon
**Serves 1 | ~490–510 kcal | 40–42 g protein | Prep: 15 min**
- 180 g cod or salmon fillet
- ¾ cup cooked brown rice
- 200 g bok choy or broccoli
- 1 tsp olive oil, garlic, lemon, salt
Season fish. Sear in hot oiled pan 3–4 min per side. Steam veg separately. Serve over rice.

### 7. Self-Made Tuna Bowl
**Serves 1 | ~430 kcal | 42 g protein | Prep: 8 min**
- 150 g canned tuna in spring water, drained
- 80 g cooked quinoa
- Mixed salad greens
- Cherry tomatoes + cucumber, chopped
- 1 tbsp olive oil + juice of ½ lemon + black pepper
Toss quinoa, greens, tomatoes, cucumber. Top with tuna. Dress with oil and lemon.

### 8. Roasted Chicken Thigh with Sweet Potato
**Serves 1 | ~460 kcal | 37 g protein | Prep: 10 min active, 30 min oven**
- 150 g chicken thigh, skin removed
- 100 g sweet potato, cubed
- 1 tsp olive oil, rosemary, salt, pepper
- Large mixed green salad + lemon-olive oil dressing
Toss sweet potato in oil and rosemary; roast at 200 °C 25 min. Season chicken and roast same tray last 20 min.

### 9. Steamed Barramundi with Brown Rice
**Serves 1 | ~460 kcal | 42 g protein | Prep: 10 min active, 15 min steam**
- 180 g barramundi fillet
- ¾ cup cooked brown rice
- 200 g kailan or Chinese broccoli
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce + 1 tsp sesame oil (sparingly)
Steam fish over boiling water 12–14 min. Blanch kailan. Drizzle light oyster sauce.

### 10. Baked Salmon with Asparagus
**Serves 1 | ~510 kcal | 42 g protein | Prep: 10 min active, 20 min oven**
- 180 g salmon fillet
- 200 g asparagus spears
- ½ cup cooked brown rice
- 1 lemon (sliced), fresh dill, garlic, salt, olive oil spray
Place salmon and asparagus on baking tray. Season. Bake at 200 °C, 18–20 min.
