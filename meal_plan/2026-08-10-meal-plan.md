## Self-Made Meal Recipes

### Breakfasts

**A — 3-Egg Scramble with Spinach & Toast** *(Mon)* | 350 kcal · 24 g P | 10 min
- 3 large eggs, scramble in 1 tsp olive oil over medium heat
- Add 40 g baby spinach and 6 halved cherry tomatoes, stir until wilted
- Season with black pepper; serve with 1 slice toasted whole-grain bread

**B — Overnight Oats** *(Tue)* | 380 kcal · 28 g P | 5 min prep (night before)
- 50 g rolled oats + 1 scoop vanilla whey protein + 10 g chia seeds in a jar
- Pour over 200 ml unsweetened almond milk; stir well; refrigerate overnight
- Top with ½ sliced banana in the morning

**C — Egg-White Omelette with Feta** *(Wed)* | 285 kcal · 27 g P | 10 min
- Whisk 4 egg whites + 1 whole egg; pour into non-stick pan on medium heat
- Add 50 g sliced mushrooms and 30 g baby spinach; cook until set
- Crumble 30 g low-fat feta on top; fold and serve

**D — Greek Yogurt Parfait** *(Thu)* | 320 kcal · 20 g P | 5 min
- 200 g low-fat Greek yogurt in a bowl or jar
- Layer with 30 g low-sugar granola, 100 g mixed berries, 10 g chia seeds

**E — Protein Smoothie Bowl** *(Fri)* | 380 kcal · 28 g P | 10 min
- Blend: 1 scoop protein powder + 100 g frozen mixed berries + ½ banana + 150 ml unsweetened almond milk
- Pour into bowl (thick); top with 10 g pumpkin seeds and sliced kiwi

**F — Avocado Toast with Poached Eggs** *(Sat)* | 420 kcal · 22 g P | 15 min
- Toast 2 slices whole-grain bread
- Mash ½ ripe avocado with lemon juice and black pepper; spread on toast
- Poach 2 eggs (3–4 min in simmering water); place on top; garnish with tomato slices

**G — Cottage Cheese Bowl** *(Sun)* | 280 kcal · 24 g P | 5 min
- 200 g low-fat cottage cheese in a bowl
- Top with 1 sliced peach (or canned in juice, drained), 10 g mixed seeds, 1 tsp raw honey

### Dinners

**1 — Grilled Salmon with Brown Rice & Roasted Vegetables** *(Tue)* | 520 kcal · 40 g P | 25 min
- 150 g salmon fillet — season with 1 tsp olive oil, garlic powder, black pepper; grill/pan-sear 4–5 min per side
- Roast 150 g broccoli + 100 g cauliflower at 200°C for 20 min with a drizzle of olive oil
- Serve with ½ cup cooked brown rice

**2 — Baked Chicken Thighs with Sweet Potato & Broccoli** *(Thu)* | 520 kcal · 40 g P | 35 min
- 200 g skinless chicken thighs — marinate in soy sauce, garlic, ginger, 1 tsp sesame oil; bake at 200°C for 25–30 min
- Cube 150 g sweet potato; roast alongside for 25 min
- Steam 100 g broccoli florets; serve together

**3 — Baked Barramundi with Quinoa & Asparagus** *(Fri)* | 480 kcal · 44 g P | 25 min
- 180 g barramundi fillet — season with lemon zest, herbs, black pepper; bake at 190°C for 15 min
- Cook ½ cup quinoa (rinsed) in 1 cup water, 12 min; fluff with fork
- Steam 100 g asparagus spears for 5 min; serve on a bed of baby spinach

**4 — Poached Chicken with Zucchini Noodles** *(Sun lunch)* | 380 kcal · 42 g P | 20 min
- 180 g chicken breast — simmer in lightly salted water/broth with 1 bay leaf for 15 min; cool and slice
- Spiralise or use a peeler to make noodles from 1 medium zucchini + 1 large cucumber
- Dressing: 1 tbsp tahini + juice of ½ lemon + 1 tsp soy sauce + 1 tsp water; drizzle and toss

**5 — Teriyaki Chicken Salad** *(Sat lunch)* | 400 kcal · 38 g P | 15 min
- 150 g chicken breast — coat in 1 tbsp low-sodium teriyaki sauce; pan-grill 5–6 min per side; slice
- Toss mixed greens, shredded carrots, ½ cup edamame, sliced cucumber
- Dressing: 1 tsp sesame oil + 1 tbsp rice vinegar + ½ tsp honey + sesame seeds

## Key Ordering Customisations (Hawker / Restaurant)

1. **Chicken rice** — request steamed over roasted for less fat; ask for ½ rice portion; add extra broth soup
2. **Fish soup** — order with extra fish or tofu; bee hoon preferred over rice to reduce refined carbs; skip chilli
3. **Economy rice** — pick steamed/braised/poached proteins; 2 vegetable dishes over gravy dishes; ½ rice portion
4. **SaladStop / grain bowls** — double protein portion; lemon-tahini or balsamic dressing (not creamy); add boiled egg
5. **Wraps / sandwiches** — whole-wheat base; turkey/tuna/chicken fillings; avocado instead of mayo
