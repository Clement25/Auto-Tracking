## Self-Made Meal Recipes

### Breakfasts

#### 1. Greek Yogurt Parfait *(Monday)*
**Ingredients:** 200g non-fat Greek yogurt, 80g mixed berries, 40g low-sugar granola, 1 tsp honey
**Prep:** 5 min. Layer yogurt → berries → granola in a bowl or jar. Drizzle honey on top.

#### 2. Egg White Omelette *(Tuesday)*
**Ingredients:** 4 large egg whites, 1 whole egg, 100g baby spinach, 100g sliced mushrooms, olive oil spray, salt & pepper, 1 slice whole grain toast
**Prep:** 15 min. Spray a non-stick pan, sauté mushrooms 3 min. Add spinach until wilted. Pour beaten egg mixture over. Cook until set, fold in half. Serve with toast.

#### 3. Overnight Oats *(Wednesday — prep Tuesday night)*
**Ingredients:** 50g rolled oats, 200ml low-fat milk, 15g chia seeds, 80g strawberries (sliced)
**Prep:** 5 min (night before). Combine oats, milk, chia seeds in a jar or bowl. Stir, seal, refrigerate overnight. Top with strawberries in the morning.

#### 4. Scrambled Eggs on Toast *(Thursday)*
**Ingredients:** 3 large eggs, 1 slice whole grain toast, ½ medium avocado, 1 medium tomato, salt & pepper
**Prep:** 10 min. Beat eggs. Cook on low heat, stirring gently, until just set. Toast bread. Mash avocado. Layer avocado on toast, top with eggs and sliced tomato.

#### 5. Protein Smoothie *(Friday)*
**Ingredients:** 200ml low-fat milk, 200g low-fat Greek yogurt, 1 banana, 80g frozen berries, 1 tbsp peanut butter
**Prep:** 5 min. Blend all ingredients until smooth. Pour and go.

#### 6. Avocado Toast + Poached Eggs *(Saturday)*
**Ingredients:** 2 slices whole grain bread, ½ avocado, 2 large eggs, 1 medium tomato, lemon juice, salt & pepper
**Prep:** 15 min. Bring water to a gentle simmer; add a splash of vinegar. Crack each egg into a cup, slide into water. Poach 3 min. Toast bread. Mash avocado with lemon juice. Layer tomato, avocado, eggs. Season.

#### 7. Weekend Egg & Salmon Scramble *(Sunday)*
**Ingredients:** 2 whole eggs, 2 egg whites, 100g smoked salmon (sliced), 50g baby spinach, 2 slices whole grain toast, olive oil spray
**Prep:** 15 min. Spray pan, sauté spinach briefly. Beat eggs/whites together, pour in. Cook on medium-low, fold gently. Add smoked salmon in last 30 seconds to warm through. Serve on toast.

---

### Dinners

#### 8. Grilled Chicken + Brown Rice + Roasted Vegetables *(Monday)*
**Ingredients:** 150g chicken breast, 75g dry brown rice (yields ~150g cooked), 200g mixed veg (broccoli florets, zucchini slices, red bell pepper strips), 1 tsp olive oil, 2 garlic cloves (minced), juice of ½ lemon, dried Italian herbs, salt & pepper
**Prep:** 25 min. Marinate chicken 10 min in garlic, lemon, herbs. Grill or pan-sear on medium-high 6–7 min/side until cooked through. Cook rice per packet. Toss veg in oil, spread on baking tray, roast 200°C / 20 min.

#### 9. Baked Salmon + Quinoa + Steamed Broccoli *(Wednesday)*
**Ingredients:** 150g salmon fillet, 65g dry quinoa (yields ~150g cooked), 200g broccoli florets, 1 tsp olive oil, juice of ½ lemon, fresh or dried dill, salt & pepper
**Prep:** 25 min. Preheat oven to 200°C. Season salmon with oil, lemon, dill, salt. Bake on lined tray 15 min. Cook quinoa per packet (rinse first). Steam broccoli 5 min until tender-crisp.

#### 10. Baked Chicken Patties + Sweet Potato + Salad *(Thursday)*
**Ingredients:** 150g lean ground chicken, 1 egg (binder), ½ tsp garlic powder, dried herbs (thyme/oregano), salt & pepper. 1 medium sweet potato (~200g). 150g mixed salad (lettuce, cucumber, cherry tomatoes). 1 tsp olive oil + lemon dressing.
**Prep:** 30 min. Mix chicken with egg, garlic powder, herbs. Form 3 patties. Place on lined tray, bake at 200°C for 22–25 min. Pierce sweet potato, microwave 5 min or bake 45 min. Toss salad with oil and lemon.

#### 11. Tuna + Sweet Potato + Green Beans *(Friday)*
**Ingredients:** 150g canned tuna in water (drained), 1 medium sweet potato, 150g green beans, 1 tsp olive oil, juice of ½ lemon, salt & pepper
**Prep:** 15 min. Microwave sweet potato 5 min (pierce first). Steam green beans 4–5 min. Plate tuna, drizzle with olive oil and lemon. Season all.

#### 12. Japanese Chicken Rice Bowl *(Saturday Lunch)*
**Ingredients:** 150g chicken breast, 75g dry brown rice (yields ~150g cooked), 100g shelled edamame (frozen, thawed), 100g cucumber (julienned), 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame seeds
**Prep:** 30 min. Grill or pan-sear chicken, slice thinly. Cook rice. Arrange chicken, edamame, cucumber over rice. Drizzle with soy sauce and sesame oil. Top with sesame seeds.

#### 13. Tuna Quinoa Salad Bowl *(Sunday Lunch)*
**Ingredients:** 150g canned tuna in water (drained), 65g dry quinoa (yields ~150g cooked), 100g cherry tomatoes (halved), 50g cucumber (diced), 30g rocket/arugula, 1 tsp olive oil, juice of ½ lemon, salt & pepper
**Prep:** 20 min. Cook quinoa per packet. Drain tuna. Combine all in a bowl, dress with oil and lemon. Toss gently.

#### 14. Chicken & Vegetable Stir-Fry *(Sunday Dinner)*
**Ingredients:** 180g chicken breast (thinly sliced), 300g mixed veg — 100g bok choy, 50g julienned carrot, 50g snap peas, 100g red bell pepper (sliced), 2 garlic cloves (minced), 1 tsp fresh ginger (grated), 1 tsp sesame oil, 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 tbsp oyster sauce, 75g dry brown rice (yields ~150g cooked)
**Prep:** 25 min. Cook rice. Heat wok/pan on high, add sesame oil. Stir-fry chicken until golden (~4 min). Push aside, add garlic and ginger (30 sec). Add vegetables, toss 3 min. Pour in soy sauce and oyster sauce. Combine everything. Serve over rice.
