## Nearby Restaurant Guide

### Near Home (36 Margaret Drive, Queenstown)

**Margaret Drive Hawker Centre** — 38A Margaret Drive #02-34 (~2-min walk)
- **Hakka Thunder Tea Rice (Lei Cha)** — ~400–420 kcal | Herb-tea broth poured over rice with tofu, peanuts, long beans, preserved vegetables. High fibre, moderate protein, low saturated fat. Skip extra peanuts to reduce calories. Order without pork lard.
- **Japanese/Korean Stall — Saba Fish Set** — ~480–500 kcal | Grilled mackerel (rich in omega-3), brown rice, kimchi or pickled vegetable side. High protein (~35 g), heart-healthy fats. Request steamed over grilled if oily.

### Near Office (The Metropolis Tower 1, Buona Vista)

**Green Monster Salads** — The Metropolis Tower 1 (ground level) (~0 min walk)
- **Grilled Chicken Bowl** — ~460–500 kcal | Mixed greens, grilled chicken breast, quinoa, edamame, cucumber, house vinaigrette. ~35–38 g protein. Ask for dressing on the side to control calories.

**The Soup Spoon Union** — The Metropolis (~2-min walk)
- **Chicken Mushroom Soup (regular)** — ~300–350 kcal per bowl | Low-fat, high-satiety, ~25 g protein. Pair with 1 wholegrain roll for complete meal (~430 kcal total). Skip cream-based soups.

**Simply Sandwich** — The Metropolis (~1-min walk)
- **Tuna on Whole Wheat** — ~420–450 kcal | Canned tuna, lettuce, tomato, cucumber, light mayo on whole-wheat bread. ~30 g protein. Ask for less mayo or substitute with mustard.

**Greendot** — Star Vista, 1 Vista Exchange Green (~8-min walk from The Metropolis)
- **Brown Rice with Braised Tofu & Vegetables** — ~440–460 kcal | Vegetarian Asian comfort food, plant protein, low saturated fat. ~20–25 g protein. Add a side of edamame for more protein.

## Self-Made Meal Recipes

### Scrambled Eggs & Toast (Mon / Sun Breakfast)
- **Ingredients:** 3 eggs (Mon) or 2 eggs + 50 g smoked salmon (Sun), 2 slices whole-wheat bread, 5–6 cherry tomatoes, salt, pepper, 1 tsp olive oil
- **Prep:** 8 min | Whisk eggs, cook in non-stick pan on low heat, season lightly.
- **Calories:** ~380 kcal (plain) / ~430 kcal (with smoked salmon)

### Overnight Oats (Tue Breakfast)
- **Ingredients:** 50 g rolled oats, 150 ml low-fat milk, ½ banana, 1 tbsp chia seeds, pinch cinnamon
- **Prep:** 5 min prep night before | Combine all in jar, refrigerate overnight.
- **Calories:** ~380 kcal

### Greek Yogurt Parfait (Wed Breakfast)
- **Ingredients:** 200 g plain low-fat Greek yogurt, 100 g mixed berries (fresh or frozen), 30 g low-sugar granola
- **Prep:** 3 min | Layer in bowl or jar.
- **Calories:** ~380 kcal

### Egg-White Omelette (Thu Breakfast)
- **Ingredients:** 4 egg whites + 1 whole egg, 40 g baby spinach, 1 small tomato (diced), salt, pepper, 1 tsp olive oil, 1 slice whole-wheat toast
- **Prep:** 10 min | Cook egg mixture in non-stick pan, fold over vegetables.
- **Calories:** ~340 kcal

### Avocado Toast & Poached Eggs (Fri Breakfast)
- **Ingredients:** 2 slices whole-wheat bread, ½ ripe avocado, 2 eggs, salt, pepper, lemon juice
- **Prep:** 12 min | Toast bread, mash avocado with lemon/salt, poach eggs in simmering water.
- **Calories:** ~400 kcal

### Oatmeal with Protein Powder (Sat Breakfast)
- **Ingredients:** 60 g rolled oats, 200 ml unsweetened almond milk, 1 scoop vanilla whey protein (~25 g protein), ½ banana sliced
- **Prep:** 5 min | Cook oats with almond milk, stir in protein powder off heat.
- **Calories:** ~420 kcal

### Chicken Quinoa Salad (Wed Lunch / Sat Lunch)
- **Ingredients:** 120 g chicken breast, 60 g dry quinoa, 50 g cucumber, 40 g baby spinach, 5 cherry tomatoes, 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, lemon juice, salt, pepper
- **Prep:** 20 min | Poach chicken, cook quinoa, cool both, toss with vegetables and dressing. Pack in container.
- **Calories:** ~430–450 kcal

### Steamed Chicken + Bok Choy + Brown Rice (Mon Dinner)
- **Ingredients:** 150 g chicken breast, 150 g bok choy, 1 cup cooked brown rice (~180 g), 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tbsp light soy sauce, garlic, ginger
- **Prep:** 25 min | Steam chicken 15–18 min; blanch bok choy; serve over rice, drizzle with soy-sesame dressing.
- **Calories:** ~480 kcal

### Baked Salmon + Broccoli + Sweet Potato (Wed Dinner)
- **Ingredients:** 150 g salmon fillet, 200 g broccoli, 100 g sweet potato, 1 tsp olive oil, lemon, garlic, salt, pepper
- **Prep:** 30 min | Bake salmon at 200°C for 15 min; steam broccoli; bake sweet potato cubes alongside.
- **Calories:** ~510 kcal

### Stir-Fried Chicken + Mixed Vegetables + Brown Rice (Thu Dinner)
- **Ingredients:** 150 g chicken breast (sliced), 1 cup mixed vegetables (zucchini, carrot, capsicum), 1 cup cooked brown rice, 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tbsp oyster sauce, garlic
- **Prep:** 20 min | Stir-fry chicken first, add vegetables, season, serve over rice.
- **Calories:** ~470 kcal

### Baked Chicken Thigh + Roasted Vegetables (Fri Dinner)
- **Ingredients:** 200 g skinless chicken thigh, 1 zucchini, 1 capsicum, ½ onion, 1 tsp olive oil, dried oregano, salt, pepper
- **Prep:** 35 min | Marinate chicken briefly, bake at 200°C for 25–28 min; toss vegetables in olive oil and roast alongside.
- **Calories:** ~430 kcal

### Stir-Fried Tofu + Bok Choy + Brown Rice (Sun Dinner)
- **Ingredients:** 200 g firm tofu (press and cube), 150 g bok choy, 50 g shiitake mushrooms, 1 cup cooked brown rice, ½ tsp sesame oil, 1 tbsp light soy sauce, garlic
- **Prep:** 20 min | Pan-fry tofu until golden; stir-fry vegetables with garlic; combine and season.
- **Calories:** ~450 kcal

### Tuna Salad (Sun Lunch)
- **Ingredients:** 150 g canned tuna in spring water (drained), 60 g mixed lettuce leaves, 50 g cucumber, 8 cherry tomatoes, 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, lemon juice, salt, pepper
- **Prep:** 5 min | Drain tuna, toss everything together.
- **Calories:** ~380 kcal
