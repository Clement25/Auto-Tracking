## Nearby Restaurant Guide

### Near Home — Margaret Drive, Queenstown

**Margaret Drive Hawker Centre** is the primary dining hub in the neighbourhood, a short walk from home.

| Restaurant | Recommended Dish | Est. Calories | Why It Fits |
|------------|-----------------|---------------|-------------|
| **Tong Kee Chicken Rice** (#01-36) | Steamed chicken rice — breast cut, no skin, medium portion + soup | ~440 kcal | High-protein (35g), relatively low fat with breast cut; clear soup adds satiety |
| **Sin Kee Cantonese Soya Sauce Chicken** (#01-30) | Soya sauce chicken rice (breast/drumstick without skin) | ~500 kcal | Leaner preparation than fried chicken; request skinless and avoid extra sauce |
| **Hakka Thunder Tea Rice** (#02-34) | Thunder Tea Rice (ask for brown rice upgrade) with tofu & vegetable toppings | ~420 kcal | High-fibre vegetarian bowl; herbal broth adds antioxidants; low saturated fat |

**Tips for hawker dining:**
- Always request breast cut or skinless portions for poultry
- Skip fried sides (ngoh hiang, luncheon meat)
- Ask for less rice and extra vegetables when available
- Choose clear soups over creamy ones

## Self-Made Meal Recipes

### Overnight Oats (Breakfast)
**Prep time:** 5 min (night before) | **Serves:** 1 | ~380 kcal · 32g P

- 50g rolled oats
- 1 scoop vanilla whey protein powder (~25g)
- 150ml unsweetened almond milk
- 1 banana, sliced
- Pinch of cinnamon

Combine oats, protein, and almond milk in a jar. Stir well. Refrigerate overnight. Top with banana in the morning.

---

### Pan-Seared Salmon + Broccoli + Brown Rice (Dinner)
**Prep time:** 20 min | **Serves:** 1 | ~570 kcal · 42g P

- 200g salmon fillet
- 150g broccoli florets
- 150g cooked brown rice (from 60g dry)
- 1 tsp olive oil, salt, pepper, lemon juice, garlic powder

Heat pan over medium-high with oil. Season salmon. Sear 4 min per side. Steam broccoli 5 min. Serve with rice.

---

### Chicken Stir-Fry + Brown Rice (Dinner)
**Prep time:** 20 min | **Serves:** 1 | ~510 kcal · 40g P

- 150g chicken breast, sliced thin
- 1 cup bok choy
- ½ red bell pepper, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil
- 150g cooked brown rice

Stir-fry chicken in sesame oil 5 min. Add garlic, then vegetables 3 min. Add soy sauce. Serve on rice.

---

### Baked Chicken Thigh + Roasted Vegetables (Dinner)
**Prep time:** 10 min active + 30 min bake | **Serves:** 1 | ~530 kcal · 42g P

- 200g skinless chicken thigh
- 1 medium zucchini, diced
- 100g sweet potato, cubed
- 1 tsp olive oil, mixed herbs, salt, pepper

Preheat oven to 200°C. Toss sweet potato with oil and herbs, roast 15 min. Add zucchini and seasoned chicken, roast another 20 min.

---

### Tuna Quinoa Salad (Lunch — packed)
**Prep time:** 15 min | **Serves:** 1 | ~420 kcal · 36g P

- 140g canned tuna in water (drained)
- 80g cooked quinoa (from 40g dry)
- ½ cucumber, diced
- Handful mixed greens
- Juice of ½ lemon, 1 tsp olive oil, salt, pepper

Cook quinoa ahead and chill. Combine all ingredients, dress with lemon and oil.

---

### Egg White Omelette + Toast (Breakfast)
**Prep time:** 10 min | **Serves:** 1 | ~380 kcal · 28g P

- 4 egg whites (or 120ml carton egg whites)
- Handful baby spinach
- 4 button mushrooms, sliced
- 1 tomato, diced
- 2 slices whole grain bread, toasted
- Salt, pepper, non-stick spray

Sauté vegetables 2 min. Add egg whites, cook until set, fold. Serve with toast.
