## Self-Made Meal Recipes

### 1. Scrambled Eggs with Smoked Salmon
**Ingredients:** 3 eggs, 50 g smoked salmon, 1 slice rye bread, salt, pepper, 1 tsp olive oil  
**Prep time:** 8 min  
**Method:** Whisk eggs, cook on low heat with olive oil stirring gently. Serve on toast topped with smoked salmon.

### 2. Overnight Oats with Protein Powder
**Ingredients:** 50 g rolled oats, 200 ml unsweetened almond milk, 1 scoop vanilla protein powder, ½ banana (sliced), 1 tsp chia seeds  
**Prep time:** 5 min (night before) + 0 min morning  
**Method:** Combine all ingredients in a jar, stir well, refrigerate overnight. Eat cold.

### 3. Egg-White Omelette with Vegetables
**Ingredients:** 4 egg whites, 1 whole egg, 30 g baby spinach, 4 mushrooms (sliced), 5 cherry tomatoes (halved), salt, pepper, cooking spray  
**Prep time:** 10 min  
**Method:** Whisk eggs, pour into non-stick pan sprayed with oil, add vegetables, fold and serve.

### 4. Pan-Seared Chicken Breast + Brown Rice + Broccoli
**Ingredients:** 150 g chicken breast, ½ cup dry brown rice (cook as directed), 200 g broccoli florets, garlic, lemon juice, olive oil, salt, pepper  
**Prep time:** 25 min  
**Method:** Season and sear chicken 6 min/side. Steam broccoli 5 min. Serve with cooked rice, squeeze lemon over everything.

### 5. Baked Salmon + Bok Choy + Sweet Potato
**Ingredients:** 150 g salmon fillet, 200 g bok choy, 100 g sweet potato, soy sauce (low-sodium, 1 tsp), garlic, ginger, sesame oil (½ tsp)  
**Prep time:** 30 min  
**Method:** Season salmon, bake at 200°C for 15 min. Boil sweet potato cubes 12 min. Stir-fry bok choy with garlic 3 min. Plate and drizzle with sesame oil.

### 6. Avocado Toast with Poached Eggs
**Ingredients:** 1 slice whole-grain bread, ¼ avocado, 2 eggs, 5 cherry tomatoes, lemon juice, salt, pepper  
**Prep time:** 10 min  
**Method:** Toast bread. Mash avocado with lemon, salt, pepper. Poach eggs 3 min. Assemble and top with cherry tomatoes.

### 7. Protein Smoothie Bowl
**Ingredients:** 1 scoop protein powder (vanilla), 100 g frozen mango, ½ banana, 100 ml almond milk, 1 tsp chia seeds, sliced strawberries  
**Prep time:** 5 min  
**Method:** Blend protein powder, mango, banana, almond milk until thick (add ice if needed). Pour into bowl, top with chia seeds and sliced strawberries.

### 8. Tuna Salad
**Ingredients:** 1 can (185 g) tuna in water (drained), ½ cucumber (diced), 10 cherry tomatoes (halved), juice of ½ lemon, 1 tsp olive oil, salt, pepper, 2 rice cakes  
**Prep time:** 5 min  
**Method:** Mix tuna with cucumber, tomatoes, lemon, oil, salt, pepper. Serve with rice cakes.

### 9. Baked Cod + Roasted Vegetables + Quinoa
**Ingredients:** 150 g cod fillet, ½ zucchini (sliced), ½ capsicum (sliced), ½ onion (wedged), 100 g cooked quinoa, olive oil, Italian herbs, salt, pepper, lemon  
**Prep time:** 35 min  
**Method:** Toss vegetables in olive oil, herbs, salt; roast at 200°C for 20 min. Season cod, bake 15 min. Serve over quinoa.

### 10. Chicken Breast Salad with Avocado
**Ingredients:** 150 g grilled chicken breast, mixed salad greens, ¼ avocado (sliced), ½ cucumber, 5 radishes (sliced), 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar, ½ tsp olive oil, salt, pepper  
**Prep time:** 15 min (if chicken already cooked)  
**Method:** Slice chicken, arrange over greens with avocado, cucumber, radish. Drizzle with balsamic and olive oil.
