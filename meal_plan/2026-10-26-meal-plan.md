## Nearby Restaurant Guide

### Near Home — Margaret Drive, Singapore

#### Margaret Drive Hawker Centre
*(directly on Margaret Drive, ~2 min walk from 36 Margaret Drive)*

- **Hakka Thunder Tea Rice (Lei Cha)** — Stall #02-34 · Tofu, stir-fried vegetables (cabbage, spinach, long beans), anchovies, peanuts served over rice with herbal green-tea broth poured over. ~$5.50. Estimated **~380–420 kcal**, ~18 g protein. **Why it fits:** High fibre, plant-heavy, low in refined fat; herbal broth displaces high-cal sauces. Skip the fried shallots to shave ~50 kcal.

- **Yu Mi Xiang Fish Soup** — Light, clear-broth fish soup with fish fillet, lettuce, tomato and tofu. From ~$5. Estimated **~350–400 kcal**, ~28 g protein. **Why it fits:** Lean protein from fresh fish, minimal oil, hydrating broth. Ask for brown rice if available or take half-portion white rice.

- **Yong Tau Foo (clear soup base)** — Choose steamed tofu, fish tofu, bittergourd, ladies' fingers, brinjal. Estimated **~380–420 kcal**, ~25–30 g protein. **Why it fits:** You control protein and fibre content; clear soup keeps sodium and fat low versus dry versions. Avoid spicy sambal sauce.

- **Economical Mixed Rice (Cai Fan)** — Choose steamed fish, braised tofu, stir-fried leafy greens; limit to 1 portion brown rice or half white rice. Estimated **~380–450 kcal** depending on choices. Avoid deep-fried sides.

#### Commonwealth Crescent Food Centre
*(~15 min walk or short bus ride from Margaret Drive)*

- **Cantonese Congee/Porridge** — Fish or chicken congee (no preserved egg). Estimated **~250–320 kcal**, ~20 g protein. Good lighter dinner option.

### Near Office — The Metropolis Tower 1, Buona Vista

#### SaladStop! (The Metropolis)
*9 North Buona Vista Road, The Metropolis*

- **Tuna San Bowl** — Romaine, tuna, olives, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, capers, boiled egg, light vinaigrette. Estimated **~400–440 kcal**, ~30–34 g protein. **Why it fits:** High protein from tuna + egg, fibre from greens, minimal refined carbs; ask for dressing on the side.

- **Build-Your-Own Harvest Bowl** — Start with mixed greens or quinoa; add grilled chicken + edamame + cucumber + cherry tomatoes + lemon vinaigrette. Estimated **~480–520 kcal**, ~38–42 g protein. **Why it fits:** Fully customisable — skip croutons and creamy dressings to stay lean.

**Tips:** Ask for grilled (not crispy) chicken. Choose olive oil/lemon or balsamic over creamy dressings. Skip croutons and fried toppings.

#### The Soup Spoon Union (The Metropolis)
*Within The Metropolis mall*

- **Chunky Chicken & Vegetables Soup (large)** — Tomato-based broth with chicken pieces, potato, carrot, celery. Estimated **~280–340 kcal**, ~28 g protein. Pair with a whole-grain roll (~130 kcal) for a complete meal. **Why it fits:** Low-fat, warming, protein-rich; high water content supports satiety.

- **Roasted Pumpkin Soup** — Lighter option. Estimated **~130–180 kcal** for regular. Pair with a chicken main or protein add-on to reach ≥25 g protein at lunch.

**Tips:** Opt for broth-based soups over creamy bisques. Add a side protein to hit daily protein targets.

## Self-Made Meal Recipes

### Scrambled Eggs with Avocado Toast (Prep: 10 min)
**Ingredients:** 3 eggs · 1 slice whole-grain bread · ½ avocado · salt · pepper · pinch of dried herbs  
**Method:** Whisk eggs, cook over medium-low heat stirring gently until just set. Toast bread, smash avocado on top. Serve eggs alongside.  
**~420 kcal · 22 g P · 26 g C · 22 g F**

---

### Overnight Oats (Prep: 5 min evening before)
**Ingredients:** 60 g rolled oats · 1 scoop (30 g) whey protein powder · 1 tbsp chia seeds · 1 banana (sliced) · 200 ml unsweetened almond milk · pinch of cinnamon  
**Method:** Combine all in a jar, stir well, refrigerate overnight. Eat cold in the morning.  
**~380 kcal · 30 g P · 42 g C · 8 g F**

---

### Protein Smoothie (Prep: 5 min)
**Ingredients:** 1 scoop whey protein · 1 banana · large handful spinach (30–40 g) · 30 g rolled oats · 250 ml unsweetened almond milk · ice cubes  
**Method:** Blend all ingredients until smooth.  
**~360 kcal · 30 g P · 42 g C · 6 g F**

---

### Egg White Omelette (Prep: 10 min)
**Ingredients:** 4 egg whites · 1 whole egg · ½ bell pepper (diced) · 60 g mushrooms (sliced) · large handful spinach · salt · pepper · 1 slice whole-grain toast  
**Method:** Whisk egg whites and whole egg together. Heat non-stick pan over medium heat (no oil needed). Add vegetables and wilt briefly; pour in egg mixture. Cook 3–4 min, fold in half. Serve with toast.  
**~380 kcal · 30 g P · 18 g C · 12 g F**

---

### Chicken Brown Rice Bento (Prep: 20 min, make night before)
**Ingredients:** 150 g chicken breast · ¾ cup brown rice (dry: ~60 g) · cucumber · carrot · low-sodium soy sauce + sesame oil dipping sauce  
**Method:** Cook brown rice. Season chicken breast with light soy, ginger powder, pepper; bake at 200 °C for 18 min or pan-grill 6 min per side. Slice and pack with rice and raw vegetables.  
**~490 kcal · 42 g P · 42 g C · 10 g F**

---

### Quinoa Chicken Bowl (Prep: 25 min)
**Ingredients:** ½ cup quinoa (dry) · 150 g chicken breast · 1 capsicum · 1 small courgette · 100 g cherry tomatoes · 1 tsp olive oil · lemon juice · dried oregano · salt & pepper  
**Method:** Cook quinoa per packet. Dice and roast vegetables at 200 °C for 15 min with olive oil. Pan-grill chicken. Assemble bowl; squeeze lemon over top.  
**~520 kcal · 42 g P · 40 g C · 14 g F**

---

### Pan-Seared Salmon + Brown Rice + Bok Choy (Prep: 20 min)
**Ingredients:** 150–180 g salmon fillet · ¾ cup cooked brown rice · 200 g bok choy · 2 garlic cloves · 1 tsp olive oil · low-sodium soy sauce · lemon  
**Method:** Season salmon with soy and pepper; sear skin-side down 4 min, flip 2 min. Stir-fry bok choy with garlic in olive oil 3–4 min. Serve over rice; squeeze lemon on salmon.  
**~500–520 kcal · 42–44 g P · 38 g C · 12 g F**

---

### Grilled Chicken Thigh + Roasted Sweet Potato + Salad (Prep: 30 min)
**Ingredients:** 150 g chicken thigh (skinless) · 100 g sweet potato · mixed salad greens · 1 tbsp olive oil · lemon juice · salt · pepper · paprika  
**Method:** Rub chicken with paprika, salt, pepper; grill or bake at 200 °C for 22 min. Cube sweet potato, toss with a little olive oil, roast 20 min. Dress salad with remaining olive oil and lemon.  
**~450 kcal · 36 g P · 32 g C · 12 g F**

---

### Steamed Barramundi + Kailan (Prep: 20 min)
**Ingredients:** 180 g barramundi fillet · 200 g kailan (Chinese broccoli) · 2 garlic cloves (minced) · 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce · ½ tbsp sesame oil · ¾ cup cooked brown rice  
**Method:** Steam barramundi over boiling water 8–10 min. Blanch kailan 2 min; toss with garlic sautéed briefly in sesame oil. Drizzle soy over fish.  
**~460 kcal · 42 g P · 40 g C · 8 g F**

---

### Chicken Meatball Whole-Wheat Pasta (Prep: 30 min)
**Ingredients:** 70 g whole-wheat spaghetti · 150 g lean chicken mince · 1 egg white · 1 garlic clove (minced) · 200 g canned crushed tomatoes · 1 small onion · 1 tsp olive oil · dried basil & oregano  
**Method:** Mix mince with egg white, garlic, herbs; roll into balls (~10). Brown meatballs in olive oil 5 min. Add onion, then tomatoes; simmer 10 min. Cook pasta al dente; toss with sauce.  
**~520 kcal · 38 g P · 52 g C · 12 g F**

---

### Baked Chicken Breast + Cauliflower Mash + Asparagus (Prep: 30 min)
**Ingredients:** 200 g chicken breast · 300 g cauliflower florets · 100 g asparagus · 50 ml low-fat milk · 1 garlic clove · 1 tsp olive oil · salt · pepper · paprika  
**Method:** Season chicken with paprika, salt, pepper; bake at 200 °C for 22–25 min. Steam cauliflower until tender, blend with warm milk, garlic, salt. Roast asparagus at 200 °C for 10 min.  
**~480 kcal · 45 g P · 22 g C · 16 g F**

---

### Cauliflower Fried Rice (Prep: 15 min)
**Ingredients:** 300 g cauliflower (riced in food processor) · 2 eggs · 150 g chicken breast (diced) · 60 g frozen mixed vegetables (peas, corn, carrot) · 1½ tbsp low-sodium soy sauce · 1 tsp sesame oil · 2 spring onions (sliced)  
**Method:** Cook chicken in lightly oiled pan. Push to side; scramble eggs. Add cauliflower rice and frozen vegetables; stir-fry 4–5 min. Season with soy and sesame oil. Top with spring onions.  
**~420 kcal · 38 g P · 20 g C · 14 g F**

## Tips for Success

1. **Meal prep Sunday evening:** Cook a large batch of brown rice and quinoa; marinate and bake 2–3 chicken breasts; portion into containers for weekday lunches and dinners.
2. **Hydration:** Aim for 2.5–3 L of water daily. Herbal tea (non-spicy) counts toward fluids.
3. **Restaurant customisations:** Always ask for sauces/dressings on the side; request grilled over fried; skip croutons and fried shallot garnishes.
4. **Hawker centre strategy:** At Margaret Drive Hawker Centre, choose clear-broth dishes over gravy-heavy ones; pair with extra steamed vegetables if available.
5. **Protein priority:** If a meal falls short on protein, add a protein shake, hard-boiled egg, or cottage cheese as a top-up snack.
6. **Weekend cooking:** Use Saturday's extra time to batch-prepare meatballs and cauliflower mash for Sunday, saving prep time.
