## Nearby Restaurant Guide

### Near Home — Margaret Drive, Queenstown

#### Margaret Drive Hawker Centre
**Address:** 38A Margaret Drive, Singapore 142038 (~2 min walk)
**Hours:** Most stalls 7 am – 8 pm; some open until 9 pm

| Stall | Recommended Dish | ~Kcal | Why It Fits |
|-------|-----------------|-------|-------------|
| Tong Kee Chicken Rice | Steamed chicken (no skin) + half portion rice + clear soup | ~380 | High protein (~30 g), low fat if you skip skin and roasting, clear broth is filling |
| Sliced Fish Soup | Clear fish broth with bee hoon, firm tofu, lettuce | ~360 | Lean white fish, clear broth, high satiety, low calorie |
| Hakka Thunder Tea Rice | Brown rice with assorted vegetables + tea broth | ~480 | Rich in fibre, antioxidants, plant protein from tofu; skip extra rice serving |

**Ordering tips:**
- Always choose **steamed** over roasted or fried.
- Request **half rice** or substitute extra vegetables.
- Ask for sauce **on the side** to control sodium and calories.
- For fish soup, add extra **tofu** for protein without significant calories.

## Self-Made Meal Recipes

### Breakfasts

#### 1. Greek Yogurt Parfait
**Prep time:** 5 min | **Serves:** 1 | **~350 kcal, 22 g protein**

- 200 g plain full-fat Greek yogurt
- 30 g rolled oats (dry)
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- 80 g mixed berries (fresh or thawed frozen)

Layer yogurt, oats, berries, and chia in a bowl. Eat immediately or refrigerate up to 2 hours.

---

#### 2. 3-Egg Scramble on Wholegrain Toast
**Prep time:** 8 min | **Serves:** 1 | **~360 kcal, 23 g protein**

- 3 large eggs
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 80 g baby spinach
- 1 slice wholegrain bread (toasted)
- Salt and black pepper to taste

Whisk eggs with salt and pepper. Heat oil in non-stick pan over medium heat. Add spinach, stir until wilted (30 sec), pour in eggs. Scramble gently to preference. Serve with toast.

---

#### 3. Overnight Oats with Greek Yogurt, Banana & Chia
**Prep time:** 5 min (night before) | **Serves:** 1 | **~510 kcal, 28 g protein**

- 60 g rolled oats
- 200 ml low-fat milk
- 100 g plain Greek yogurt
- 1 medium banana, sliced
- 1 tbsp chia seeds

Mix all ingredients in a jar or bowl. Cover and refrigerate overnight. Eat cold next morning.

---

#### 4. Veggie Egg Omelette with Toast
**Prep time:** 10 min | **Serves:** 1 | **~330 kcal, 24 g protein**

- 3 large eggs
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 100 g sliced mushrooms
- 50 g diced red bell pepper
- Handful baby spinach
- 1 slice wholegrain toast

Sauté mushrooms and pepper in oil for 2 min. Pour in whisked eggs, add spinach. Fold omelette when set. Serve with toast.

---

#### 5. Protein Smoothie Bowl
**Prep time:** 5 min | **Serves:** 1 | **~420 kcal, 28 g protein**

- 1 scoop vanilla protein powder (~25 g protein)
- 200 ml unsweetened almond milk
- 1 medium banana (frozen)
- 80 g frozen mixed berries
- 20 g granola (topping)
- 1 tsp shredded coconut (topping)

Blend protein powder, almond milk, frozen banana, and berries until thick. Pour into a bowl, top with granola and coconut.

---

#### 6. Avocado Poached Egg Toast
**Prep time:** 10 min | **Serves:** 1 | **~380 kcal, 18 g protein**

- 2 large eggs
- ½ ripe avocado
- 1 slice wholegrain bread (toasted)
- 5–6 cherry tomatoes, halved
- Squeeze of lemon juice
- Salt and black pepper

Poach eggs in simmering water for 3 min. Mash avocado with lemon, salt, and pepper. Spread on toast, top with poached eggs and cherry tomatoes.

---

#### 7. Protein Oatmeal with Blueberries
**Prep time:** 5 min | **Serves:** 1 | **~380 kcal, 28 g protein**

- 60 g rolled oats
- 200 ml water
- 1 scoop vanilla protein powder
- 80 g fresh or frozen blueberries

Cook oats in water for 3–4 min. Remove from heat, stir in protein powder. Top with blueberries.

---

### Dinners

#### 1. Steamed Seabass with Brown Rice & Bok Choy
**Prep time:** 20 min | **Serves:** 1 | **~430 kcal, 35 g protein**

- 150 g seabass fillet
- 3 slices fresh ginger
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp reduced-sodium soy sauce
- 150 g cooked brown rice
- 200 g bok choy
- 1 tsp olive oil + 2 garlic cloves, minced

Steam fish on a plate with ginger for 10–12 min. Drizzle sesame oil and soy sauce over. Stir-fry bok choy with garlic in olive oil for 3 min. Serve with brown rice.

---

#### 2. Baked Herb Chicken Thigh with Sweet Potato & Broccoli
**Prep time:** 30 min | **Serves:** 1 | **~470 kcal, 38 g protein**

- 160 g skinless, boneless chicken thigh
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- ½ tsp dried rosemary
- 150 g sweet potato, cubed
- 200 g broccoli florets
- Salt and pepper

Preheat oven to 200 °C. Toss chicken with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, salt, and pepper. Place on tray with sweet potato and broccoli. Bake 25–28 min, turning vegetables halfway.

---

#### 3. Teriyaki Chicken with Quinoa & Steamed Broccolini
**Prep time:** 25 min | **Serves:** 1 | **~480 kcal, 40 g protein**

- 150 g chicken breast
- 2 tbsp reduced-sodium teriyaki sauce
- 80 g quinoa (dry) → ~160 g cooked
- 200 g broccolini or broccoli

Marinate chicken in teriyaki sauce for 10 min. Pan-fry in non-stick pan for 6–7 min per side. Cook quinoa per packet. Steam broccolini 4 min. Slice chicken and serve over quinoa with broccolini.

---

#### 4. Baked Salmon with Quinoa & Roasted Asparagus
**Prep time:** 25 min | **Serves:** 1 | **~510 kcal, 42 g protein**

- 150 g salmon fillet
- 1 tsp olive oil
- Juice of ½ lemon
- ½ tsp dried dill
- 80 g quinoa (dry)
- 200 g asparagus spears

Preheat oven to 200 °C. Place salmon and asparagus on a lined tray. Drizzle with olive oil, lemon, and dill. Roast 15 min. Cook quinoa. Serve together.

---

#### 5. Turkey Mince Lettuce Wraps with Brown Rice
**Prep time:** 20 min | **Serves:** 1 | **~450 kcal, 40 g protein**

- 150 g lean turkey mince
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- ½ small onion, diced
- ½ red bell pepper, diced
- 1 tbsp reduced-sodium soy sauce
- 4–5 large iceberg lettuce leaves
- 100 g cooked brown rice (side)

Brown mince in olive oil with garlic, onion, and pepper for 8 min. Add soy sauce, stir. Spoon into lettuce cups. Serve with brown rice on the side.

---

### Lunch (Self-Made)

#### Tuna Quinoa Bowl
**Prep time:** 10 min | **Serves:** 1 | **~430 kcal, 38 g protein**

- 150 g canned tuna in water (drained)
- 160 g cooked quinoa (from ~80 g dry)
- ½ cucumber, sliced
- 10 cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Juice of ½ lemon
- Salt and pepper

Mix all ingredients. Eat at room temperature or cold from the fridge.

## Key Tips for the Week

1. **Meal prep Sunday:** Cook a batch of brown rice and quinoa to save time on weeknights.
2. **Protein first:** Always eat your protein component before the carbs — it improves satiety signals.
3. **Hawker hacks:** Steamed over fried, half-rice portion, sauce on the side, clear soup over coconut-milk soup.
4. **Hydration:** Aim for 2.5–3 L of water/day; it curbs false hunger and supports fat metabolism.
5. **SaladStop! ordering:** Use their online app before arriving to skip lunchtime queues at The Metropolis.
