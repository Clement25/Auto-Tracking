## Self-Made Meal Recipes

### Breakfast

#### Greek Yogurt Berry Bowl (Mon)
- 200 g low-fat Greek yogurt · 100 g mixed berries (fresh or frozen) · 2 tbsp low-sugar granola
- **Prep:** 2 min. Layer yogurt, top with berries, finish with granola.

#### Scrambled Eggs on Toast (Tue)
- 3 large eggs · 2 slices whole-grain bread · 1 tsp olive oil · black pepper
- **Prep:** 5 min. Beat eggs, scramble in a non-stick pan over medium-low heat. Toast bread.

#### Overnight Oats (Wed)
- 50 g rolled oats · 200 ml unsweetened almond milk · 1 tbsp chia seeds · 1 banana (sliced)
- **Prep:** 5 min the night before. Mix oats, milk, and chia in a jar. Refrigerate overnight. Add banana in the morning.

#### Protein Smoothie Bowl (Thu)
- 1 scoop vanilla whey protein · 1 frozen banana · 100 g frozen spinach · 150 ml almond milk · 1 tbsp chia seeds · handful of berries
- **Prep:** 5 min. Blend all except toppings until thick. Pour into bowl, top with chia seeds and berries.

#### Avocado Toast with Egg (Fri)
- 2 slices whole-grain bread · ½ avocado · 1 egg (poached) · pinch of salt & black pepper
- **Prep:** 8 min. Toast bread. Mash avocado onto toast. Poach egg (3 min in simmering water with splash of vinegar). Top toast with egg.

#### Protein Banana-Oat Pancakes (Sat)
- 2 large eggs · 1 ripe banana · 30 g rolled oats · optional: dash of vanilla extract
- **Prep:** 10 min. Blend all ingredients until smooth. Cook in a lightly oiled non-stick pan, 2–3 min per side on medium heat.

#### Cottage Cheese Fruit Bowl (Sun)
- 200 g low-fat cottage cheese · 1 kiwi + ¼ mango (diced) · 1 tsp honey · 10 g crushed walnuts
- **Prep:** 3 min. Spoon cottage cheese into bowl, top with fruit, drizzle honey, scatter walnuts.

### Lunch (Self-Made)

#### Tuna Salad Wrap (Tue)
- 1 can tuna in water (150 g drained) · 1 large whole-grain tortilla · 2 tbsp low-fat Greek yogurt · 1 tsp Dijon mustard · lettuce leaves · 1 tomato (sliced) · squeeze of lemon
- **Prep:** 5 min. Mix tuna with yogurt, mustard, and lemon. Lay on tortilla with lettuce and tomato. Roll tightly.

#### Steamed Tofu & Bok Choy Bowl (Sat)
- 150 g firm tofu (cubed) · 80 g dry brown rice (cooked per packet) · 1 bunch bok choy · 1 tbsp light soy sauce · 1 tsp sesame oil · 1 clove garlic (minced)
- **Prep:** 20 min. Cook rice. Steam tofu 5 min. Stir-fry bok choy with garlic, deglaze with soy sauce. Plate rice, tofu, greens. Drizzle sesame oil.

#### Tuna & Quinoa Salad (Sun)
- 1 can tuna in water (120 g drained) · 80 g quinoa (cooked) · 2 large handfuls mixed greens · 100 g cherry tomatoes (halved) · juice of ½ lemon · 1 tsp olive oil · black pepper
- **Prep:** 15 min. Cook quinoa (or use pre-cooked). Toss all ingredients. Dress with lemon juice, olive oil, pepper.

---

### Dinner (Self-Made)

#### Pan-Seared Salmon with Quinoa & Broccoli (Mon)
- 150 g salmon fillet · 100 g quinoa (dry) · 150 g broccoli florets · 1 tsp olive oil · lemon juice · salt & pepper
- **Prep:** 25 min. Cook quinoa per packet. Roast broccoli at 200°C for 15 min (or steam). Season salmon, sear in oiled pan 3–4 min per side until cooked through. Serve with lemon wedge.

#### Chicken Breast Stir-Fry with Brown Rice (Wed)
- 180 g chicken breast (sliced) · 80 g dry brown rice · 150 g broccoli + 1 bell pepper (sliced) · 1 tbsp oyster sauce (low-sodium) · 1 tsp sesame oil · 1 clove garlic
- **Prep:** 25 min. Cook rice. Stir-fry garlic and chicken in sesame oil 5–6 min. Add vegetables and oyster sauce. Toss 2–3 min until tender-crisp.

#### Baked Chicken Thighs with Sweet Potato (Thu)
- 200 g skinless chicken thighs · 150 g sweet potato (cubed) · 2 large handfuls mixed salad greens · 1 tsp olive oil · garlic powder · dried thyme · salt & pepper · squeeze of lemon for dressing
- **Prep:** 35 min. Preheat oven to 200°C. Rub chicken with oil and spices. Toss sweet potato in oil and salt. Roast both on a tray 25–30 min. Dress salad greens with lemon and a drizzle of olive oil.

#### Pan-Seared Chicken Breast with Broccoli & Rice (Sun)
- 200 g chicken breast · 100 g dry brown rice · 200 g broccoli + cauliflower (mix) · 1 tsp olive oil · garlic · light soy sauce
- **Prep:** 30 min. Cook rice. Steam vegetables. Season and sear chicken in oiled pan 5–6 min each side until cooked through. Serve with veggies and rice. Drizzle with light soy sauce.

## Tips for Success

1. **Meal prep on Sunday:** Cook a large batch of brown rice and quinoa. Boil 4–6 eggs. This cuts weekday prep time to under 10 min.
2. **Customise at SaladStop!:** Choose light dressings (lemon tahini or citrus vinaigrette) and skip croutons to save ~100 kcal.
3. **At hawker centres:** Always request steamed over roasted/fried options, skip the skin on chicken, and ask for more soup and greens.
4. **Hydration:** Aim for 2–2.5 L water/day. Herbal teas and plain coffee are fine.
5. **Protein shake timing:** Use as a post-workout or afternoon snack to easily hit the 100 g protein target.
