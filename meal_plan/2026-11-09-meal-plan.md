## Nearby Restaurant Guide

### Near Home (36 Margaret Drive, Singapore)

**Margaret Drive Hawker Centre** (~2 min walk, 38 Margaret Drive)
- **Hakka Thunder Tea Rice** (Stall #02-29) — ~400–420 kcal | Packed with vegetables (cabbage, chye sim, beans), tofu, peanuts, and a unique green herb broth. High fibre, moderate protein; ask for extra tofu for more protein. ~$5–6.
- **Steamed Fish with Rice** (various seafood stalls) — ~430–460 kcal | Sea bass or pomfret steamed with ginger and soy sauce. High in lean protein (~35g), low in saturated fat. Skip the fried rice; opt for steamed rice and stir-fried vegetables.
- **Steamed Chicken Rice** (Hainanese chicken rice stall) — ~460–490 kcal | Order steamed (not roasted) chicken without skin. Ask for brown rice where available, skip the chilli sauce to avoid spicy.

### Near Office (The Metropolis, 9 North Buona Vista Drive, Buona Vista)

**The Soup Spoon Union** (The Metropolis, #01-05, 9 North Buona Vista Drive)
- **Chicken & Vegetable Soup** — ~300–350 kcal per regular bowl | Lean protein from chicken, fibre-rich vegetables, low in fat. Pair with a wholegrain roll (+130 kcal) for a complete lunch. MUIS certified halal.
- **Tomato & Lentil Soup** — ~280–320 kcal | Plant protein from lentils, lycopene from tomatoes. Customise by asking for no cream add-on.
- **Velvety Mushroom Stroganoff** — ~252 kcal (lighter option) | Good for a smaller appetite day; add grilled chicken on the side for protein.

**Lean Bento** (near Buona Vista / one-north)
- **Teriyaki Salmon Bento** — ~520–550 kcal | Omega-3-rich salmon, brown rice, edamame and mixed vegetables. High protein (~40g), excellent macro balance for a midday meal.
- **Teriyaki Chicken Bento** — ~490–520 kcal | Lean chicken thigh (or breast), edamame, pickled vegetables and brown rice. Ask for extra greens instead of extra rice.

**The Lawn** (Biopolis Way, ~5 min from Metropolis)
- **Grilled Chicken Quinoa Salad** — ~460–500 kcal | Perfectly seared chicken, quinoa for complete protein, fresh greens and a light vinaigrette. High protein (~38–42g), naturally low in refined carbs.

## Self-Made Meal Recipes

### Spinach & Mushroom Omelette (Mon Breakfast)
**Ingredients:** 3 eggs, 1 cup baby spinach, 4 button mushrooms (sliced), 1 tsp olive oil, salt & pepper
**Method:** Whisk eggs. Sauté spinach and mushrooms in olive oil 2 min. Pour in eggs, fold when set.
**Prep time:** 10 min | ~260 kcal | 22g P

### Overnight Oats (Tue Breakfast)
**Ingredients:** 50g rolled oats, 180ml skimmed milk, 1 tbsp chia seeds, 100g strawberries (sliced), dash of vanilla
**Method:** Mix oats, milk, chia seeds in a jar. Refrigerate overnight. Top with strawberries in the morning.
**Prep time:** 5 min (night before) | ~345 kcal | 14g P

### Protein Smoothie (Wed Breakfast)
**Ingredients:** 1 scoop unflavoured/vanilla whey (~25g protein), 1 banana, 100g baby spinach, 150ml skimmed milk, 1 tbsp peanut butter, ice cubes
**Method:** Blend all ingredients until smooth. Drink immediately.
**Prep time:** 3 min | ~380 kcal | 30g P

### Packed Tuna Wrap (Wed Lunch)
**Ingredients:** 1 wholegrain wrap, 100g canned tuna in water (drained), 2 large lettuce leaves, 1 tomato (sliced), 1 tbsp hummus, squeeze of lemon
**Method:** Spread hummus on wrap. Layer lettuce, tomato, tuna. Season with lemon, pepper. Roll and pack.
**Prep time:** 7 min | ~400 kcal | 36g P

### Stir-Fried Tofu & Vegetables (Thu Dinner)
**Ingredients:** 200g firm tofu (pressed, cubed), 100g baby corn, 100g snap peas, 8 shiitake mushrooms, 2 tsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 garlic clove (minced), 100g cooked brown rice
**Method:** Pan-fry tofu cubes until golden (8 min). Add garlic, then vegetables, stir-fry 4 min. Add soy sauce and sesame oil. Serve over rice.
**Prep time:** 20 min | ~490 kcal | 24g P

### Baked Salmon with Roasted Veg (Fri Dinner)
**Ingredients:** 150g salmon fillet, 150g asparagus, 100g cherry tomatoes, 150g baby potatoes (halved), 1 tsp olive oil, lemon zest, dried dill, black pepper
**Method:** Preheat oven 200°C. Toss potatoes and veg with olive oil. Roast 20 min. Season salmon; lay on tray; bake further 12 min until flaky.
**Prep time:** 10 min active + 32 min bake | ~540 kcal | 40g P

### Banana Protein Pancakes (Sat Breakfast)
**Ingredients:** 2 eggs, 1 ripe banana (mashed), 1 scoop vanilla whey protein, 2 tbsp oat flour, pinch of cinnamon
**Method:** Mash banana. Mix in eggs, whey, oat flour and cinnamon into a batter. Cook 2–3 small pancakes per side on a non-stick pan over medium heat (~3 min per side).
**Prep time:** 15 min | ~410 kcal | 35g P

### Quinoa Bowl with Grilled Chicken (Sun Lunch)
**Ingredients:** 150g chicken breast (marinate: 1 tbsp low-sodium soy, lemon, garlic), 80g cooked quinoa, 1 cup roasted broccoli, 100g cherry tomatoes, 1 tbsp tahini, 1 tbsp lemon juice, water to thin
**Method:** Grill or pan-sear chicken 6–7 min per side. Slice. Assemble over quinoa and veg. Drizzle tahini-lemon dressing.
**Prep time:** 25 min | ~510 kcal | 45g P
