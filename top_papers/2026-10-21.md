# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-10-21)

## [MemT-1.0: Memory-Native Foundation Models for Agentic Tasks](https://huggingface.co/papers)
**Authors:** (MemT team)
**TL;DR:** Introduces MemT-1.0-Nano (36B/3B active) and MemT-1.0-Flash (288B/17B active), two memory-native foundation models designed for agentic workloads. The architecture natively integrates long-term memory retrieval into the forward pass, rather than treating memory as an external lookup. Topped the Hugging Face daily papers list on Oct 20 with 117 upvotes.
**Tags:** `agents`, `memory`, `foundation-models`, `agentic-systems`

---

## [OxyGen: Unified KV Cache Management for VLA Inference under Multi-Task Parallelism](https://github.com/Ponkux/DailyArXiv-cp/issues/597)
**Authors:** (OxyGen team)
**TL;DR:** OxyGen proposes a unified key-value cache management scheme for vision-language-action (VLA) models running concurrent tasks. By sharing and reusing KV caches across heterogeneous robot tasks in parallel, it significantly reduces memory pressure and inference latency for embodied agents. Appeared in the October 19 embodied-AI digest.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `VLA`, `inference-efficiency`, `robotics`, `kv-cache`

---

## [HarnessPro: Recursive Harness Self-Improvement with Pareto Optimization for Performance-Cost Trade-offs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2026.harnesspr)
**Authors:** (HarnessPro team)
**TL;DR:** HarnessPro proposes a method for AI agent execution harnesses to recursively improve themselves, using Pareto optimization to balance task performance against compute cost. The system iteratively refines harness logic, finding configurations that avoid excessive compute without sacrificing benchmark quality. Featured in the Oct 20 Hugging Face trending list.
**Tags:** `agents`, `harness`, `self-improvement`, `efficiency`, `Pareto-optimization`

---

## [Sage-A1.5: Long-Investigation Agents with Evidence Checking and Stall Recovery](https://www.alphaxiv.org/)
**Authors:** (Sage team)
**TL;DR:** Sage-A1.5 is an agent designed for extended, evidence-intensive research tasks. It introduces mechanisms to check intermediate evidence for quality and to automatically redirect its search strategy when progress stalls, reducing dead-end reasoning loops. Listed on alphaXiv for October 20, 2026.
**Tags:** `agents`, `research-agents`, `long-horizon`, `evidence-checking`

## [Harness²: Continual Harness-Level Learning for Agent Execution](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.MA/current)
**Authors:** (Google)
**TL;DR:** Harness² (Google) improves agent execution frameworks through recursive task-level refinement combined with continual harness-level learning, enabling the harness itself to adapt over time without retraining the underlying model. Highlighted in the October 2026 ArXiv AI agents digest.
**Tags:** `agents`, `execution-harness`, `continual-learning`, `google`

---

## [Hybrid Reasoning Agents: Latent-Space Thinking with Selective Tool-Call Verbalization](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.MA/current)
**Authors:** (various)
**TL;DR:** Proposes a hybrid reasoning architecture where agents think primarily in compressed latent representations and only verbalize reasoning as natural language when invoking a tool or outputting a final answer. This reduces token generation overhead while preserving interpretability at decision boundaries. Featured in recent ArXiv multiagent digests.
**Tags:** `agents`, `reasoning`, `latent-space`, `efficiency`

---

## [LERR-Nav: Language-Enhanced Relational Reasoning for Object Navigation](https://github.com/Ponkux/DailyArXiv-cp/issues/595)
**Authors:** (LERR-Nav team)
**TL;DR:** LERR-Nav improves object-navigation in embodied agents by enriching scene representations with relational reasoning conditioned on natural language goals. The approach helps agents generalize to novel object-room configurations without retraining. Appeared in the Oct 17 embodied AI daily digest.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `object-navigation`, `language-grounding`, `relational-reasoning`

---

## [OmniDex: Generalizable Dexterous Grasping in Cluttered Scenes](https://github.com/Ponkux/DailyArXiv-cp/issues/595)
**Authors:** (OmniDex team)
**TL;DR:** OmniDex presents a unified dexterous hand grasping policy capable of operating in densely cluttered environments across diverse object categories. It combines large-scale synthetic pretraining with a small number of real-world demonstrations to achieve strong generalization. Highlighted in the Oct 17 embodied AI digest.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `dexterous-manipulation`, `grasping`

---

## [Thinker-WM: World-Model-Augmented Robot Policy with Counterfactual Post-Training](https://github.com/Ponkux/DailyArXiv-cp/issues/588)
**Authors:** (Thinker-WM team)
**TL;DR:** Thinker-WM augments robot policies with a learned world model and applies counterfactual post-training to improve robustness, letting the robot mentally simulate outcomes before executing actions. DreamTrue is a related concurrent work. Appeared in the Oct 10 embodied AI digest and was presented at CoRL 2026.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `world-models`, `robot-learning`, `CoRL-2026`

---

## [LIVIN: Benchmarking Spatial and Embodied Intelligence in Digital Twins of Lived-In Homes](https://github.com/Ponkux/DailyArXiv-cp/issues/591)
**Authors:** (LIVIN team)
**TL;DR:** LIVIN introduces a benchmark built from photo-realistic digital twins of real lived-in homes, assessing whether embodied agents can reason spatially and interact in messy, realistic domestic environments. The benchmark spans navigation, object search, and rearrangement tasks across hundreds of home configurations. Featured in multiple October 2026 embodied AI digests.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `benchmark`, `spatial-reasoning`, `indoor-navigation`, `digital-twins`

## [Belief Formation in LLM Agents (NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Foundations of Agentic Systems Theory)](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.MA/current)
**Authors:** (various)
**TL;DR:** Investigates how large language model-based agents form, update, and express beliefs about the world and about other agents. Accepted at the NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Foundations of Agentic Systems Theory, the paper formalizes belief-state representations in LLM agent contexts and evaluates consistency under belief-update challenges.
**Tags:** `agents`, `belief-formation`, `LLM`, `multi-agent`, `NeurIPS-2026`

## [Inspect Robots: Evaluating the Capabilities and Safety of Embodied AI](https://github.com/Ponkux/DailyArXiv-cp/issues/586)
**Authors:** (Inspect Robots team)
**TL;DR:** Proposes a structured evaluation framework—Inspect Robots—to systematically test both the task-completion capabilities and the safety properties of embodied AI systems. Submitted to a CoRL 2026 workshop, the framework covers manipulation, navigation, and human-robot interaction safety scenarios.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `safety`, `evaluation`, `benchmarking`, `CoRL-2026`
