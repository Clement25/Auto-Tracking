# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-08-04)

## [HAS-Bench: Evaluating LLM-Based Human-Agent Systems under Configurable Human Participation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04329)
**Authors:** Yaozu Wu, Wei-Chieh Huang, Jizhou Guo, et al. (University of Tokyo, UIUC, MBZUAI, McGill University, Zhejiang University)
**TL;DR:** Introduces a graph-based framework (HAS-FRAMEWORK) representing humans and LLM-powered agents as first-class participants with explicit roles, permissions, and communication paths. HAS-BENCH evaluates Human-Agent Systems under configurable human participation levels across six domains. Experiments show human participation can substantially improve task completion and failure recovery, but gains depend critically on when, how, and by whom input is exercised.
**Tags:** `agents`, `human-agent-collaboration`, `benchmark`, `evaluation`, `llm-agents`

## [Beyond the Leaderboard: A Synthesis of Tool-Use, Planning, and Reasoning Failures in Large Language Model Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05775)
**Authors:** (Multiple authors, 2026)
**TL;DR:** Synthesizes findings from 27 benchmark, taxonomy, and audit papers (2023–2026) spanning 19 distinct benchmarks to identify systematic failure modes in LLM agents. The paper identifies recurring gaps between leaderboard performance and real-world deployment reliability. Key failure patterns include multi-step planning breakdowns, tool-call hallucinations, and compounding reasoning errors in long-horizon tasks.
**Tags:** `agents`, `tool-use`, `planning`, `reasoning`, `evaluation`, `failure-analysis`

## [PlanBench-XL: Evaluating Long-Horizon Planning of LLM Tool-Use Agents in Large-Scale Tool Ecosystems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22388)
**Authors:** (Multiple authors, submitted June 21, 2026)
**TL;DR:** Addresses how LLM agents operate in large tool ecosystems where tasks require discovering relevant tools, inferring implicit sub-goals, and adapting to dynamic environments over long horizons. Introduces a comprehensive benchmark pushing agents beyond simple tool selection to complex, multi-step planning across large tool inventories. Results reveal significant gaps in current agents' ability to handle tool discovery and implicit goal inference.
**Tags:** `agents`, `tool-use`, `planning`, `benchmark`, `long-horizon`

## [AgentLTL: A Trace-Verification Framework for Measuring, Enforcing, and Training Procedural Compliance in Tool-Using LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02599)
**Authors:** (Multiple authors, 2026)
**TL;DR:** Proposes AgentLTL, a framework using Linear Temporal Logic to formally specify and verify procedural compliance in tool-using LLM agents. The approach enables both measurement of compliance at inference time and training-time enforcement via constraint-based learning signals. Experiments demonstrate improved safety and reliability in agents operating under procedural constraints.
**Tags:** `agents`, `tool-use`, `safety`, `formal-verification`, `compliance`, `llm`

## [Agentifying Agentic AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.17332)
**Authors:** (AAAI 2026 WMAC Bridge Program)
**TL;DR:** Argues that learning-based LLM mechanisms must be complemented by structured reasoning and coordination models drawn from the classical multi-agent systems community. Revisits BDI (Belief-Desire-Intention) architectures and formal communication protocols as necessary scaffolds for robust agentic AI. Proposes hybrid architectures combining neural and symbolic components to achieve reliable multi-agent collaboration at scale.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `BDI`, `reasoning`, `symbolic-ai`, `coordination`

## [Embodied AI in Action: Insights from SAE World Congress 2026 on Safety, Trust, Robotics, and Real-World Deployment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10653)
**Authors:** (Multiple authors, 2026)
**TL;DR:** Surveys how embodied AI is rapidly moving from research into real-world systems such as autonomous vehicles, mobile robots, and industrial machines, based on insights from SAE World Congress 2026. Highlights key themes: safety certification, human-robot trust calibration, and the sim-to-real transfer gap. Identifies sensor fusion, interpretable decision-making, and regulatory frameworks as the three most pressing open challenges.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `safety`, `deployment`, `autonomous-vehicles`

## [Safe Embodied AI for Long-horizon Tasks: A Cross-layer Analysis of Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05660)
**Authors:** (Multiple authors, June 2026)
**TL;DR:** Presents a cross-layer safety analysis spanning hardware, perception, planning, and control layers for robotic manipulation over long-horizon tasks. Identifies how failure modes cascade across layers and proposes targeted mitigation strategies at each level. Empirical evaluation on manipulation benchmarks shows that cross-layer awareness significantly reduces catastrophic failures compared to single-layer safety approaches.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `safety`, `manipulation`, `long-horizon`

## [Embodied-R1.5: Evolving Physical Intelligence via Embodied Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11324)
**Authors:** (Multiple authors, June 2026)
**TL;DR:** Introduces Embodied-R1.5, an updated embodied foundation model that evolves physical intelligence through iterative self-improvement on real-world interaction data. Combines vision-language-action (VLA) architecture with reinforcement learning from physical feedback to improve dexterity and generalization. Demonstrates significant improvements over prior Embodied-R1 on diverse manipulation and navigation benchmarks.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `foundation-models`, `robotics`, `VLA`, `reinforcement-learning`

## [Multi-agent Embodied AI: Advances and Future Directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.05108)
**Authors:** (Multiple authors, May 2026)
**TL;DR:** Surveys the intersection of multi-agent systems and embodied AI, covering how teams of AI agents integrated with physical bodies can perceive, reason, and collectively act in shared environments. Reviews advances in cooperative manipulation, multi-robot exploration, and emergent communication among embodied agents. Outlines open research challenges including coordination under partial observability, communication bandwidth constraints, and heterogeneous agent teams.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `multi-agent`, `robotics`, `cooperation`, `survey`

## [Self-evolving Embodied AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04411)
**Authors:** Tongtong Feng, Xin Wang, Wenwu Zhu (2026)
**TL;DR:** Proposes a self-evolving paradigm for embodied AI where agents continuously improve their physical capabilities through autonomous interaction with environments, without relying on curated demonstration data. Introduces a curriculum-based self-play mechanism that progressively increases task difficulty based on agent competence. Results show agents trained with this paradigm outperform imitation-learning baselines on novel manipulation tasks.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `self-supervised`, `curriculum-learning`, `robotics`, `continual-learning`

## [The Evolution of Tool Use in LLM Agents: From Single-Tool Call to Multi-Tool Orchestration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22862)
**Authors:** (Multiple authors, March 2026)
**TL;DR:** Traces the evolution of tool use in LLM agents from simple single-step API calls to complex multi-tool orchestration across long trajectories. Analyzes how agents must now preserve intermediate state, recover from tool failures, and operate under latency and cost constraints when coordinating multiple tools. Proposes a taxonomy of orchestration patterns and evaluates current frontier models across representative orchestration benchmarks.
**Tags:** `agents`, `tool-use`, `llm`, `orchestration`, `multi-tool`
