# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-11-24)

## [When Tools Fail, Agents Fabricate](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28341)
**Authors:** Listed in arXiv cs.AI new listings, 24 November 2026
**TL;DR:** Investigates how tool-using LLM agents handle failed tool calls and whether they honestly report those failures. Proposes a "verification-first" oversight framework to detect and mitigate silent fabrication when tools return errors, with implications for deploying agents in production.
**Tags:** `agents`, `tool-use`, `reliability`, `safety`, `NeurIPS-2026`

---

## [LiteGUI: Post-Training Framework for Lightweight Vision-Only GUI Agents](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/new)
**Authors:** Listed in arXiv cs.AI new listings, 24 November 2026
**TL;DR:** Presents a post-training approach to build compact GUI agents that rely solely on visual input, eliminating the need for accessibility trees or HTML. Achieves competitive performance on standard GUI benchmarks at a fraction of the parameter count of larger models.
**Tags:** `agents`, `gui`, `vision`, `efficient-AI`

---

## [Embodied-Harness: Towards Recursive Self-Improvement for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27689)
**Authors:** arXiv submission, 20 November 2026
**TL;DR:** Proposes a dual-agent system where one agent refines the execution harness for robotic manipulation rather than updating model weights. Benchmarked on RoboTwin 2.0, RoboSPA, and RMBench, showing consistent gains in manipulation success rates through iterative harness improvement.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `manipulation`, `self-improvement`, `agents`

---

## [Embodied Interaction Policy: A Unified Model for Full-Duplex Human-Robot Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27327)
**Authors:** arXiv submission, 20 November 2026
**TL;DR:** Introduces a unified streaming framework that couples an Audio-Interaction expert with a Motion expert through layer-wise joint attention, enabling robots to listen, speak, and act simultaneously. Addresses the full-duplex challenge where speech and motion must be synchronized in real time.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `human-robot-interaction`, `speech`, `motion`, `multimodal`

---

## [Embodied First Exam: Can Coding Agents Control Robots?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12177)
**Authors:** arXiv submission, 9 November 2026
**TL;DR:** Proposes an agent-native benchmark where coding agents choose their own control modality — direct control, code-generation, or policy training — to solve robotic tasks. Reveals significant gaps between strong code-generation performance and actual physical task success, challenging how we evaluate embodied coding agents.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `benchmark`, `coding-agents`, `robotics`, `evaluation`

---

## [Toward the Era of Agentic Embodied Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09124)
**Authors:** arXiv submission, 6 November 2026
**TL;DR:** Presents A2-MEAS, a multi-agent framework that uses code as a scalable interface for robot control and failure recovery, decoupling high-level reasoning from low-level actuation. Demonstrates that multi-agent coordination significantly improves fault tolerance in long-horizon robot tasks.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `multi-agent`, `code-as-interface`, `robotics`, `agentic`

---

## [Embodied Science Agents: Making Embodied Experimental Design Learnable](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12958)
**Authors:** arXiv submission, 7 November 2026
**TL;DR:** Links digital planning agents with physical laboratory instrument control so that AI can autonomously design and run scientific experiments. Introduces a curriculum-learning pipeline that progressively transfers experimental-design skills from simulation to real lab equipment.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `science-agents`, `lab-automation`, `agents`, `experimental-design`

---

## [From Egocentric Human Video to Robot Manipulation in the Era of Embodied Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13610)
**Authors:** arXiv submission, 10 November 2026
**TL;DR:** A comprehensive survey examining how first-person human video can serve as scalable training data for robot manipulation policies. Covers data collection, cross-embodiment transfer, and reward learning from video observations, mapping open problems across the domain.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `video-learning`, `imitation-learning`, `survey`

## [More Agents, More Reliable? Scaling Multi-Agent LLM Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07140)
**Authors:** arXiv cs.MA listing, November 2026
**TL;DR:** Empirically tests whether adding more agents to an LLM multi-agent system improves reliability or compounds errors. Finds diminishing and sometimes negative returns beyond a critical number of agents, proposing coordination protocols that maintain reliability at scale.
**Tags:** `multi-agent`, `reliability`, `scaling`, `llm`, `agents`

---

## [Phantom Consensus: Social Reasoning in Multi-Agent LLM Decision-Making](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.MA/current)
**Authors:** arXiv cs.MA listing, NeurIPS 2026 Workshop
**TL;DR:** Introduces the "phantom consensus" phenomenon where LLM agents falsely converge on shared beliefs without genuine information exchange. Measures the prevalence of social-reasoning shortcuts in collective decision tasks and proposes mitigation strategies for agentic deployments.
**Tags:** `multi-agent`, `social-reasoning`, `llm`, `decision-making`, `NeurIPS-2026`

---

## [HabitusMem: Belief Memory over Evolving User Preferences for LLM Agents](https://github.com/kouweizhu/agents-radar/issues/1046)
**Authors:** Listed in arXiv AI Research Digest, 20 November 2026
**TL;DR:** Proposes a memory architecture for LLM agents that tracks and updates beliefs about changing user preferences using evolutionary search. Outperforms static retrieval-based memory on long-horizon personalization benchmarks, particularly when preferences shift over time.
**Tags:** `agents`, `memory`, `personalization`, `llm`, `preference-learning`

---

## [Forms of LLM-Integrated Applications: From LLM-Chats to Autonomous AI Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11899)
**Authors:** Survey paper, accepted at FLLM 2026 (17–20 November 2026)
**TL;DR:** A taxonomy of seven recurring LLM application architectures, from conversational chatbots to fully autonomous coding agents, analyzed through a unified vocabulary of agents, tools, and control flow. Provides practitioners with a framework for selecting and comparing architectures for specific deployment scenarios.
**Tags:** `agents`, `survey`, `architecture`, `llm`, `taxonomy`
