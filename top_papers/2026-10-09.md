# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-10-09)

## [LIVIN: Benchmarking Spatial and Embodied Intelligence in Digital Twins of Lived-In Homes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.LIVIN)
**Authors:** (arXiv October 2026)
**TL;DR:** LIVIN introduces a benchmark for evaluating spatial and embodied intelligence using digital twins of real-world lived-in homes. It tests agents on tasks requiring 3D scene understanding, long-horizon planning, and object interaction within photorealistic household environments.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `benchmark`, `spatial-intelligence`, `digital-twins`, `navigation`

## [OmniDex: Dexterous Hand Grasping in Cluttered Scenes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.OmniDex)
**Authors:** (arXiv October 2026)
**TL;DR:** OmniDex addresses the challenging problem of dexterous robotic hand grasping in cluttered, unstructured scenes. The method learns generalizable grasp policies that handle occlusion, contact-rich manipulation, and diverse object geometries without scene-specific engineering.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `dexterous-manipulation`, `grasping`, `reinforcement-learning`

## [Dex-One2Many: Learning Dexterous Manipulation from a Single Human Demonstration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.DexOne2Many)
**Authors:** (arXiv October 8, 2026)
**TL;DR:** Dex-One2Many proposes a framework that enables robots to generalize dexterous manipulation skills to novel objects and configurations from just one human demonstration. It leverages structured motion priors and simulation-to-real transfer to achieve strong one-shot generalization.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `dexterous-manipulation`, `imitation-learning`, `one-shot`

## [DreamTrue: Robot World Model with Counterfactual Post-Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.DreamTrue)
**Authors:** (arXiv October 8, 2026)
**TL;DR:** DreamTrue proposes a robot world model that incorporates counterfactual post-training to improve physical plausibility and causal consistency. The model allows robots to mentally simulate "what-if" interventions before executing actions, reducing real-world failures.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `world-models`, `counterfactual-reasoning`, `robotics`, `model-based-rl`

## [SuperNav: Agentic Visual Navigation with Iterative Feedback Revision](https://www.alphaxiv.org/)

**TL;DR:** SuperNav is an agentic navigation system that uses visual feedback to iteratively revise movement decisions during deployment. By coupling perception with a self-critique loop, SuperNav achieves superior navigation success rates in unseen indoor environments.
**Tags:** `agents`, `navigation`, `visual-feedback`, `embodied-ai`, `indoor-navigation`
