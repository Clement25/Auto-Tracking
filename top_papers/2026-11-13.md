# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-11-13)

## [Predictability of Collective Behavior in Networks of LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12256)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2611.12256, submitted Nov 9, 2026)
**TL;DR:** This paper studies information cascades on networks of LLM agents deciding whether to share a post. The key finding is that anticipating collective agent behavior requires evaluating agents in their local network context, not in isolation — a critical insight for deploying multi-agent systems at scale.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `llm`, `collective-behavior`, `networks`

## [MULTILOOP: Evolving Multi-Agent Harnesses for Complex Information-Seeking Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15754)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2611.15754, NeurIPS 2026 CL4FMAgents Workshop)
**TL;DR:** MULTILOOP investigates whether automated harness optimization can identify and fix failures in multi-agent LLM systems, using complex information-seeking tasks from DeepSearchQA as a benchmark. It presents a framework for evolving multi-agent workflows that iteratively improves system-level reliability on hard reasoning tasks.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `llm`, `harness-optimization`, `information-seeking`

## [More Agents, More Reliable? Reliability of Multi-Agent LLM Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07140)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2611.07140, submitted Nov 6, 2026)
**TL;DR:** This paper examines whether adding more LLM agents to a system actually improves reliability under uncertainty about how the system will be used. The findings are nuanced: more agents help in some configurations but can introduce new failure modes, with implications for teams building cooperative agent software.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `llm`, `reliability`, `robustness`

---

## [Signed but Not Safe: Safety Requirements in Multi-Agent LLM Handoffs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07483)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2611.07483, submitted Nov 6, 2026)
**TL;DR:** This work investigates whether safety and behavioral requirements survive handoffs between agents in multi-agent LLM systems. It identifies systematic ways that contracts and constraints can be lost or violated across agent boundaries, raising concerns for real-world deployments.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `llm`, `safety`, `security`

## [Forms of LLM-Integrated Applications: From LLM-Chats to Autonomous AI Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11899)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2610.11899, submitted ~36 days ago; accepted at FLLM 2026)
**TL;DR:** This survey maps out seven recurring architectural forms of LLM-integrated applications — from simple chat interfaces to fully autonomous agent systems — using a deliberately minimal definition of an AI agent. It provides a taxonomy that clarifies the design space for practitioners building agentic applications.
**Tags:** `agents`, `llm`, `survey`, `architecture`, `taxonomy`

## [TokenPilot: Cache-Efficient Context Management for LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17016)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2606.17016)
**TL;DR:** TokenPilot proposes a new context management strategy for LLM agents that improves KV-cache efficiency during long agentic tasks. By intelligently managing what stays in context, it reduces inference costs and latency without sacrificing agent performance on multi-step tasks.
**Tags:** `agents`, `llm`, `inference-efficiency`, `context-management`, `caching`

## [Embodied AI for Scientific Discovery: From Physical Action to Scientific Evidence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04371)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2611.04371, submitted Nov 3, 2026)
**TL;DR:** This review frames robot learning and AI-driven experiment selection as a unified problem, where experiments are partially observed and actions are costly and irreversible. It argues that combining embodied AI with scientific discovery pipelines opens a new paradigm where physical agents can autonomously advance empirical research.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `scientific-discovery`, `robot-learning`, `survey`

**TL;DR:** Open-HumanWAM presents an open recipe for scaling paired human and robot demonstration data to train embodied foundation models. The approach enables richer cross-embodiment generalization and the authors release data collection tools and training code publicly.

## [Embodied Science Agents: Self-Driving Labs Require Physical Grounding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12958)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2611.12958, Nov 11, 2026; accepted at DAI 2026)
**TL;DR:** This paper argues that self-driving labs lose critical physical grounding when foundation models act only as planners without embodied sensing. It proposes a tighter coupling between physical lab hardware and foundation model reasoning, demonstrated on chemistry and materials experiments.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `scientific-discovery`, `self-driving-labs`, `foundation-models`, `robotics`

## [Concertina: Embodying Manipulation and Cognition in One Diffusion Vision-Language Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07513)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2611.07513, Nov 6, 2026)
**TL;DR:** Concertina unifies robotic manipulation and high-level cognitive planning into a single diffusion-based vision-language model. By training manipulation and reasoning jointly, the model achieves better grounding than pipeline approaches, enabling more reliable execution of multi-step physical tasks.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `manipulation`, `diffusion-models`, `vision-language`

## [Look Ahead: Search-Driven Reasoning in Embodied AI](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.RO/new)
**Authors:** (posted Nov 7, 2026)
**TL;DR:** This paper introduces a search-driven reasoning framework that enables embodied AI agents to simulate future states before committing to actions. It applies tree-search methods adapted for continuous physical environments, improving planning quality on long-horizon manipulation and navigation tasks.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `planning`, `reasoning`, `search`, `robotics`
