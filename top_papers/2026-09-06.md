# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-09-06)

## [ADAPT: Agile Diffusion Action Priors for Robust and Steerable Online Text-Driven Humanoid Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00677)
**Authors:** Yan Wu, Chenhao Li, Kaifeng Zhao, Gen Li, Marco Hutter, Siyu Tang
**TL;DR:** ADAPT is an end-to-end closed-loop framework for text-conditioned humanoid whole-body control that uses diffusion-based action priors learned from text-labeled state-action trajectories. Unlike dominant text-to-motion pipelines, ADAPT enables continuous language-command following with balance, natural motion, and smooth transitions — all in a single model. Submitted September 1, 2026.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `humanoid`, `diffusion`, `language-control`

## [CORAL: Towards Autonomous Multi-Agent Evolution for Open-Ended Discovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01658)
**Authors:** Ao Qu, Han Zheng, Zijian Zhou, Yihao Yan, Yihong Tang, Shao Yong Ong, Fenglu Hong, Kaichen Zhou, Chonghe Jiang, Minwei Kong, et al.
**TL;DR:** CORAL is the first framework for autonomous multi-agent evolution on open-ended problems, replacing rigid control with long-running agents that explore, reflect, and collaborate through shared persistent memory and asynchronous execution. Heartbeat-based interventions and isolated workspaces provide practical safeguards. Benchmarked on 10 math/algorithmic/systems tasks, CORAL achieves 3–10× higher improvement rates over fixed evolutionary-search baselines. Accepted at COLM 2026.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `evolution`, `open-ended-discovery`, `LLM`

## [Reinforcement Learning for LLM-based Multi-Agent Systems through Orchestration Traces](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.02801)
**Authors:** (Multiple authors)
**TL;DR:** Proposes learning multi-agent coordination policies from orchestration traces — records of past multi-agent runs — using reinforcement learning to optimize team-level outcomes. The method avoids costly environment simulators by bootstrapping from real execution logs. Experiments show significant gains in task completion rate and efficiency across coding and research agent benchmarks.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `reinforcement-learning`, `orchestration`, `LLM`

## [Safe Embodied AI for Long-horizon Tasks: A Cross-layer Analysis of Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.05660)
**Authors:** (Multiple authors)
**TL;DR:** Analyzes safety challenges specific to long-horizon robotic manipulation tasks across perception, planning, and actuation layers, identifying failure modes that compound over extended task horizons. The paper introduces a cross-layer safety evaluation protocol and demonstrates that current embodied AI systems fail silently in over 30% of long-horizon scenarios. Practical mitigation strategies are proposed for each failure class.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `safety`, `manipulation`, `long-horizon`

## [Cosmos 3: Omnimodal World Models for Physical AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02800)
**Authors:** (NVIDIA Research)
**TL;DR:** Cosmos 3 extends physical world modeling to omnimodal inputs — text, images, video, and proprioceptive sensor streams — enabling robots and autonomous vehicles to build richer internal representations of the physical world. The model is pre-trained on large-scale real and simulated data and demonstrated to improve downstream robot policy learning. This is the third generation of NVIDIA's Cosmos physical-AI platform.

## [QUEST: Training Frontier Deep Research Agents with Fully Synthetic Tasks](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.24218)
**Authors:** (Multiple authors)
**TL;DR:** QUEST introduces a method for training capable research agents entirely on synthetic tasks, bypassing the bottleneck of curated human-annotated research datasets. Synthetic tasks are auto-generated to match the difficulty distribution of real scientific problems, enabling scalable RL training. Agents trained with QUEST outperform baselines on ResearchBench and ScienceAgentBench by significant margins.
