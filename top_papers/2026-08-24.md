# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-08-24)

## [ComBodied Agents: a New Paradigm of Human-Centric Agentic AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10915)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2608.10915, August 11, 2026)
**TL;DR:** Introduces a human-centered agentic AI paradigm that perceives, models, predicts, and supports individual human-state trajectories over time. Uses software tools, sensors, wearables, robots, and human services as action channels to create AI that genuinely centers on the individual human rather than generic tasks.
**Tags:** `agents`, `embodied-ai`, `human-centric`, `multi-modal`

---

## [Towards the Harness of Embodied Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11246)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2608.11246, August 3, 2026)
**TL;DR:** Proposes Scene Graph as Context — a persistent, symbolic representation of the world — and Evaluation as Exit Codes, which detects when an action should terminate, judges whether it succeeded, and on failure diagnoses the cause. This closed-loop design significantly improves embodied agent performance in physical environments.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `scene-graph`, `agents`, `robotics`, `evaluation`

---

## [Mind Viruses: Self-Propagating Ideas in Multi-Agent LLM Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10218)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2608.10218, August 2026)
**TL;DR:** Studies emergent risks in interconnected AI agents where ideas or goals can self-propagate through multi-agent systems analogously to viral spread. Researchers constructed "mind viruses" and demonstrated their spread in collaborative agent teams and chains of interacting agents, raising important AI safety concerns.
**Tags:** `multi-agent`, `llm`, `safety`, `agents`, `emergent-behavior`

---

## [Multi-agent Embodied AI: Advances and Future Directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.05108)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2505.05108, May 2026)
**TL;DR:** Comprehensive survey of multi-agent embodied AI systems, covering advances in coordination, communication, and joint action in physical environments. Identifies key challenges and future research directions including scalability, sim-to-real transfer, and emergent collaboration.
**Tags:** `multi-agent`, `embodied-ai`, `survey`, `robotics`, `coordination`

---

## [OrchestrXR: A Multi-Agent System for Idea-to-Prototype XR Study Authoring](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01588)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2607.01588, July 2026)
**TL;DR:** Presents a multi-agent system that automates the full pipeline from research idea to working XR (extended reality) prototype for user studies. Multiple specialized LLM agents collaborate across design, scripting, and deployment stages, dramatically reducing XR study authoring time.
**Tags:** `multi-agent`, `xr`, `ar`, `vr`, `agents`, `automation`

---

## [ProACT: Towards Breakdown-Aware Proactive Agent in Multi-User Collaboration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03730)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2607.03730, July 2026)
**TL;DR:** Introduces ProACT, a proactive AI agent framework for multi-user collaborative environments that detects "breakdowns" — moments where human collaboration stalls or fails — and intervenes constructively. Evaluated across team workflows showing significant improvements in task completion and collaboration efficiency.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `collaboration`, `proactive`, `human-ai`

---

## [StepFinder: A Temporal Semantic Framework for Failure Attribution in Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03467)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2606.03467, June 2026)
**TL;DR:** Addresses the critical challenge of attributing failures in multi-agent systems to specific steps or agents in the execution trace. Uses temporal semantic analysis to precisely locate where and why a multi-agent pipeline failed, enabling targeted debugging and correction.
**Tags:** `multi-agent`, `debugging`, `evaluation`, `agents`, `llm`

---

## [A1: A Fully Transparent Open-Source, Adaptive and Efficient Truncated Vision-Language-Action Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05672)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2604.05672, April 2026)
**TL;DR:** Presents A1, a fully transparent and open-source Vision-Language-Action (VLA) model for embodied AI, featuring an adaptive truncated architecture that reduces inference costs while maintaining strong performance on manipulation and navigation tasks. All weights, data, and training code are released publicly.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `vla`, `robotics`, `open-source`, `vision-language`

---

## [Self-evolving Embodied AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04411)
**Authors:** Tongtong Feng, Xin Wang, Wenwu Zhu (arXiv:2602.04411, February 2026)
**TL;DR:** Proposes a framework for embodied AI agents that continuously self-improve by learning from interactions with their environment without human supervision. The system uses experience replay and self-supervised objectives to evolve its skills over extended deployment periods.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `self-supervised`, `continual-learning`, `robotics`, `agents`

---

## [Embodied AI: Emerging Risks and Opportunities for Policy Action](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.00117)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2509.00117)
**TL;DR:** Analyzes the policy landscape around embodied AI systems — robots, autonomous vehicles, and AI-powered physical agents — identifying both emerging risks (safety, misuse, accountability) and opportunities for regulatory frameworks. Provides actionable recommendations for policymakers to guide responsible development of embodied AI.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `policy`, `safety`, `robotics`, `governance`
