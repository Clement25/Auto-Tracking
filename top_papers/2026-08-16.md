# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-08-16)

## [Can AI Agents Conduct Open-Ended AI Research? Early Evidence from Two Case Studies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27191)
**Authors:** (Multiple authors — preprint)
**TL;DR:** This paper evaluates whether frontier AI agents can autonomously conduct open-ended AI research. Agents were given six days and thousands of dollars of compute to work on two unpublished NeurIPS 2026 submissions, but fell short of making substantial research progress, revealing key limitations in autonomous scientific reasoning.
**Tags:** `agents`, `ai-research`, `evaluation`, `autonomous-systems`

## [Embodied AI Agents: Modeling the World](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.22355v1)
**Authors:** Meta AI Research team
**TL;DR:** A comprehensive overview of embodied AI agents as AI systems instantiated in visual, virtual, or physical form that learn and interact with users and environments. Published August 11, 2026 by Meta AI Research, it surveys the landscape of world-model-based approaches enabling real-world perception and action.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `world-models`, `robotics`, `survey`

## [RoboDojo: A Unified Platform to Evaluate Embodied AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04434)
**Authors:** University of Hong Kong + consortium (~20 universities incl. UC Berkeley, Tsinghua)
**TL;DR:** RoboDojo introduces a unified sim-and-real benchmark for comprehensive evaluation of generalist robot manipulation policies. It enables standardized comparison across embodiments and tasks, helping close the gap between simulated and real-world robot performance.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `benchmark`, `manipulation`

---

## [CommCP: Efficient Multi-Agent Coordination via LLM-Based Communication with Conformal Prediction](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.MA/current)
**Authors:** (Recent preprint)
**TL;DR:** This paper proposes using conformal prediction to filter noisy inter-agent messages in multi-robot coordination settings, significantly improving reliability. By coupling LLM-based communication with statistically grounded message filtering, agents achieve better task success rates in complex coordination scenarios.
**Tags:** `multi-agent`, `llm`, `robotics`, `coordination`, `conformal-prediction`

## [ProACT: Towards Breakdown-Aware Proactive Agent in Multi-User Collaboration](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.03730)
**Authors:** (Recent preprint)
**TL;DR:** ProACT studies how AI agents can proactively detect and recover from breakdowns in multi-user collaborative settings. The system anticipates communication failures and task ambiguities, intervening before errors compound — advancing robust human-AI teaming.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `human-ai-collaboration`, `proactive`

---

## [Efficient Agentic Reasoning Through Self-Regulated Simulative Planning](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.22138)
**Authors:** (Preprint)
**TL;DR:** Proposes a self-regulated simulation approach that allows LLM agents to mentally rehearse plans before acting, reducing costly trial-and-error in real environments. The framework shows strong gains on multi-step reasoning benchmarks while lowering compute overhead compared to full tree-search methods.
**Tags:** `agents`, `planning`, `reasoning`, `simulation`, `efficiency`

---

## [Embodied-R1.5: Evolving Physical Intelligence via Embodied Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.11324)
**Authors:** (Preprint)
**TL;DR:** Embodied-R1.5 presents a continually self-improving embodied foundation model that adapts its physical intelligence through interaction with the environment. The model integrates vision-language understanding with action generation, demonstrating improved sim-to-real transfer across manipulation and navigation tasks.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `foundation-models`, `robotics`, `sim-to-real`, `continual-learning`

## [AgenticPay: A Multi-Agent LLM Negotiation System for Buyer-Seller Transactions](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.MA/current)
**Authors:** (Recent preprint)
**TL;DR:** AgenticPay introduces a 110+ task benchmark evaluating how well multi-agent LLM systems handle buyer-seller negotiation through natural language. Results show that current agents struggle with complex pricing strategies, creating a clear challenge for socially grounded economic AI.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `negotiation`, `llm`, `benchmark`, `economics`

## [Self-Evolving Embodied AI](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.04411)
**Authors:** (Preprint)
**TL;DR:** This paper investigates agents that autonomously improve their embodied capabilities through environmental feedback loops without human supervision. The system uses curiosity-driven exploration and self-generated curricula to progressively master more complex physical tasks.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `self-improvement`, `reinforcement-learning`, `curriculum-learning`

## [Agentic Artificial Intelligence: Architectures, Taxonomies, and Evaluation of Large Language Model Agents](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.12560v1)
**Authors:** (Preprint)
**TL;DR:** Proposes a unified taxonomy decomposing LLM-based agents into Perception, Brain, Planning, Action, Tool Use, and Collaboration modules, then systematically evaluates agent architectures across software engineering, scientific discovery, and robotics benchmarks. A comprehensive reference for understanding the agent design space.
**Tags:** `agents`, `llm`, `survey`, `taxonomy`, `evaluation`

## [Forage V2: Knowledge Evolution and Transfer in Autonomous Agent Organizations](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.19837)
**Authors:** (Preprint)
**TL;DR:** Forage V2 models how autonomous agent collectives accumulate, evolve, and transfer knowledge across organizational structures. It demonstrates that structured knowledge-sharing protocols significantly boost collective task performance, offering a blueprint for scalable multi-agent systems.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `knowledge-transfer`, `autonomous-systems`, `organization`
