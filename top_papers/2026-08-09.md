# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-08-09)

## [Qwen-UI-Agent Technical Report: Toward Next-Generation Real-World Centric Foundation GUI Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28227)
**Authors:** Qwen Team (Alibaba)
**TL;DR:** Qwen-UI-Agent is a real-world-centric GUI agent for mobile, computer use, and web browsers optimized for everyday tasks on real Android devices. It achieves state-of-the-art on mobile benchmarks (82.1% MobileWorld, 97.5% AndroidDaily) while remaining competitive with frontier models like Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.6 Sol on OSWorld and WebArena. A major step toward reliable cross-platform agent deployments under changing real-world conditions.
**Tags:** `gui-agents`, `mobile-agents`, `computer-use`, `vision-language-action`, `benchmark`

## [Self-Evolving World Models for LLM Agent Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30639)
**Authors:** Xuan Zhang, Wenxuan Zhang, See-Kiong Ng, Yang Deng (NUS, SUTD, SMU)
**TL;DR:** Introduces WorldEvolver, a framework that allows a world model to self-revise its context at deployment time without updating model parameters. Three modules — Episodic Memory, Semantic Memory, and Selective Foresight — jointly filter unreliable predictions before they influence agent reasoning. Evaluated on ALFWorld and ScienceWorld, WorldEvolver improves both world model accuracy and downstream agent success rates.
**Tags:** `agents`, `world-models`, `llm-planning`, `memory`, `self-evolution`

## [Qwen-AgentWorld: Language World Models for General Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24597)
**Authors:** Qwen Team (Alibaba)
**TL;DR:** Proposes a language-based world model framework that enables general-purpose LLM agents to simulate environment dynamics through natural language, reducing the need for task-specific simulators. The system allows agents to plan ahead by querying the language world model rather than directly interacting with the environment, improving sample efficiency and generalization.
**Tags:** `agents`, `world-models`, `language-models`, `planning`, `generalization`

## [HAS-Bench: Evaluating LLM-Based Human-Agent Systems under Configurable Human Participation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04329)
**Authors:** Yijia Shao et al.
**TL;DR:** Presents HAS-Bench, a benchmark for evaluating systems where humans and LLM agents collaborate with configurable levels of human involvement. The benchmark surfaces how agent performance degrades as human oversight decreases and reveals that current agents fail to adapt their strategies to varying participation levels. Provides a key framework for studying human-in-the-loop agent deployment.
**Tags:** `agents`, `human-agent-interaction`, `benchmark`, `evaluation`, `llm`

## [Evaluation and Benchmarking of LLM Agents: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21504)
**Authors:** Multiple authors (KDD '25 paper)
**TL;DR:** A comprehensive survey covering the full landscape of LLM agent evaluation — taxonomies of agent behavior, safety considerations, existing benchmarks, and gaps in current methodology. Identifies that most benchmarks focus on single-turn tasks and lack coverage of long-horizon, multi-agent, and real-world deployment scenarios. An essential reference for researchers designing new agent evaluation protocols.
**Tags:** `agents`, `evaluation`, `benchmark`, `survey`, `safety`

## [Safe Embodied AI for Long-horizon Tasks: A Cross-layer Analysis of Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05660)
**Authors:** Authors from robotics research groups (2026)
**TL;DR:** Analyzes safety failures in embodied AI systems across perception, planning, and actuation layers for long-horizon manipulation tasks. Finds that the majority of safety-critical failures originate from compounding errors across layers rather than single-layer breakdowns. Proposes cross-layer monitoring mechanisms that significantly reduce safety violations in extended task sequences.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `safety`, `manipulation`, `long-horizon`

## [Embodied-R1.5: Evolving Physical Intelligence via Embodied Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11324)
**Authors:** Research team (2026)
**TL;DR:** Extends the R1 series to physical robot control by training embodied foundation models using reinforcement learning with real-world interaction feedback. The model demonstrates improved generalization to novel manipulation tasks compared to imitation-learning-only baselines. Represents a step toward truly adaptive physical agents that improve through self-play in the physical world.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `foundation-models`, `reinforcement-learning`, `manipulation`, `robotics`

## [Embodied Passive Aeroacoustic Perception Enables Relative Sensing and Pursuit Between Aerial Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00401)
**Authors:** Yanbaihui Liu et al. (IROS 2026)
**TL;DR:** Demonstrates that aerial robots can sense and pursue one another using passive aeroacoustic signals — sound generated by rotor airflow — without active sensors or communication. The approach enables lightweight relative localization and pursuit behavior in GPS-denied environments. Accepted to IROS 2026, marking a novel sensory modality for multi-drone coordination.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `aerial-robots`, `drones`, `perception`, `multi-agent`

## [Diffusion-Based Body Schema Learning Enabling Abnormal-State Adaptation in Musculoskeletal Robots](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.RO/current)
**Authors:** Robotics researchers (August 2026)
**TL;DR:** Applies diffusion models to learn body schema representations in musculoskeletal robots, allowing them to detect and adapt to abnormal joint states such as muscle damage or wear. The learned schema enables real-time compensation strategies without human re-programming. Addresses a major gap in robustness for bio-inspired robot platforms.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `diffusion-models`, `body-schema`, `adaptation`

## [OC-VLA++: Monocular Geometry-Guided Cross-View Consistency for Viewpoint-Robust Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.RO/current)
**Authors:** Robotics and vision researchers (August 2026)
**TL;DR:** Improves vision-language-action (VLA) models for robotic manipulation by incorporating monocular depth geometry to enforce consistent action predictions across viewpoints. The method significantly reduces manipulation failure rates when camera positions vary at test time, a common real-world deployment challenge. Demonstrates strong improvements on standard robotic manipulation benchmarks.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `vla`, `robotics`, `manipulation`, `vision`, `viewpoint-robustness`

## [Staged Multi-Agent Training (SMAT) for Hip Exoskeletons: Metabolic and Biomechanical Validation](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.RO/current)
**Authors:** Biomechanics and AI researchers (August 2026)
**TL;DR:** Proposes a staged multi-agent training curriculum for learning co-adaptive controllers in hip exoskeletons, validated through both metabolic cost measurements and biomechanical motion analysis. The simulation-trained controller transfers effectively to real hardware, reducing metabolic cost in walking tasks. Demonstrates the promise of RL-based methods for personalized wearable robotics.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `exoskeletons`, `multi-agent`, `reinforcement-learning`, `wearable-robotics`

## [InfiAgent: Self-Evolving Pyramid Agent Framework for Infinite Scenarios](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22502)
**Authors:** InfiAgent Team
**TL;DR:** Presents a hierarchical agent framework organized as a pyramid of specialized sub-agents that can self-evolve by acquiring new capabilities for previously unseen scenarios. The pyramid structure allows high-level agents to delegate to lower-level specialists while learning to expand the specialist pool when gaps are encountered. Evaluated across diverse open-ended task settings demonstrating strong zero-shot generalization.
**Tags:** `agents`, `self-evolution`, `hierarchical`, `llm`, `multi-agent`
