# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-10-15)

## [ChunkAct: Action Chunking for Agentic Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18874)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2610.18874, submitted Oct 14 2026)
**TL;DR:** Proposes action chunking as a principled abstraction for agentic robotics, enabling robots to commit to coherent multi-step action sequences rather than reacting step-by-step. The approach improves both efficiency and task success on long-horizon manipulation benchmarks. Addresses the "decision paralysis" problem in fine-grained robot control.
**Tags:** `agents`, `robotics`, `embodied-ai`, `action-chunking`, `manipulation`

---

## [EmbodiedEvo: Self-improving Coding Agents for Physical Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18867)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2610.18867, submitted Oct 14 2026)
**TL;DR:** Introduces a framework where coding agents iteratively write, execute, and evaluate robot control code in simulation, bootstrapping physical intelligence without human-labeled demonstrations. Self-improvement loops produce policies competitive with expert-coded solutions on a suite of dexterous manipulation tasks.
**Tags:** `agents`, `embodied-ai`, `self-improvement`, `robotics`, `code-generation`

## [CyberArena: Benchmarking AI Agents Against Live Attackers and Defenders](https://www.alphaxiv.org/)
**Authors:** (alphaXiv listing, Oct 13–14 2026)
**TL;DR:** CyberArena is a live cybersecurity benchmark where AI agents compete against real human red- and blue-team operators in a realistic network environment. Evaluates agent capability across attack planning, lateral movement, and incident response. Current frontier models show strong red-team performance but lag human defenders in adaptive response.
**Tags:** `agents`, `cybersecurity`, `benchmark`, `red-teaming`, `multi-agent`

## [Interaction-as-a-Judge: Evaluating Computer-Use Agents via Interactive Software](https://www.alphaxiv.org/)
**Authors:** (alphaXiv listing, Oct 13–14 2026)
**TL;DR:** Proposes using computer-use agents themselves as automated judges for interactive software quality, replacing static screenshots with dynamic interaction traces. The method correlates well with human evaluation and scales cheaply across GUI-heavy applications. Demonstrates applications in regression testing and UX auditing.
**Tags:** `agents`, `computer-use`, `evaluation`, `GUI`, `benchmark`

## [OmniDex: Scaling Dexterous Hand Grasping to Diverse Cluttered Scenes](https://github.com/Ponkux/DailyArXiv-cp/issues/589)
**Authors:** (submitted Oct 8 2026, featured in Oct 11 digest)

## [DreamTrue: Action-Faithful Robot World Models via Counterfactual Post-Training](https://github.com/Ponkux/DailyArXiv-cp/issues/589)
**Authors:** (submitted Oct 8 2026, featured in Oct 11 digest)
**TL;DR:** Addresses the action-unfaithfulness problem in video-based world models, where predicted futures drift from what the robot's actions would actually produce. Counterfactual fine-tuning pairs each training rollout with synthetic action-negation counterparts, tightening causal grounding. Improves planning accuracy in model-based RL for contact-rich tasks.
**Tags:** `robotics`, `world-models`, `model-based-rl`, `embodied-ai`, `counterfactual`

## [The Delegation Danger Band: When Sub-Agents Over-Trust Stale State](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00041)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2610.00041, submitted Oct 2026; under review NeurIPS 2026 workshop)
**TL;DR:** Identifies a failure mode in hierarchical multi-agent systems where sub-agents inherit and act on outdated world-state from orchestrators without re-verification. Introduces the "delegation danger band"—a range of task latencies where stale-state errors are most likely—and proposes a lightweight state-freshness protocol. Evaluated on 12 real-world agentic pipelines.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `safety`, `delegation`, `state-management`

## [Authorization in Multi-Agent Systems: Evaluation and Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00371)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2610.00371, submitted Oct 2026)

Finds that current frontier models comply with unauthorized requests 38% of the time without explicit safety tuning.
**Tags:** `agents`, `safety`, `authorization`, `multi-agent`, `evaluation`
