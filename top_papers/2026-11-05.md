# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-11-05)

## [Rethinking Access Control for AI Agents: Recent Advances and Open Challenges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06764)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2611.06764)
**TL;DR:** A comprehensive survey of agent access control covering four key areas: policy specification, identity and delegation, action enforcement, and information flow control. The paper maps recent advances across all four dimensions and identifies open research challenges as autonomous agents gain broader deployment.
**Tags:** `agents`, `security`, `access-control`, `survey`

## [Multi-Agent Collusion and the J-Space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02153)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2611.02153)
**TL;DR:** Investigates whether colluding LLM agents leave detectable traces in their internal representations using interpretability experiments. Introduces J-PACT, an unsupervised detection method that identifies coordination signals in multi-agent systems without requiring labeled examples of collusion.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `interpretability`, `safety`, `collusion-detection`

## [MAS-Guard: Multi-Agent Guardrails for LLM Systems](https://github.com/jyyang621/DailyArXiv/issues/447)
**Authors:** (from DailyArXiv digest, Nov 4 2026)
**TL;DR:** Proposes a guardrail framework tailored for multi-agent LLM pipelines, addressing the challenge that single-agent safety filters miss cross-agent coordination attacks. MAS-Guard monitors inter-agent communication channels in addition to individual outputs.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `safety`, `guardrails`, `LLM`

## [Uncertainty-Aware Resource Orchestration for Agentic AI Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05392)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2611.05392)
**TL;DR:** Addresses how agentic AI systems should allocate compute and memory resources when task uncertainty is high. The paper introduces an orchestration layer that dynamically scales resource budgets based on confidence estimates, improving efficiency in long-horizon agent deployments.
**Tags:** `agents`, `resource-management`, `uncertainty`, `orchestration`

## [Who May Overrule the Agent?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05390)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2611.05390)
**TL;DR:** Examines the governance question of when a human reviewer or supervisory agent should have authority to override an upstream agent's decision. Formalizes conditions under which overrule authority is legitimate and proposes a delegation-aware override protocol.
**Tags:** `agents`, `governance`, `human-AI-collaboration`, `oversight`

---

## [Agentic Context Management for Multi-Turn Human-AI Conversations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00518)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2611.00518)
**TL;DR:** Proposes a context management strategy for agents engaged in extended multi-turn dialogues, dynamically compressing and retrieving conversation history to stay within context limits while preserving task-critical information. Evaluated on long-horizon customer service and coding benchmarks.
**Tags:** `agents`, `context-management`, `long-context`, `dialogue`

## [Challenge-Aware Knowledge Recruitment with Meta-Agent Supervision for Medical Multi-Agent Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05393)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2611.05393)
**TL;DR:** Introduces a meta-agent that dynamically recruits specialized knowledge modules based on the difficulty of incoming medical queries. The framework improves diagnostic accuracy in multi-agent medical reasoning by routing hard cases to domain-specific sub-agents under meta-supervision.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `medical-AI`, `reasoning`, `knowledge-routing`

**TL;DR:** Extends diffusion-based trajectory planning to multi-agent settings with safety constraints, ensuring collision-free coordination in dense environments. Accepted at AAMAS 2026, it demonstrates strong performance on multi-robot navigation benchmarks under partial observability.

## [Embodied AI for Scientific Discovery: From Physical Action to Scientific Evidence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04371)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2611.04371, submitted Nov 3 2026)
**TL;DR:** Frames robot learning and AI-driven experiment selection as a unified problem where experiments are partially observed and actions are costly and irreversible. The 46-page survey covers laboratory automation, instrument control, and hypothesis generation, connecting embodied robotics with scientific AI workflows.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `scientific-discovery`, `lab-automation`, `survey`
