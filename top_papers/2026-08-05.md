# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-08-05)

## [EmbodiedVAE: Disentangled Video VAE for Efficient and Controllable Embodied Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02990)
**Authors:** Jiayi Luo, Hanxin Zhu, Chen Gao, Jiankun Wang, Cong Wang, Tianyu He, Jianxin Li, Zhibo Chen (Beihang University, USTC, NUS, CASIA, Microsoft Research Asia)
**TL;DR:** Latent diffusion models for robotic manipulation suffer from VAEs optimized for natural scenes, yielding non-compact and non-controllable representations. EmbodiedVAE introduces a dual-encoder, single-decoder architecture with asymmetric spatio-temporal compression that automatically disentangles robot arm motion from background, achieving superior reconstruction quality with high compression rate and enabling more precise action control. Submitted August 4, 2026 — the most recent embodied AI paper at time of writing.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `VAE`, `manipulation`, `world-models`, `latent-diffusion`

## [Relational Priors as Convergence Pressure in LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03239)
**Authors:** Ming Shen et al.
**TL;DR:** This paper shows that inter-agent relation semantics (trust, challenge, deference) in LLM multi-agent systems act primarily as convergence pressure rather than accuracy boosters. Using a minimal signed-network formulation of relational priors injected into system prompts, tested across commons-governance simulation and multi-agent debate, the key finding is: relational positivity helps when utility rewards behavioral alignment (e.g., sustainable resource governance) but does not reliably improve factual accuracy. Submitted August 3–4, 2026.
**Tags:** `multi-agent`, `LLM`, `coordination`, `social-dynamics`, `agents`

## [Microscopic Dynamics of Consensus Formation in Multi-Agent LLM Naming Games](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02178)
**Authors:** Cristiano De Nobili, Vijayasri Iyer, Alessandro Codello, Raffaella Burioni
**TL;DR:** Decentralized populations of LLM agents can spontaneously reach consensus on shared conventions via a minimal LLM Naming Game, where each listener decision is a single-token LLM call at decoding temperature T. The paper decomposes each interaction into in-inventory and out-inventory channels with conditional rates π(T) and φ(T), whose balance governs ordering-disordering drift; mean-field theory yields an analytical ordering condition that generalizes the classical consensus threshold to a critical line in the (π, φ) plane. Submitted August 3, 2026.
**Tags:** `multi-agent`, `LLM`, `consensus`, `emergent-behavior`, `agents`, `theory`

## [HyperAgent: Planning and Acting over Tool-Schema Hypergraphs for Tool-Use LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02650)
**Authors:** Zian Zhai et al.
**TL;DR:** HyperAgent models tool relationships at the schema level by constructing a directed Tool-Schema Hypergraph, where tools are hyperedges connecting their required input-schema nodes to output-schema nodes. It extracts a task-relevant tool context graph to guide the construction of a schema-aware Task DAG for dynamic planning, then during execution builds a state-conditioned tool support graph through deficit-oriented expansion. Submitted July 31, 2026; outperforms prior tool-use agents on complex multi-step tasks.
**Tags:** `agents`, `tool-use`, `planning`, `LLM`, `hypergraph`

## [Qwen-UI-Agent: Toward Next-Generation Real-World Centric Foundation GUI Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28227)
**Authors:** Qwen Team (Alibaba)
**TL;DR:** Qwen-UI-Agent unifies mobile use, computer use, browser use, DeepSearch, and GUI grounding into a single foundation model without requiring apps to expose dedicated APIs. It achieves state-of-the-art performance across real-device and benchmark-based mobile/computer/browser use evaluations. Trending on HuggingFace; represents a significant step toward a universal OS-level agent for digital tasks.
**Tags:** `agents`, `GUI-agents`, `computer-use`, `mobile-use`, `browser-use`, `LLM`

## [ACE-Brain-0.5: A Unified Embodied Foundational Model for Physical Agentic AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04426)
**Authors:** ACE-Brain Team
**TL;DR:** ACE-Brain-0.5 addresses fragmentation in embodied AI by organizing robot intelligence into five coupled functions: spatial perception, decision making, embodied interaction, self-monitoring, and self-improvement, built on a shared spatial scaffold (ACE-Brain-0). The result is a closed-loop foundation model for Physical Agentic AI that learns unified representations across perception, planning, and action, rather than treating them as separate modules. Code available at GitHub.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `foundation-models`, `spatial-intelligence`, `agents`

## [EmbodiedGen V2: An Agentic, Simulation-Ready 3D World Engine for Embodied AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07459)
**Authors:** Xinjie Wang et al. (Horizon Robotics, WuwenAI)
**TL;DR:** EmbodiedGen V2 automates the full pipeline from generative 3D assets to RL-ready task environments, connecting cross-simulator assets, interaction affordances, and task-driven multi-room scenes into an agentic, reusable simulation pipeline. Results show 96.5% human acceptance, 98.6% collision success, and 83.3% of task-driven worlds directly usable without manual modification; online RL with generated environments improves sim success from 9.7% to 79.8%, with real-robot transfer from 21.7% to 75.0%.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `simulation`, `3D-generation`, `RL`, `robotics`, `world-models`

## [SentinelBench: A Benchmark for Long-Running Monitoring Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05342)
**Authors:** Microsoft Research
**TL;DR:** SentinelBench tests AI agents on 100 time-evolving monitoring tasks across 10 synthetic web environments (email, calendars, finance, professional networking, entertainment), where agents must detect external events and respond promptly. Key finding: GPT-5.4 with a `wait_for` tool solves 69/100 tasks at $0.48/task median cost, while using `sleep` solves only 56/100 at $4.65 — 9.7× more expensive for fewer successes. Establishes a rigorous standard for long-horizon agentic attention.
**Tags:** `agents`, `benchmarks`, `monitoring`, `long-horizon`, `LLM`, `evaluation`

## [Beyond the Leaderboard: A Synthesis of Tool-Use, Planning, and Reasoning Failures in LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05775)
**Authors:** arXiv:2607.05775 authors
**TL;DR:** This meta-analysis synthesizes 27 benchmark, taxonomy, and audit papers spanning 19 benchmarks from 2023–2026 into a cross-cutting taxonomy of agent failure modes across tool use, planning, long-horizon reasoning, multi-agent coordination, safety, and measurement validity. Finds that most public leaderboards mask systematic weaknesses in real-world deployment by rewarding narrow evaluation settings.
**Tags:** `agents`, `evaluation`, `survey`, `tool-use`, `planning`, `LLM`

## [MECoBench: A Systematic Study of Multimodal Agent Collaboration in Embodied Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31966)
**Authors:** arXiv:2606.31966 authors
**TL;DR:** MECoBench systematically studies how multimodal agents (combining vision, language, and action) collaborate in embodied environments, introducing tasks requiring agents to jointly solve problems where no single agent has sufficient sensing or manipulation capabilities. Results show that naive message-passing between agents often fails due to inconsistent world models; structured communication protocols with shared scene representations substantially improve collaborative success rates.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `multi-agent`, `multimodal`, `collaboration`, `robotics`, `benchmarks`

## [HAS-Bench: Evaluating LLM-Based Human-Agent Systems under Configurable Human Participation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04329)
**Authors:** arXiv:2607.04329 authors
**TL;DR:** HAS-Bench provides a configurable benchmark for human-agent collaboration where the level of human involvement (feedback frequency, task delegation depth) can be systematically varied, enabling study of how participation level changes task outcomes across diverse domains. Results reveal that agents are often oversensitive to feedback style and fail to leverage partial human guidance effectively. Provides both evaluation metrics and realistic simulations of human oversight patterns.
**Tags:** `agents`, `human-agent-collaboration`, `benchmarks`, `LLM`, `evaluation`
