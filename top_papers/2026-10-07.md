# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-10-07)

## [ResearchClawBench: A Benchmark for End-to-End Autonomous Scientific Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07591)
**Authors:** Koutian (et al.)
**TL;DR:** ResearchClawBench introduces 40 tasks across 10 scientific domains tied to real published papers, with the target paper hidden so agents must rediscover results from related literature and raw data. Seven autonomous research agents and 17 native LLMs were evaluated; the best agent (Qiushi Engine) reached 30.2% accuracy. Sets a rigorous new bar for measuring AI-driven scientific discovery end-to-end.
**Tags:** `agents`, `scientific-research`, `benchmarks`, `llm-evaluation`

## [POLAR: Risk Prevention for Tool-Calling LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08082)
**Authors:** (arXiv Oct 2026)
**TL;DR:** POLAR proposes a safety framework that intercepts risky tool calls in LLM agents before execution, categorizing potential harms and applying policy-guided prevention. The system is evaluated on a suite of real-world agentic tasks and substantially reduces harmful actions without significant performance degradation. Addresses a critical gap as tool-calling agents become production-deployed.
**Tags:** `agents`, `safety`, `tool-use`, `llm`, `risk-prevention`

---

## [DSV-Mem: Multimodal Memory Evaluation for MLLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08102)
**Authors:** (arXiv Oct 2026)
**TL;DR:** DSV-Mem presents a benchmark and evaluation framework for assessing multimodal memory in multi-modal LLM-based agents, covering both short- and long-term retention of visual and textual context. Experiments reveal significant gaps in current MLLM agents' ability to recall and reason over prior observations. Provides a structured path for improving persistent memory in embodied and dialogue agents.
**Tags:** `agents`, `memory`, `multimodal`, `mllm`, `benchmarks`

---

## [Research with AI Agents: How Agentic Systems Are Changing Scientific Work](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31219)
**Authors:** Johannes Lotz, Markus Wenzel
**TL;DR:** This paper asks when agentic AI systems deliver reliable efficiency gains in scientific research and which tasks should remain human-driven. It reviews current studies on literature search, data analysis, software development, and clinical decision support, finding consistent gains in automation-friendly tasks. Offers a decision framework for researchers considering adopting AI agents in their workflows.
**Tags:** `agents`, `scientific-research`, `human-ai-collaboration`, `literature-review`

## [AgensFlow: A Coordination-Policy Substrate for Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27466)
**Authors:** (arXiv May 2026)
**TL;DR:** AgensFlow frames multi-agent coordination as an online policy-learning problem under partial observability, providing a unified substrate where agents learn when and how to share information. The system outperforms prior coordination methods on collaborative task benchmarks and scales to dozens of agents. A practical architecture for production multi-agent deployments.
**Tags:** `multi-agent`, `coordination`, `reinforcement-learning`, `agents`

---

## [Efficient Vision-Language-Action Management and Serving for Robot Factories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12075)
**Authors:** (arXiv Sept 2026)
**TL;DR:** This paper addresses the unique inference serving requirements of Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models in industrial robot settings, proposing optimizations for multi-rate execution, latency-critical batch-1 inference, and non-token I/O interfaces. The system achieves significantly lower end-to-end latency than standard LLM serving stacks. Directly relevant to deploying embodied AI in manufacturing.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `vla`, `robotics`, `inference-serving`, `systems`

## [Inspect Robots: Open-Source Framework to Benchmark AI-Driven Robots](https://inspect-robots.org)
**Authors:** Jay Chooi, Robocurve (open-source, CoRL 2026 SPAIS Workshop)
**TL;DR:** Inspect Robots is an MIT-licensed evaluation framework for vision-language-action models that separates the policy (the model) from the embodiment (real robot or simulator), enabling reproducible cross-setup benchmarking. Submitted to the Science of Physical AI Safety (SPAIS) Workshop at CoRL 2026. Targets the gap between curated demo videos and real-world robot reliability and safety.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `evaluation`, `robotics`, `safety`, `benchmarks`, `vla`

## [EnvDreamer: Large-Scale Multimodal-to-Environment Generation for Embodied AI](https://arxiv.org/search/?searchtype=all&query=EnvDreamer+embodied+AI&start=0)
**Authors:** (arXiv Oct 2026)
**TL;DR:** EnvDreamer generates large-scale, diverse 3D environments from multimodal inputs (text, images, scene graphs) to serve as training grounds for embodied AI agents. The approach dramatically expands the variety of environments available for agent training without manual scene authoring. Addresses the data bottleneck for generalizable embodied policies.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `environment-generation`, `3d-worlds`, `data-augmentation`, `generative-ai`

## [rMuscle: Robotic Muscle Memory for Efficient Vision-Language-Action Model Inference](https://arxiv.org/search/?searchtype=all&query=rMuscle+robotic+muscle+memory+VLA&start=0)
**Authors:** (arXiv Sept 30, 2026)
**TL;DR:** rMuscle introduces a "muscle memory" caching mechanism for VLA models, allowing robots to reuse computed action primitives for frequently encountered sub-tasks, reducing inference overhead by up to 4×. The system maintains task performance while enabling real-time control on resource-constrained hardware. Practical step toward efficient on-robot VLA deployment.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `vla`, `robotics`, `inference-efficiency`, `caching`

## [Efficient Vision-Language-Action Models for Embodied Manipulation: A Systematic Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.17111)
**Authors:** (arXiv Oct 2026)
**TL;DR:** A systematic survey of methods for making VLA models computationally efficient for robotic manipulation, covering model compression, knowledge distillation, architecture design, and hardware co-optimization. Reviews over 100 papers and provides a taxonomy of efficiency strategies. Essential reference for deploying capable manipulation policies on real hardware.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `vla`, `manipulation`, `efficiency`, `survey`

## [Social-WM: Safety-Aware Latent World Models for Robot Social Navigation](https://arxiv.org/search/?searchtype=all&query=Social-WM+robot+social+navigation&start=0)
**Authors:** (arXiv Oct 2026)
**TL;DR:** Social-WM integrates safety constraints directly into latent world models for robot social navigation, enabling robots to predict and avoid unsafe interactions with humans in crowded environments. Demonstrated in simulation and on a real mobile robot platform. Advances safety-aware planning for human-robot coexistence.
