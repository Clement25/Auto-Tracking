# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-08-23)

## [Can AI Agents Conduct Open-Ended AI Research? Early Evidence from Two Case Studies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27191)
**Authors:** (Anonymous NeurIPS submission evaluation team)
**TL;DR:** Ran shadow evaluations on two unpublished NeurIPS 2026 submissions, giving frontier agents six days and thousands of dollars of compute. Agents completed all engineering tasks without human help but could not make substantial progress on the core research questions. Five recurring failure modes identified: poor judgment of publishable-research bar, uncreative responses to design shortcomings, ineffective backtracking, poor resource awareness, and instruction drift.
**Tags:** `agents`, `ai-research`, `evaluation`, `autonomy`, `llm-agents`

## [Embodied AI Agents: Modeling the World](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.22355)
**Authors:** Meta AI Research Team
**TL;DR:** Defines embodied AI agents as systems instantiated in visual, virtual, or physical form that learn and interact with users and their surroundings. The paper surveys the landscape of world-modeling approaches—from implicit latent models to explicit scene graphs—and argues that accurate world models are the central missing ingredient for robust embodied intelligence. Provides a unified framework covering embodied perception, interaction, and sim-to-real adaptation.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `world-models`, `robotics`, `perception`, `sim-to-real`

## [Safe Embodied AI for Long-horizon Tasks: A Cross-layer Analysis of Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05660)
**Authors:** (Preprint, June–August 2026)

## [A1: A Fully Transparent Open-Source Adaptive and Efficient Truncated Vision-Language-Action Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05672)
**Authors:** (Preprint, 2026)
**TL;DR:** Introduces A1, an open-source VLA model that uses truncated backpropagation through time to make training tractable on long robot episodes. Achieves state-of-the-art results on standard manipulation benchmarks while being 3× faster to train than baseline VLAs. Full weights, training code, and datasets released publicly.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `vla`, `robotics`, `open-source`, `manipulation`

## [Efficient Agentic Reasoning Through Self-Regulated Simulative Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22138)
**Authors:** (Preprint, 2026)
**TL;DR:** Presents a self-regulated planning framework where an agent internally simulates the consequences of candidate actions before committing to them, using a lightweight world model to prune unpromising branches. Reduces total inference cost by ~35% on multi-step reasoning benchmarks while maintaining accuracy, outperforming chain-of-thought and tree-of-thought baselines.
**Tags:** `agents`, `planning`, `reasoning`, `efficiency`, `llm-agents`

## [Forage V2: Knowledge Evolution and Transfer in Autonomous Agent Organizations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19837)
**Authors:** (Preprint, 2026)
**TL;DR:** Extends the Forage multi-agent framework with mechanisms for structured knowledge evolution—agents accumulate expertise and selectively transfer skills to new agents, reducing cold-start costs in large agent organizations. Shows that knowledge transfer cuts onboarding time for new sub-agents by 60% across coding, research, and data-analysis tasks.
**Tags:** `multi-agent`, `knowledge-transfer`, `agents`, `autonomous-systems`

## [A2RAG: Adaptive Agentic Graph Retrieval for Cost-Aware and Reliable Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/search/?searchtype=all&query=A2RAG+agentic+graph+retrieval)

## [Qwen-RobotSuite: Vision-Language-Action Models for Manipulation, World Modeling, and Navigation](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/06/16/meet-qwen-robotsuite-three-embodied-ai-models-for-vla-manipulation-video-world-modeling-and-navigation/)
**Authors:** Alibaba Qwen Team
**TL;DR:** Introduces three specialized embodied AI models built on the Qwen vision-language backbone: Qwen-RobotManip (VLA for dexterous manipulation), Qwen-RobotWorld (video-based world model for predicting environment dynamics), and Qwen-RobotNav (language-conditioned navigation). Together they form a modular suite that achieves new state-of-the-art results on RLBench, CARLA, and EmbodiedScan benchmarks.

## [AI Agents in Clinical Medicine: A Systematic Review](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.08.22.25334232.full.pdf)
**Authors:** (MedRxiv Preprint, 2025–2026)
**TL;DR:** Systematic review of 120+ studies deploying LLM-based agents in clinical workflows, covering diagnostic reasoning, treatment planning, patient communication, and clinical trial matching. Finds consistent accuracy gains over single-model baselines but significant gaps in robustness, explainability, and regulatory compliance that must be addressed before broad clinical adoption.
