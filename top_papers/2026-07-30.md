# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-07-30)

## [TurboVLA: Real-Time Vision-Language-Action Model at 32 Hz on an RTX 4090 with <1 GB VRAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27205)
**Authors:** Hengyi Xie, Chenfei Yao, Xianjin Wu, Xuanyang Xi, Yiping Tang, Di Xu, Yingying Zhu, Dingkang Liang, Xiang Bai, Han Ding
**TL;DR:** TurboVLA replaces the dominant LLM-centric V→L→A architecture with a direct V+L→A mapping that independently encodes vision and language then fuses them via lightweight bidirectional mechanisms. The result is 97.7% average task success with only 0.2B parameters, 31.2 ms inference latency, and 0.9 GB VRAM on a consumer RTX 4090 — enabling real-time robot control at 32 Hz without expensive hardware.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `VLA`, `robotics`, `efficiency`, `real-time`

## [Can AI agents conduct open-ended AI research? Early evidence from two case studies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27191)
**Authors:** David Africa, Stephan Rabanser, Andrew Schwartz, Sayash Kapoor, Peter Kirgis
**TL;DR:** Presents two in-depth case studies probing whether current LLM agents can autonomously carry out the full research loop — hypothesis generation, experimentation, analysis, and write-up — in AI research itself. Finds that agents can replicate constrained research tasks but struggle with genuine open-ended discovery, highlighting the gap between benchmark performance and true scientific autonomy.
**Tags:** `agents`, `ai-research`, `automation`, `evaluation`

## [OmegaUse-OfficeVal: Benchmarking LLM Agents on Long-Horizon Office-Suite Tasks with Economic Grounding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27155)
**Authors:** Zhenghai Chen, Jingjia Cao, Qi Bao, Yusai Zhao, Jingbo Zhou (Baidu Frontier Research)
**TL;DR:** OmegaUse-OfficeVal is a benchmark for evaluating LLM agents on multi-step, real-world office tasks (spreadsheets, docs, email, calendar) with economic utility as the grounding metric rather than binary pass/fail. It closes the gap between agent benchmark leaderboards and actual productivity value by grounding success in measurable economic impact.
**Tags:** `agents`, `benchmark`, `office-automation`, `llm`, `long-horizon`

## [TREK: A Travel Reasoning and Evaluation Kit for LLM Agents in Complex Trip Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26977)
**Authors:** Jinhu Qi, Wentao Zhang, Siu Man Ng, Feiyang Xu, Yanyu Chen, Yaoman Li, Irwin King
**TL;DR:** TREK benchmarks LLM agents on feasible itinerary synthesis: a single plan must simultaneously be constraint-correct, hallucination-free, spatio-temporally executable, and budget-valid. Existing benchmarks grade these properties one at a time with soft rubrics; TREK is the first to certify that a plan is truly executable end-to-end and uses auditable, reproducible scoring.
**Tags:** `agents`, `benchmark`, `planning`, `tool-use`, `llm`

## [HumanCLAW: Can Vision-Language Models Act Through a Body?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27180)
**Authors:** (authors listed in full paper)
**TL;DR:** Introduces the concept of "Action Intelligence" — the ability of a VLM to not just recognize space and objects but to physically navigate and manipulate through a body. HumanCLAW tests whether models that know where the sofa is can actually move their embodied form there and sit down, exposing a sharp gap between semantic knowledge and motor grounding.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `VLM`, `action-intelligence`, `robotics`, `grounding`

## [Data Pyramid for Embodied Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24744)
**Authors:** Yifan Ye, Yankai Fu, Yaoxu Lv, Bohan Hou, Jun Cen, Lingdong Kong, et al. (29 authors, NUS, HKUST, Tsinghua, and others)
**TL;DR:** Organizes the embodied data ecosystem as a five-level pyramid — real-robot, UMI-style, egocentric/exocentric, simulation, and general VL data — ranked by the scalability vs. robot-alignment tradeoff. Identifies six open challenges including large-scale tactile datasets, failure/recovery data collection, cross-embodiment action alignment, and principled data recipes for policy learning.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `data`, `robotics`, `survey`, `manipulation`

## [ASPIRE: Agentic Skills Discovery for Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00272)
**Authors:** Runyu Lu, Yubo Wu, Ethan Kou, Letian Fu, Wenli Xiao, Ajay Mandlekar, Yinzhen Xu, Guanya Shi, Ken Goldberg, Ang Chen, Mosharaf Chowdhury, Yuke Zhu, Linxi "Jim" Fan, Guanzhi Wang (NVIDIA, UC Berkeley, CMU, UIUC, U Michigan)
**TL;DR:** ASPIRE is a continual learning system that autonomously writes and refines robot control programs (code-as-policy) while compounding reusable skills into a growing library. It transfers skills across tasks, sim-to-real, and across robot embodiments, enabling robots to accumulate procedural knowledge without human annotation of each new task.
**Tags:** `robotics`, `embodied-ai`, `skill-learning`, `code-as-policy`, `continual-learning`, `agents`

## [EmbodiedGen V2: An Agentic, Simulation-Ready 3D World Engine for Embodied AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07459)
**Authors:** Xinjie Wang, Liu Liu, Taojun Ding, Andrew Choi, Chaodong Huang, Mengao Zhao, Ziang Li, Jackson Jiang, Chunlei Yu, Shengxiang Liu, Wei Xu, Zhizhong Su (Horizon Robotics)
**TL;DR:** EmbodiedGen V2 generates sim-ready, policy-ready 3D task environments from natural-language descriptions, eliminating the manual assembly bottleneck that has blocked scalable robot training. Generated environments cover manipulation, navigation, and mobile manipulation across simulators; online RL with generated worlds improves sim success from 9.7% to 79.8% and transfers to real robots with task success rising from 21.7% to 75.0%.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `simulation`, `robotics`, `world-generation`, `RL`, `data-generation`

## [Cortex: A Bidirectionally Aligned Embodied Agent Framework for Long-horizon Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05377)
**Authors:** Jiaqi Peng, Xiqian Yu, Delin Feng, Yuqiang Yang, Wenzhe Cai, Jing Xiong, Ganlin Yang, Jinliang Zheng, Jiafei Cao, Xueyuan Wei, Jiangmiao Pang, Yuan Shen, Tai Wang
**TL;DR:** Cortex addresses the short-sightedness bottleneck of monolithic VLA models by adding bidirectional alignment between a high-level VLM planner and a low-level VLA executor. A customized planning interface conveys executable, tractable sub-goals downward while execution feedback propagates upward, enabling recovery from compounding errors on long-horizon tasks.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `VLA`, `robotics`, `planning`, `long-horizon`, `hierarchical`

## [Multi-Agent LLMs Fail to Explore Each Other](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11250)
**Authors:** Hyeong Kyu Choi, Jiatong Li, Wendi Li, Xin Eric Wang, Sharon Li
**TL;DR:** Formalizes the Multi-Agent Exploration problem as a partially observable stochastic game (POSG) and shows that current LLM agents exhibit myopic, polarized interaction patterns that cause suboptimal coordination. Introduces MACE (Multi-Agent Contextual Exploration), a lightweight framework with structured peer-selection that substantially improves exploration and downstream task performance.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `exploration`, `coordination`, `llm`

## [Security and Privacy in Agentic AI: Grand Challenges and Future Directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06608)
**Authors:** Adam Jenkins, Agnieszka Kitkowska, Caterina Maidhof, Diego Paracuellos, Francesco Sovrano, and 20 others (30 experts from academia, industry, government)
**TL;DR:** Based on a horizon-scanning exercise with 30 international experts, this paper maps the emerging security and privacy threat landscape for agentic AI — systems that act autonomously, chain tools, and accumulate privileges. Identifies grand challenges around agent identity, audit trails, prompt injection at scale, inter-agent trust, and the erosion of meaningful human oversight as agent autonomy increases.
**Tags:** `agents`, `security`, `privacy`, `safety`, `agentic-ai`

## [Grounded World Models in Biological Organisms and Future Embodied AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13560)
**Authors:** Giovanni Pezzulo and 5 co-authors
**TL;DR:** Argues that current AI world models, driven by passive large-scale training, attach information from modalities onto a linguistic scaffold — the inverse of biological intelligence, where a grounded model built from physical interaction provides the scaffold to which language attaches. Illustrates five neural circuits for grounded world modelling (navigation, affordance perception, active sensing, allostatic control, emotion) as design inspiration for more robustly embodied AI systems.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `world-models`, `neuroscience`, `cognitive-science`, `grounding`
