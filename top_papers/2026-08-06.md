# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-08-06)

## [LongHorizon-Harness: Advancing Long-Horizon Agents for Real-World Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01964)
**Authors:** Ziyu Ma et al. (8 co-authors)
**TL;DR:** Proposes a Manage-Execute-Audit (MEA) loop that reformulates long-horizon agent execution as a task-state management problem, maintaining verified state outside the execution context. Achieves large gains on WeaveBench (51.8%→80.7%), Terminal-Bench 2.1, and OSWorld 2.0 across multiple models including Claude Opus 4.7.
**Tags:** `agents`, `long-horizon`, `evaluation`, `task-state-management`

---

## [HyperAgent: Planning and Acting over Tool-Schema Hypergraphs for Tool-Use LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02650)
**Authors:** Zian Zhai, Xingyu Tan, Gaowang Zou, Xiaoyang Wang, Wenjie Zhang
**TL;DR:** Addresses unreliable tool-use planning in LLM agents by modeling tool relations at the schema level via a directed Tool-Schema Hypergraph, where tools are hyperedges from required input-schema nodes to output-schema nodes. HyperAgent's graph-guided dynamic planning reduces inefficient exploration and improves execution reliability on complex tasks.
**Tags:** `agents`, `tool-use`, `planning`, `hypergraph`

## [S³: Improving Agent Safety through Multi-Stage Defense](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02683)
**Authors:** Zibo Xiao, Haoyu Wang, Jun Sun
**TL;DR:** Introduces Stage-Specific Safety Skills (S³), a unified abstraction representing heterogeneous safety designs as reusable, composable components with explicit stage semantics (memory, planning, tool execution). An automated pipeline converts existing safety designs into reusable safety skills, and a guard agent orchestrates them for comprehensive risk detection and mitigation across the full agentic workflow.
**Tags:** `agents`, `safety`, `multi-stage-defense`, `composable-safety`

## [ContinualSkillBench: Can LLM Agents Truly Evolve Their Capabilities?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03874)
**Authors:** Tianyi Guan, Yiding Wang, Haotong Yang, Siyuan Cao, Shirui Liu, Yi Hu, Jiaqi Li, Muhan Zhang (Peking University, BIGAI)
**TL;DR:** A dynamic evaluation framework for in-context continual skill learning covering five domains, each with 100 interconnected subtasks of increasing difficulty. Key finding: in-context learning performs comparably to explicit skill maintenance on average, suggesting gains come from contextual adaptation rather than reusable skill abstraction alone.
**Tags:** `agents`, `evaluation`, `continual-learning`, `skill-libraries`

---

## [ABSeeker: Training Long-Horizon Search Agents via Answer-Backtracked Credit Assignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05102)
**Authors:** Yijun Lu, Rui Ye, Jiajun Wang, Yuwen Du, Tian Jin, Songhua Liu, Siheng Chen
**TL;DR:** Proposes Answer-Backtracked Credit Assignment (ABC), converting sparse trajectory-level outcomes into dense step-level supervision by tracing back from answers to recover intermediate clues. ABSeeker achieves 37.3% on BrowseComp and 39.1% on BrowseComp-ZH, improving to 55.3%/52.9% with context management, matching much larger ~30B parameter agents at only 4B scale.
**Tags:** `agents`, `search`, `reinforcement-learning`, `credit-assignment`

## [Hierarchical Graph Memory for LLM Agents with Path-level Localization and Rewrite](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05095)
**Authors:** Xiawei Yue, Boran Wang, Xiaoqing Zhang, Shuxin Zheng, Ziwei Zhang
**TL;DR:** Proposes a hierarchical graph-based memory structure for LLM agents with novel path-level localization and rewrite mechanisms. The approach enables agents to efficiently retrieve and update relevant knowledge within structured graph memories, improving performance on complex long-horizon tasks.
**Tags:** `agents`, `memory`, `graph`, `knowledge-retrieval`

## [BANDMAS: Causality-Inspired Semantic Packet Scheduling for Bandwidth-Efficient Multi-Agent Collaboration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00458)
**Authors:** Jiangwen Dong, Wanyu Lin
**TL;DR:** Addresses the communication bottleneck in LLM-based multi-agent systems where agents exchange messages across specializations. Proposes causality-inspired semantic packet scheduling to efficiently aggregate and transmit only semantically relevant information between agents, reducing bandwidth while preserving collaboration quality.
**Tags:** `multi-agent`, `communication`, `efficiency`, `semantic-scheduling`

## [Human Centric Embodied Intelligence for Soft Wearable Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03556)
**Authors:** Rainier Natividad, Raye Chen-Hua Yeow
**TL;DR:** A comprehensive review of how embodied AI and physical intelligence are being integrated into soft wearable robotic systems designed for human augmentation and rehabilitation. Surveys approaches to perception, intent recognition, and adaptive control for robots in direct physical contact with humans, highlighting key challenges in safety, compliance, and personalization.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `wearable-robotics`, `human-robot-interaction`, `soft-robotics`

## [Safe Embodied AI for Long-horizon Tasks: A Cross-layer Analysis of Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05660)
**Authors:** (Multiple authors)
**TL;DR:** Analyzes safety challenges across system layers—perception, planning, and actuation—for embodied AI agents performing long-horizon robotic manipulation. Proposes a cross-layer framework for detecting and mitigating failures that propagate across execution steps, with focus on contact-rich manipulation scenarios where errors compound over time.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `safety`, `manipulation`, `long-horizon`

## [Towards Embodied Agentic AI: Review and Classification of LLM- and VLM-Driven Robot Autonomy and Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05294)
**Authors:** Sahar Salimpour, Lei Fu, Kajetan Rachwał, Pascal Bertrand, Kevin O'Sullivan, Robert Jakob, Farhad Keramat, Leonardo Militano, Giovanni Toffetti, Harry Edelman, Jorge Peña Queralta
**TL;DR:** Comprehensive survey reviewing how LLMs and VLMs have enabled novel robot autonomy paradigms—from early GPT-style interfaces to complex agentic architectures where AI acts as coordinator, planner, or generalist controller. Reviews vision-language-action (VLA) models and large behavior models (LBMs), covering community tools, ROS packages, and industrial frameworks emerging from this fast-evolving space.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `survey`, `VLM`, `VLA`, `robot-autonomy`, `human-robot-interaction`
