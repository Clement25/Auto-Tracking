# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-08-30)

## [ComBodied Agents: a New Paradigm of Human-Centric Agentic AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10915)
**Authors:** Qianggang Ding, Xingyao Wang, Rui Feng, Zhibin Wang, Feixiang Yao, Kelong Mao, Hao Sun, Zhiyao Luo, Jiankai Tang, Lei Li, Jiadong Guo, Minheng Ni, Weicong Lin, Chenxi Yang, Hongxiang Gao, Zhenghua Chen, Yang Bai, Min Wu, Jun Cheng, Huazhu Fu, Dacheng Tao, Bang Liu
**TL;DR:** Proposes "ComBodied Agents," a human-centered paradigm that models, predicts, and supports individual human-state trajectories over time—bridging the gap between digital agents (software state) and embodied agents (physical state) to address why a person may miss medication, not just that they did. Uses software tools, sensors, wearables, robots, and human services as action channels rather than end goals, putting the person's evolving state at the center.
**Tags:** `agents`, `embodied-ai`, `human-centric`, `healthcare-ai`

## [Towards the Harness of Embodied Agents (Thea)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11246)
**Authors:** Qi Wang, Tianyi Wang, Chengyang Li, Shikun Ban, Yurun Chen, Yizhong Ge, Jason Qin, Chengtai Li, Wentao Zhu
**TL;DR:** Introduces Thea, an embodied agent harness that wraps robot capabilities as callable tools within an agentic loop, directly extending the coding-agent harness paradigm to the physical world. Key innovations include Scene Graph as Context (replacing text logs with structured scene representations) and Evaluation as Exit Codes (enabling environment-grounded stopping conditions), effectively closing the loop between the agent and physical reality.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `agent-harness`, `scene-graph`

## [Self-Evolving Embodied Agents via Skill-Harness Evolution (SHAPER)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11350)
**Authors:** Peidong Wang, Zhiming Ma, Ying Chang, Xufang Luo, Xiaocui Yang, Shi Feng, Yuqing Yang, Dongsheng Li
**TL;DR:** Presents SHAPER, a train-free framework for embodied adaptation that keeps foundation model weights frozen and instead evolves reusable skills and a context-code harness through environment rollouts. Performance improvement comes entirely from non-parametric system evolution—skills and execution infrastructure—rather than fine-tuning, enabling rapid adaptation to new environments without retraining costs.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `self-evolving`, `robotics`, `train-free`, `agents`

## [Neurosymbolic Embodied Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16794)
**Authors:** Mohammad Albinhassan (Imperial College London), Yuming Feng (Johns Hopkins University), Alessandra Russo (Imperial College London), Pranava Madhyastha (City, University of London)
**TL;DR:** Addresses the key failure mode of LLM-based planners—generating plausible but non-executable plans that violate environment dynamics. The system decomposes long-horizon household tasks into two phases: vision-language exploration to acquire goal-relevant predicates, followed by constrained symbolic planning with PDDL that uses Monte Carlo tree search to evaluate only executable action continuations, guaranteeing executability under the transition model.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `neurosymbolic`, `planning`, `PDDL`, `MCTS`, `household-ai`

## [Towards Embodied Agentic AI: Review and Classification of LLM- and VLM-Driven Robot Autonomy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05294)
**Authors:** (Multiple authors)
**TL;DR:** A comprehensive survey that reviews and classifies emerging design patterns in which AI agents interface with robot control software, libraries, and middleware. ## [Safe Embodied AI for Long-horizon Tasks: A Cross-layer Analysis of Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05660)
**Authors:** (Multiple authors)
**TL;DR:** Conducts a cross-layer safety analysis of embodied AI systems performing long-horizon manipulation tasks, identifying failure modes that span the perception, planning, and execution layers simultaneously. Proposes safety constraints and evaluation methodology that accounts for cumulative risk across task horizons rather than treating each subtask independently.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `safety`, `robotics`, `manipulation`, `long-horizon`

## [Risk Analysis Techniques for Governed LLM-based Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05687)

## [Adversarial Attacks in Multi-Agent LLM Pipelines: Unveiling Structural Vulnerabilities](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.05687)
**Authors:** (Multiple authors, accepted at IEEE GLOBECOM 2026)
**TL;DR:** Reveals structural vulnerabilities in multi-agent LLM architectures that can be exploited through adversarial inputs, demonstrating that attack surface expands non-linearly with the number of coordinating agents. Categorizes attack vectors including prompt injection across agent boundaries, trust hijacking, and coordination manipulation, with empirical results on several production-style multi-agent frameworks.
**Tags:** `multi-agent`, `adversarial-attacks`, `security`, `LLM`, `agents`

---

## [ROMA: Recursive Open Meta-Agent Framework for Long-Horizon Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.18096)
**Authors:** (Multiple authors)
**TL;DR:** Proposes ROMA, a recursive meta-agent framework that decomposes long-horizon tasks into subtask trees executed in parallel across multiple agents, avoiding context-window limitations while maintaining coherent task-level goals. The recursive structure allows each sub-agent to itself spawn nested agents for its subtasks, enabling unlimited effective task horizon without architectural changes.
**Tags:** `multi-agent`, `LLM`, `long-horizon`, `task-decomposition`, `agents`

## [StellaVLA: State-of-the-Art Vision-Language-Action Model](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.RO/recent)
**Authors:** (Multiple authors)
**TL;DR:** StellaVLA ranks #1 on the VLA-Arena leaderboard (as of August 1, 2026) with an overall score of 0.63, achieving 98.8% success rate on LIBERO and 85.1% on LIBERO-Plus benchmarks. Its key innovation is supporting both human and robot demonstrations as well as cross-embodiment (XR) demonstrations as in-context structured examples, enabling effective adaptation to out-of-distribution tasks without fine-tuning.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `VLA`, `robotics`, `vision-language`, `in-context-learning`
