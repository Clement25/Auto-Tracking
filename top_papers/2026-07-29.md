# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-07-29)

## [HAS-Bench: Evaluating LLM-Based Human-Agent Systems under Configurable Human Participation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04329)
**Authors:** Anonymous / Under Review
**TL;DR:** Introduces HAS-Bench, a benchmark for evaluating LLM-based human-agent systems under varying levels of human participation, examining how human agency affects task success, outcome quality, safety, and failure recovery. Evaluates backbone LLMs including GPT-4.1, Claude-Sonnet-4, and DeepSeek-V3-0324 across diverse problem patterns.
**Tags:** `agents`, `benchmarking`, `human-ai-interaction`, `evaluation`

## [OrchestrXR: A Multi-Agent System for Idea-to-Prototype XR Study Authoring](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01588)
**Authors:** Shuqi Liao, Chenfei Zhu, Karthik Ramani, Voicu Popescu
**TL;DR:** OrchestrXR is a multi-agent human-AI workflow for early-stage extended reality (XR) study authoring, spanning study design, 3D scene generation, and interaction logic generation with strong intent preservation. A user study with 12 XR researchers confirms effective support for the idea-to-prototype pipeline.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `XR`, `human-computer-interaction`

## [Embodied.cpp: A Portable Inference Runtime of Embodied AI Models on Heterogeneous Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02501)
**Authors:** (ACM/ECCV 2026 submission authors)
**TL;DR:** Proposes Embodied.cpp, a portable runtime targeting real-world deployment of vision-language-action (VLA) models and world-action models (WAMs) on heterogeneous robot hardware, supporting multi-rate execution in closed-loop control and latency-first batch-1 inference. Addresses the gap between existing ML runtimes and the strict requirements of embodied deployment.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `inference`, `deployment`, `VLA`

## [ACE-Brain-0.5: A Unified Embodied Foundational Model for Physical Agentic AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04426)
**Authors:** ACE-Brain Team (Ziyang Gong, Haoming Gu, Zehang Luo, Tianyi Zhang, et al.)
**TL;DR:** ACE-Brain-0.5 organizes robot intelligence into five coupled functions — spatial perception, decision making, embodied interaction, self-monitoring, and self-improvement — building on the spatial-intelligence scaffold of ACE-Brain-0. Extends an understanding-centric model into a closed-loop foundation model capable of autonomous long-horizon task execution.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `foundation-models`, `robotics`, `physical-ai`

---

## [Embodied-R1.5: Evolving Physical Intelligence via Embodied Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11324)
**Authors:** (Embodied-R Team)
**TL;DR:** Presents a unified Embodied Foundation Model (EFM) with 8B parameters achieving SOTA on 16 out of 24 embodied VLM benchmarks, surpassing Gemini-Robotics-ER-1.5 and GPT-5.4, using a Planner-Grounder-Corrector (PGC) closed-loop framework for self-correcting long-horizon task execution. Built on 15B+ tokens of automated training data with multi-task balanced RL to reduce task conflicts.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `foundation-models`, `VLA`, `robotics`, `reinforcement-learning`

## [Distributed AI Agents for Cognitive Underwater Robot Autonomy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.23735)
**Authors:** Markus Buchholz, Ignacio Carlucho, Michele Grimaldi, Yvan R. Petillot
**TL;DR:** Introduces UROSA (Underwater Robot Self-Organizing Autonomy), a ROS 2 architecture leveraging distributed LLM agents for advanced cognitive capabilities in autonomous underwater vehicles, featuring retrieval-augmented generation via vector databases and reinforcement learning-driven behavioral optimization. Validated in realistic simulation and real-world underwater deployments, outperforming traditional rule-based systems on unforeseen scenarios.
**Tags:** `agents`, `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `underwater`, `multi-agent`

## [Vision Language Action Models in Robotic Manipulation: A Systematic Review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.10672)
**Authors:** (Systematic review team)
**TL;DR:** Comprehensively analyzes 102 VLA models, 26 foundational datasets, and 12 simulation platforms shaping embodied robotic manipulation, mapping the rapid evolution from early vision-language pre-training to real-world deployable action models. Provides a structured taxonomy and identifies open challenges in generalization, data efficiency, and safety.
**Tags:** `VLA`, `robotics`, `embodied-ai`, `survey`, `manipulation`

## [Evaluating Memory in LLM Agents via Incremental Multi-Turn Interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.05257)
**Authors:** (Anonymous / Revised June 2026)
**TL;DR:** Proposes a benchmark for evaluating LLM agent memory through four core competencies: accurate retrieval, test-time learning, long-range understanding, and selective forgetting, tested via incremental multi-turn interaction sequences. Reveals significant gaps in current LLMs' ability to manage long-context episodic memory across extended agent tasks.
**Tags:** `agents`, `memory`, `benchmarking`, `LLM`, `evaluation`

## [Safe Embodied AI for Long-horizon Tasks: A Cross-layer Analysis of Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.05660)
**Authors:** (Safety-in-robotics research team)
**TL;DR:** Conducts a cross-layer safety analysis of embodied AI systems performing long-horizon manipulation tasks, examining failure modes at the perception, planning, and actuation layers. Proposes mitigation strategies that improve task completion rates while maintaining safety constraints across multi-step robot workflows.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `safety`, `robotics`, `manipulation`, `long-horizon`

---

## [A Survey on Autonomy-Induced Security Risks in Large Model-Based Agents](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.23844)
**Authors:** (AI security research team)
**TL;DR:** Surveys the security risks that emerge as large-model agents gain greater autonomy, including prompt injection, goal hijacking, credential theft, and lateral movement across services, as illustrated by real-world incidents including the July 2026 Hugging Face agentic breach. Proposes a taxonomy of autonomy-induced risks and reviews existing and proposed defenses.
**Tags:** `agents`, `security`, `safety`, `survey`, `autonomous-ai`
