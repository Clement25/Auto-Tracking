# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-08-01)

## [Multi-Agent LLMs Fail to Explore Each Other](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11250)
**Authors:** (Multiple — arxiv:2607.11250, submitted Jul 13, 2026)
**TL;DR:** This paper demonstrates that modern LLM agents fail to explore effectively when interacting with one another, exhibiting myopic and polarized interaction patterns that lead to suboptimal coordination and increased regret. The authors introduce Multi-Agent Contextual Exploration (MACE), a lightweight framework that promotes structured peer selection, substantially improving both exploration behavior and downstream task performance.
**Tags:** `multi-agent`, `LLM`, `coordination`, `exploration`, `agents`

---

## [Agent Planning Benchmark (APB): A Diagnostic Framework for Planning Capabilities in LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04874)
**Authors:** (Multiple — arxiv:2606.04874, submitted Jun 2026)
**TL;DR:** APB is a planning-specific diagnostic benchmark containing 4,209 multimodal cases across 22 domains and five evaluation settings, covering holistic planning, feedback-conditioned step-wise planning, and robustness under extraneous tools, broken tools, and unsolvable tasks. Evaluation across 12 MLLMs reveals systematic weaknesses in long-horizon planning, tool-noise robustness, calibrated refusal, and inference-time refinement. The benchmark provides a standardized framework for identifying and comparing agent planning deficiencies.
**Tags:** `agents`, `planning`, `benchmark`, `LLM`, `evaluation`

## [Dynamic Harness Adaptation for LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.MA/recent)
**Authors:** (Multiple — submitted Jul 29, 2026)
**TL;DR:** This paper addresses a critical gap in LLM agent deployment: agents may recover from failures within an episode, yet the same execution failure recurs in later tasks because post-episode feedback rarely revises the persistent harness that guides future interactions. The work proposes dynamic harness adaptation mechanisms that allow agents to learn persistently from failure signals across task boundaries, reducing recurring error patterns.
**Tags:** `agents`, `LLM`, `adaptation`, `memory`, `failure-recovery`

---

## [Aligning Agents via Planning: A Benchmark for Trajectory-Level Reward Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08178)
**Authors:** (Multiple — arxiv:2604.08178, submitted Apr 2026)
**TL;DR:** Plan-RewardBench is a trajectory-level preference benchmark designed to evaluate how well reward models distinguish preferred versus distractor agent trajectories in complex tool-using scenarios. The benchmark targets the alignment challenge of training agents that plan well across multi-step tool use, filling a gap left by token-level or outcome-only reward modeling approaches.
**Tags:** `agents`, `planning`, `reward-modeling`, `alignment`, `benchmark`

## [Self-evolving Embodied AI](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.04411)
**Authors:** Tongtong Feng, Xin Wang, Wenwu Zhu (arxiv:2602.04411, Feb 2026)
**TL;DR:** This paper presents a framework for embodied agents that can autonomously improve themselves through continuous interaction with the physical world, without requiring repeated human supervision or re-training. The approach enables robots and embodied agents to accumulate experience and refine their policies over time, moving toward open-ended skill acquisition.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `self-evolution`, `robotics`, `continual-learning`

## [Hy-embodied-0.5: Embodied Foundation Models for Real-World Agents](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.07430)
**Authors:** (Multiple — arxiv:2604.07430, Apr 2026)
**TL;DR:** This paper introduces Hy-embodied-0.5, a compact yet capable embodied foundation model designed for deployment on real-world robotic agents with limited compute. The model combines vision-language understanding with action generation, demonstrating strong performance on manipulation and navigation benchmarks while remaining deployable on edge hardware.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `foundation-models`, `robotics`, `vision-language-action`

## [Mem: Multi-Scale Embodied Memory for Vision Language Action Models](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.03596)
**Authors:** (Multiple — arxiv:2603.03596, Mar 2026)
**TL;DR:** Mem proposes a multi-scale memory architecture for Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models that enables embodied agents to retain and retrieve relevant experiences across short, medium, and long time horizons. The approach significantly improves task success rates on long-horizon manipulation tasks by allowing agents to leverage hierarchically structured episodic memory during planning and execution.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `VLA`, `memory`, `robotics`, `vision-language-action`

## [Foundation Models in Robotics: A Comprehensive Review of Methods, Models, Datasets, Challenges and Future Research Directions](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.15395v1)
**Authors:** (Multiple — arxiv:2604.15395, Apr 2026)

## [World Model for Robot Learning: A Comprehensive Survey](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.00080v1)
**Authors:** (Multiple — arxiv:2605.00080, May 2026)
**TL;DR:** This survey systematically examines world models as an emerging paradigm for robot learning, covering architectures, training objectives, and applications in model-based reinforcement learning and planning. The paper analyzes how world models enable robots to internally simulate future states, enabling more sample-efficient and generalizable skill acquisition compared to model-free approaches.

## [Reinforcement Learning for LLM-based Multi-Agent Systems through Orchestration Traces](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.02801v1)
**Authors:** (Multiple — arxiv:2605.02801, May 2026)
**TL;DR:** This work proposes training LLM-based multi-agent systems using reinforcement learning over orchestration traces — structured records of inter-agent communication and tool use — rather than individual agent outputs. The method enables end-to-end optimization of multi-agent pipelines and demonstrates improved performance on complex software engineering and research tasks.
**Tags:** `multi-agent`, `LLM`, `reinforcement-learning`, `orchestration`, `agents`
