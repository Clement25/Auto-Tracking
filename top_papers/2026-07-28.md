# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-07-28)

## [StateAct: Program State, before Pixels, for Long-Horizon Computer-Use Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22798)
**Authors:** Yan Yang, Xiangru Jian, Ziyang Luo, Zirui Zhao, Yutong Dai, Ziji Shi, Hanshu Yan, Jun Hao Liew, Silvio Savarese, Junnan Li
**TL;DR:** Achieves new SOTA on OSWorld 2.0 by shifting computer-use agents away from pixel-level observations toward program state as the primary grounding signal. The insight is that structured state (DOM trees, accessibility APIs, runtime introspection) is far more reliable for long-horizon planning than screenshots, reducing perceptual errors in multi-step desktop tasks.
**Tags:** `agents`, `computer-use`, `long-horizon`, `SOTA`

## [Data Pyramid for Embodied Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24744)
**Authors:** Yifan Ye, Yankai Fu, Yaoxu Lv, Bohan Hou, Jun Cen, Lingdong Kong, et al. (28 authors)
**TL;DR:** Organizes the embodied manipulation data ecosystem as a five-layer "pyramid" spanning real-robot, UMI-style, egocentric/exocentric, simulation, and general VL data. Identifies six open challenges including scalable tactile datasets, failure/recovery data collection, and cross-embodiment action alignment. Motivated by the gap between internet-scale LLM training and the physically-coupled data needed for robot learning.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `data`, `manipulation`, `survey`

## [Molt: A Scalable PyTorch-Native Training Framework for Agentic Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21653)
**Authors:** Jian Hu, Huiying Li, Hao Zhang, Binfeng Xu, Yifan Zhang, Shaokun Zhang, Hemil Desai, Michael Demoret, Pavlo Molchanov, Jan Kautz, Yi Dong (NVIDIA)
**TL;DR:** A compact, PyTorch-native RL training framework for agentic policies, designed so a single researcher (or AI coding assistant) can reason about the entire codebase. Uses an asynchronous loop that avoids training on tokens the agent didn't generate, maintaining policy-version consistency across multimodal and MoE models. Statistically comparable to a Megatron-based stack at much lower iteration cost.
**Tags:** `agents`, `reinforcement-learning`, `training`, `framework`, `NVIDIA`

## [EmbodiedGen V2: An Agentic, Simulation-Ready 3D World Engine for Embodied AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07459)
**Authors:** Xinjie Wang, Liu Liu, Taojun Ding, Andrew Choi, Chaodong Huang, Mengao Zhao, et al.
**TL;DR:** A generative 3D engine that converts free-form descriptions into executable simulation environments for embodied agents, covering manipulation, navigation, and mobile manipulation. Key result: online RL with generated environments lifts simulation success from 9.7% → 79.8%, and the policies transfer to real robots with task success improving from 21.7% → 75.0%.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `simulation`, `world-model`, `robotics`, `3D-generation`

## [Multi-Agent LLMs Fail to Explore Each Other](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11250)
**Authors:** Hyeong Kyu Choi, Jiatong Li, Wendi Li, Xin Eric Wang, Sharon Li
**TL;DR:** Demonstrates that LLM agents systematically fail to explore the capabilities of peer agents they interact with, leading to underperformance in collaborative tasks. Introduces Multi-Agent Contextual Exploration (MACE), a structured peer-selection framework that improves exploration behavior and downstream task performance in multi-agent settings.
**Tags:** `multi-agent`, `exploration`, `LLM-agents`, `collaboration`

## [Beyond the Leaderboard: Tool-Use, Planning, and Reasoning Failures in LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05775)
**Authors:** Wael Albayaydh, Rui Zhao, Ivan Flechais
**TL;DR:** Synthesizes 27 papers (2023–2026) spanning 19 benchmarks into a cross-cutting failure taxonomy for LLM agents. Identifies six failure clusters: tool invocation/parameter errors, planning/constraint failures, long-horizon degradation from context accumulation, multi-agent coordination failures, safety/security failures, and measurement validity issues. Argues that leaderboard scores mask systematic failure modes that matter for deployment.
**Tags:** `agents`, `survey`, `failure-analysis`, `tool-use`, `planning`

## [Long-Horizon-Terminal-Bench: Testing Agents on Terminal Tasks with Dense Reward Grading](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08964)
**Authors:** Zongxia Li, Zhongzhi Li, Yucheng Shi, Ruhan Wang, Junyao Yang, Zhichao Liu, et al.
**TL;DR:** A 46-task terminal benchmark across 9 categories (experiment reproduction, software engineering, multimodal analysis, interactive games, scientific computing). Each task is decomposed into graded subtasks enabling dense intermediate rewards and partial credit, revealing where agents stall on open-ended, long-horizon workflows instead of giving binary pass/fail scores.
**Tags:** `agents`, `benchmark`, `long-horizon`, `terminal`, `evaluation`

## [NavVerse: Benchmarking Indoor-to-Outdoor Embodied Navigation in Continuous Simulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19695)
**Authors:** NavVerse team
**TL;DR:** A physics-enabled benchmark with 100 indoor scenes, 50 urban outdoor scenes, and 50 indoor-to-outdoor transition scenes (10,000 episodes total) for Object Navigation, Vision-and-Language Navigation, and Place Navigation. Targets the gap left by prior benchmarks that evaluate only in controlled indoor environments, testing agent robustness across realistic scene transitions.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `navigation`, `benchmark`, `simulation`, `VLN`

## [Skill Self-Play: Pushing the Frontier of LLM Capability with Co-Evolving Skills](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22529)
**Authors:** Siyuan Huang, Pengyu Cheng, Haotian Liu, Tao Chen, Yihao Liu, Jingwei Ni, et al.
**TL;DR:** Addresses the diversity-vs-verifiability dilemma in LLM self-evolution: broad tasks are hard to verify, narrow tasks lack coverage. Skill Self-Play uses agent "skills" as a middle ground — each skill enables deep, verifiable execution in a specific scenario while dynamic routing maintains open-ended variety. Introduces a co-evolutionary loop of proposer, solver, and skill controller.
**Tags:** `LLM`, `self-play`, `agents`, `training`, `skill-learning`

## [Embodied.cpp: A Portable Inference Runtime for Embodied AI Models on Heterogeneous Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02501)
**Authors:** Ling Xu, Chuyu Han, Borui Li, Hao Wu, Shiqi Jiang, Ting Cao, Chuanyou Li, Sheng Zhong, Shuai Wang
**TL;DR:** Addresses the fragmented deployment of VLA and world-action models across heterogeneous robot hardware by capturing a shared, hardware-agnostic execution path. Organized into five layers — input adapters, sequence builders, backbone execution, head plugins, and deployment adapters — enabling single-build deployment across diverse edge devices without Python stack fragmentation.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `inference`, `VLA`, `deployment`

## [DataPrep-Bench: Benchmarking LLMs as Training Data Preparators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20465)
**Authors:** Hao Liang, Qifeng Cai, Yibo Lin, Jianzhuo Du, Qifeng Xia, Sizhe Qiu, et al.
**TL;DR:** The first unified benchmark evaluating both (1) data construction — transforming raw sources into supervised training data — and (2) data quality evaluation — predicting the training value of candidate datasets before downstream training. Covers six domains across multiple base models under a shared downstream-grounded protocol. Directly relevant to agentic data-curation pipelines.
**Tags:** `benchmark`, `data`, `LLM`, `agents`, `training-data`

## [Grounded World Models in Biological Organisms and Future Embodied AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13560)
**Authors:** Giovanni Pezzulo et al.
**TL;DR:** Draws principled parallels between world models in biological organisms and the predictive learning frameworks driving modern embodied AI. Argues that embodied agents need grounded world models — representations that couple observations with physical states and consequences — rather than purely statistical pattern matching, with implications for how future systems should be trained and evaluated.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `world-models`, `neuroscience`, `cognition`, `theory`
