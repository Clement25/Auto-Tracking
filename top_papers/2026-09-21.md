## [rMuscle: Robotic Muscle Memory for Efficient Vision-Language-Action Model Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19104)
**Authors:** (See arXiv for full author list)
**TL;DR:** rMuscle introduces a "robotic muscle memory" mechanism that caches frequently-executed action sequences in VLA models, analogous to procedural memory in humans. By bypassing full VLA inference for learned routine actions, rMuscle reduces latency and computational cost substantially during robot task execution.

**Authors:** Mengxiao Wang, Nitesh Saxena
**TL;DR:** A comprehensive security audit of 15 representative LLM-based financial trading agents finds that 80% fail standard robustness tests under market turbulence and 100% exhibit at least one exploitable security vulnerability across three attack classes. The paper calls for mandatory security evaluations before deploying LLM agents in financial markets.
