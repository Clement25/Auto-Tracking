# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-10-20)

## [DiscoveryRSI: Learning from Research Histories for Scientific Hypothesis Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.discoveryRSI)
**Authors:** (arXiv cs.AI, Oct 16 2026)
**TL;DR:** DiscoveryRSI uses multi-agent evolutionary search and recursive learning over past research histories to help AI agents produce more testable scientific hypotheses. Improvements are concentrated in falsifiability and evidence planning, making it a step toward autonomous scientific discovery.
**Tags:** `agents`, `scientific-discovery`, `multi-agent`, `hypothesis-generation`

## [Harness²: Recursive Refinement of Agent Execution Harnesses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.harness2)
**Authors:** Google DeepMind (Oct 9 2026)
**TL;DR:** Harness² improves agent execution harnesses through recursive task-level refinement and continual harness-level learning, without requiring ground-truth verifiers. The approach addresses a key bottleneck in deploying reliable agentic systems at scale.
**Tags:** `agents`, `execution-harness`, `agentic-systems`, `google`

## [OpenGCUA: An Open Generalist Computer-Use Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.openGCUA)

**TL;DR:** OpenGCUA is an open-source generalist computer-use agent that combines visual and programmatic interfaces for broad desktop automation tasks. By releasing the model openly, the authors aim to accelerate community research on general-purpose GUI agents.

## [MetaShare: Failure-Aware Collective Self-Improvement for Open-Ended Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21887)
**Authors:** (NeurIPS 2026 Workshop, Oct 2026)
**TL;DR:** MetaShare enables groups of open-ended agents to collectively improve by sharing and learning from failure cases rather than successes alone. The approach significantly reduces redundant exploration and accelerates skill acquisition across the agent population.
**Tags:** `agents`, `collective-learning`, `self-improvement`, `open-ended`, `neurips`

## [Darwin-Claw: Experience Consolidation for Self-Evolving Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23574)
**Authors:** (arXiv cs.AI, Oct 2026)
**TL;DR:** Darwin-Claw presents a mechanism for self-evolving agents to consolidate and reuse past experiences, enabling continual learning without catastrophic forgetting. The agent iteratively curates its experience buffer using evolutionary selection principles.
**Tags:** `agents`, `continual-learning`, `self-evolving`, `experience-replay`

## [OmniDex: Scaling Dexterous Hand Grasping to Cluttered Scenes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.omnidex)
**Authors:** (arXiv cs.RO, Oct 2026)
**TL;DR:** OmniDex scales dexterous robotic hand grasping to highly cluttered and unstructured scenes, addressing the gap between lab-controlled manipulation tasks and real-world deployment. The system uses a large-scale training regime and a generalizable grasp planning module.

## [SmolWAM: A Latent World-Action Model for Manipulation on Edge Devices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.smolwam)
**Authors:** (NeurIPS 2026 Workshop, Oct 2026)
**TL;DR:** SmolWAM is a compact latent world-action model designed to enable robot manipulation on resource-constrained edge devices. It jointly models world dynamics and action policies in a shared latent space, achieving competitive performance with a fraction of the compute.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `world-models`, `edge-computing`, `neurips`

## [ParallelWorld: Test-Time Scaling for Agentic Embodied Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.parallelworld)
**Authors:** (arXiv cs.AI / cs.RO, Oct 2026)
**TL;DR:** ParallelWorld introduces a test-time scaling approach for embodied agents, running multiple parallel reasoning traces across simulated world branches and selecting the most consistent plan. This dramatically improves robustness in long-horizon embodied tasks.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `test-time-scaling`, `reasoning`, `planning`
