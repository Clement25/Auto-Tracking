## [Embodied AI: Emerging Risks and Opportunities for Policy Action](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.00117)
**Authors:** arXiv contributors (2509.00117)
**TL;DR:** Examines the emerging policy landscape around embodied AI systems deployed in the physical world, cataloguing risks such as unintended physical harm, misuse in autonomous weapons, and accountability gaps. Proposes concrete regulatory frameworks and international coordination mechanisms to harness the technology's benefits while mitigating harms.
