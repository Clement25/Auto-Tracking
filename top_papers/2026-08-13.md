# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-08-13)

## [ComBodied Agents: a New Paradigm of Human-Centric Agentic AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10915)
**Authors:** (submitted Aug 11–12, 2026; full author list at link)
**TL;DR:** Introduces ComBodied Agents, a framework that makes the evolving state and agency of a *person* the primary object of modeling—unlike pure software agents (state transformations) or embodied robots (physical transformations). A closed loop of multimodal perception, longitudinal memory, Personal World Models, and admissible intervention policies enables software tools, sensors, wearables, and robots to serve as action channels rather than end goals.
**Tags:** `agents`, `embodied-ai`, `human-centric`, `health-ai`, `personal-world-models`

## [Towards the Harness of Embodied Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11246)
**Authors:** Qi Wang, Tianyi Wang, Chengyang Li, Shikun Ban, Yurun Chen, Yizhong Ge, Jason Qin, Chengtai Li, Wentao Zhu
**TL;DR:** Proposes Thea, an agentic harness that applies the coding-agent harness paradigm to physical-world robots—each robot capability wrapped as a callable tool. Introduces Scene Graph as Context (persistent symbolic world state) and Evaluation as Exit Codes (action termination detection and failure diagnosis) to handle the unique challenges of reading and judging physical-world outcomes.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `agents`, `harness`, `scene-graph`

## [Self-Evolving Embodied Agents via Skill-Harness Evolution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11350)
**Authors:** Peidong Wang, Zhiming Ma, Ying Chang, Xufang Luo, Xiaocui Yang, Shi Feng, Yuqing Yang, Dongsheng Li
**TL;DR:** SHAPER is a train-free adaptation framework for embodied agents that keeps model weights frozen and improves performance by evolving reusable skills and a context-code harness through environment rollouts. The same frozen foundation model acts as both planner and optimizer, enabling deployment-time adaptation without any parameter updates or reward signals.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `agents`, `self-evolving`, `train-free`, `skill-learning`

## [BridgeVLA++: A Data-Efficient, Generalizable, and Memory-Augmented Vision-Language-Action Framework for 3D Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05042)
**Authors:** (see paper page at bridgevla-plus.github.io)
**TL;DR:** Extends BridgeVLA (which aligns 3D observations and actions in shared multi-view 2D heatmap space) with a unified spatio-temporal memory: temporal memory decides *what* to do next, spatial memory decides *where* exactly to act, both injected through shared VLM patch-token space and shareable across two arms for bimanual manipulation. Achieves state-of-the-art on RLBench, COLOSSEUM, GemBench, and MemoryBench.
**Tags:** `robotics`, `VLA`, `manipulation`, `embodied-ai`, `memory`, `bimanual`

## [EmbodiedVAE: Disentangled Video VAE for Efficient and Controllable Embodied Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02990)
**Authors:** (submitted Aug 4, 2026; ECCV 2026)
**TL;DR:** A dual-encoder, single-decoder video VAE with asymmetric spatio-temporal compression that automatically disentangles robot arm motion from background environment. An optimal-transport-based consistency module enforces motion fidelity and inter-frame coherence, achieving ~2 dB PSNR improvement over SOTA video VAEs while enabling finer-grained action control in world models for robotic manipulation.
**Tags:** `robotics`, `video-generation`, `world-models`, `manipulation`, `embodied-ai`, `ECCV-2026`

## [VANE: Reliable Test-Time Training for Vision-Language-Action Models via Future Visual Representation Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09448)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2608.09448)
**TL;DR:** VANE enables lightweight, closed-loop adaptation of VLA policies at deployment time using only unlabeled streams—no additional reward signals or retraining required. By predicting future visual representations, the model self-supervises adaptation to distribution shifts encountered in real-world manipulation, improving reliability without the cost of full fine-tuning.
**Tags:** `robotics`, `VLA`, `test-time-training`, `embodied-ai`, `adaptation`

## [Needle 2: 14 MB Foundation Model for Tiny Devices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18363)
**Authors:** Cactus Compute team
**TL;DR:** A 45 M-parameter foundation model compressed to a single 14 MB binary using CQ2-bit quantization that runs in ~28 MB RAM on phones, wearables, smart-home devices, and robots. Introduces the Simple Attention Network (Hadamard MLP, GQA, Engram KV memory, multi-lane hyper-connections) and supports tool-calling, structured extraction, confidence scoring, and tool retrieval—5–70× smaller than comparable models while remaining competitive on function-calling benchmarks.
**Tags:** `edge-ai`, `embodied-ai`, `tiny-models`, `tool-calling`, `robotics`, `on-device`

## [Towards Embodied Agentic AI: Review and Classification of LLM- and VLM-Driven Robot Autonomy and Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05294)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2508.05294, v4 updated Aug 2026)
**TL;DR:** A comprehensive survey and classification of how large language models and vision-language models are enabling novel approaches to robot autonomy. Reviews vision-language-action (VLA) models, large behavior models (LBMs), and multimodal interaction frameworks across the full spectrum from task planning to physical skill execution, mapping the field into perception, brain, planning, action, tool use, and collaboration components.
**Tags:** `survey`, `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `LLM`, `VLM`, `VLA`, `agents`

## [Kronos: Foundation Model for the Language of Financial Markets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.02739)
**Authors:** Shiyu Coder team (AAAI 2026 accepted)
**TL;DR:** The first open-source foundation model trained on K-line (candlestick OHLCV) data from 45+ global exchanges. A two-stage pipeline—hierarchical discrete tokenizer for K-line sequences + decoder-only autoregressive Transformer—produces probabilistic multi-horizon forecasts across model sizes from 4 M to 499 M parameters. Demonstrates that domain-specific tokenization unlocks strong zero-shot and fine-tuned forecasting performance.
**Tags:** `foundation-model`, `time-series`, `finance`, `AAAI-2026`, `forecasting`

## [KILVO: Kinematic-Inertial-LiDAR-Visual Odometry with Robust Multimodal Adaptation for Humanoid Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05647)
**Authors:** Jixin Gao, Fucheng Liu, Teng Zhang, Fusheng Zha (accepted IEEE/ASME Transactions on Mechatronics)
**TL;DR:** Presents a tightly-coupled odometry system for humanoid robots that fuses kinematic, inertial (IMU), LiDAR, and visual sensing into a single estimation pipeline with robust multimodal adaptation—handling sensor dropouts and environmental degradation that typically break single-modality methods. Designed for the unique kinematics and motion profiles of bipedal humanoid platforms.
**Tags:** `robotics`, `humanoid`, `odometry`, `sensor-fusion`, `LiDAR`, `embodied-ai`
