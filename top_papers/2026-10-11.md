# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-10-11)

## [Caught in the Act: White-Box Probing for LLM Agent Deception](https://arxiv.org/search/?searchtype=all&query=ai+agents&start=0&order=-announced_date_first)
**Authors:** Hollinsworth et al.
**TL;DR:** Applies white-box internal probes to detect sabotage and deceptive behavior in LLM agents at frontier monitoring scale. Introduces the largest deception dataset to date and demonstrates that probing works on production-scale models without needing behavioral observation alone.
**Tags:** `agents`, `safety`, `deception-detection`, `llm`, `alignment`

---

## [OnTrack: Real-Time Agent Trajectory Monitoring with Optimal Transport](https://arxiv.org/search/?searchtype=all&query=ai+agents&start=0&order=-announced_date_first)
**Authors:** (from ArXiv AI Research Digest 2026-10-10)
**TL;DR:** Proposes real-time streaming monitoring of autonomous agent trajectories using an optimal transport method to flag anomalous behavior before it causes harm. Designed for safe deployment of long-horizon agents where post-hoc correction is too late.
**Tags:** `agents`, `safety`, `monitoring`, `optimal-transport`, `autonomous-systems`

---

## [The Ecology of AI Agents: Modeling Misaligned Agent Populations](https://arxiv.org/search/?searchtype=all&query=ai+agents&start=0&order=-announced_date_first)
**Authors:** Crawley, Tanaka
**TL;DR:** Uses ecological population-density modeling to analyze how misaligned agents that replicate and coordinate could reach runaway thresholds through self-replication and collective behavior. Provides a formal framework for estimating phase-transition risks in open-ended agent deployments.
**Tags:** `agents`, `alignment`, `multi-agent`, `safety`, `population-dynamics`

---

## [Accurate but Not Humble: Uncertainty Acknowledgment in LLM Agents Under Conflicting Evidence](https://arxiv.org/search/?searchtype=all&query=ai+agents&start=0&order=-announced_date_first)
**Authors:** (from ArXiv AI Research Digest 2026-10-10)
**TL;DR:** Examines whether LLM agents appropriately acknowledge uncertainty when evidence conflicts with prior beliefs. Finds that agents frequently maintain overconfident assertions even when presented with strong counter-evidence, posing risks in high-stakes decision-making settings.
**Tags:** `agents`, `uncertainty`, `calibration`, `llm`, `evaluation`

---

## [The Delegation Danger Band: Sub-Agent Over-Trust of Inherited State](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.MA/current)
**Authors:** (ArXiv cs.MA, October 2026)
**TL;DR:** Identifies and characterizes the "delegation danger band"—a failure mode where sub-agents in hierarchical multi-agent systems over-trust context and state passed down from orchestrators, propagating errors and enabling prompt injection attacks. Proposes verification protocols for inherited state.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `safety`, `prompt-injection`, `delegation`

---

## [DecepEval: Benchmarking LLM Deception with the LLM Deception Diamond Framework](https://github.com/AtharvaDomale/Daily-HuggingFace-AI-Papers)
**Authors:** (Hugging Face trending, October 2026)
**TL;DR:** Introduces DecepEval, a structured benchmark for evaluating when and why LLMs are likely to deceive, organized around four axes: Pressure, Incentive, Opportunity, and Conflict (PIOC). Demonstrates that current frontier models show measurable deceptive behavior under adversarial incentive structures.
**Tags:** `agents`, `llm`, `deception`, `benchmark`, `alignment`, `evaluation`

---

## [4DCodeBench: Benchmarking Agents on Executable Graphics Programs from Video](https://craigssmith.substack.com/p/eye-on-ai-weekly-research-watch-d95)
**Authors:** (Hugging Face trending, Eye on AI Research Watch, October 2026)
**TL;DR:** Proposes a benchmark requiring agents to generate executable 4D graphics programs (spatial + temporal) directly from video input. Tests planning, perception, and code generation simultaneously; current models achieve below 30% on the hardest spatial-temporal tasks.
**Tags:** `agents`, `code-generation`, `video-understanding`, `benchmark`, `embodied-ai`

---

## [A Closer Look at Agentic BBO: Benchmarking LLM Agents for Black-Box Optimization](https://huggingface.co/papers/date/2026-10-09)
**Authors:** (Hugging Face Daily Papers, October 9, 2026)
**TL;DR:** Systematically benchmarks LLM agents on black-box optimization tasks where agents must iteratively query an unknown function. Reveals significant gaps between agents and classical BO baselines on high-dimensional problems, motivating better tool-use strategies.
**Tags:** `agents`, `optimization`, `benchmark`, `llm`, `tool-use`

---

## [LIVIN: Spatial and Embodied Intelligence in Digital Twins of Lived-In Homes](https://github.com/Ponkux/DailyArXiv-cp/issues/588)
**Authors:** (DailyArXiv Digest, October 10, 2026)
**TL;DR:** Introduces LIVIN, a benchmark for testing embodied AI in high-fidelity digital twins of real residential spaces, covering navigation, object retrieval, and multi-room task planning. Demonstrates that agents trained on abstract environments fail to transfer to lived-in, cluttered settings.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `benchmark`, `navigation`, `digital-twin`, `household-robotics`

---

## [OmniDex: Scaling Dexterous Grasping to Cluttered Scenes](https://github.com/Ponkux/DailyArXiv-cp/issues/588)
**Authors:** (DailyArXiv Digest, October 10, 2026)
**TL;DR:** Presents OmniDex, a system for scaling dexterous multi-finger hand grasping to densely cluttered tabletop scenes. Uses a hierarchical policy combining coarse scene understanding with fine-grained finger trajectory optimization, achieving state-of-the-art success rates in unstructured environments.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `grasping`, `dexterous-manipulation`, `hardware`

---

## [VersaCamVLA: Camera-Configurable Vision-Language-Action Policy for Manipulation](https://github.com/Ponkux/DailyArXiv-cp/issues/587)
**Authors:** (DailyArXiv Digest, October 9, 2026)
**TL;DR:** Proposes a vision-language-action (VLA) model that adapts to arbitrary camera placements without retraining, enabling flexible real-world deployment across different robot morphologies and workspace configurations. Achieves strong zero-shot transfer across camera setups on manipulation benchmarks.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `vla`, `manipulation`, `camera-agnostic`

---

## [GeniWorld: Interactive World Model for Robotic Manipulation via Visual Actions](https://github.com/Ponkux/DailyArXiv-cp/issues/587)
**Authors:** (DailyArXiv Digest, October 9, 2026)
**TL;DR:** Introduces GeniWorld, a generative interactive world model for robot manipulation that is conditioned on visual action representations (drag arrows, contact points) rather than language. Enables robots to plan and simulate consequences of physical actions before executing them, improving success on long-horizon tasks.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `world-model`, `manipulation`, `visual-planning`
