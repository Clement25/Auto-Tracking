# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-09-20)

## [Agentic Services Computing](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.24380)
**Authors:** (Multiple contributors, arXiv:2509.24380)
**TL;DR:** Introduces a formal framework for "agentic services computing," treating LLM-based agents as first-class computing units that expose services. Covers task decomposition, orchestration, and service-level abstractions needed as AI agents become building blocks of larger software systems.
**Tags:** `agents`, `agentic-systems`, `service-computing`, `llm`

---

## [Chronicle: Cut-Point Replay for Regression Testing of LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20625)
**Authors:** Tisha Chawla, Susheem Koul
**TL;DR:** Proposes Chronicle, a regression testing framework for LLM agents that records "cut-points" in agent execution traces and replays them cheaply for subsequent test runs. Reduces test cost by orders of magnitude while catching behavior regressions introduced by model or prompt updates.
**Tags:** `agents`, `testing`, `regression`, `llm`, `software-engineering`

---

## [CUDA-Harness: Harnessing Agentic CUDA Kernel Generation and Optimization from Natural Language](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00058)
**Authors:** Qi Fan, An Zou, Yehan Ma
**TL;DR:** Presents an agentic pipeline that generates and iteratively optimizes CUDA kernels directly from natural language specifications. The system outperforms one-shot generation baselines by using tool-calling agents that compile, profile, and patch kernels in closed-loop.
**Tags:** `agents`, `code-generation`, `cuda`, `llm`, `tool-use`

---

## [Toward Workflow-Aware Benchmarking for Healthcare NLP Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00310)
**Authors:** Junyi Yao, Baichuan Li, Zihao Zheng, Jiayu Long
**TL;DR:** Argues that existing NLP benchmarks miss clinical workflow context, and proposes a workflow-aware evaluation suite for healthcare agents covering triage, documentation, and patient-interaction tasks. Shows that top models drop 15–30% in accuracy when workflow constraints are enforced.
**Tags:** `agents`, `healthcare`, `nlp`, `benchmark`, `evaluation`

---

## [Physics of Agents: Statistical Mechanics Predicts Collective Behavior of AI Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16578)
**Authors:** (Multiple contributors, arXiv:2608.16578)
**TL;DR:** Applies statistical mechanics to predict the collective opinion dynamics of over 10,000 simulated communities of language-model agents. Shows that phase-transition-like thresholds govern when agent communities converge vs. polarize, with implications for designing reliable multi-agent systems.
**Tags:** `multi-agent`, `collective-intelligence`, `statistical-mechanics`, `llm`, `agents`

---

## [Harnessing the Collective Intelligence of AI Agents in the Wild for New Discoveries](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.10402)
**Authors:** (Multiple contributors, arXiv:2606.10402)
**TL;DR:** Proposes a framework for deploying large heterogeneous populations of AI agents to accelerate scientific discovery, combining market-like incentives with agent specialization. Demonstrates on protein-structure and drug-synthesis tasks that collective agent ensembles outperform monolithic frontier models.
**Tags:** `multi-agent`, `collective-intelligence`, `scientific-discovery`, `agents`

---

## [PlanBench-XL: Evaluating Long-Horizon Planning of LLM Tool-Use Agents in Large-Scale Tool Ecosystems](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.22388)
**Authors:** (Multiple contributors, arXiv:2606.22388)
**TL;DR:** Introduces PlanBench-XL, a benchmark with 50,000+ tasks requiring multi-step tool chaining across ecosystems of 500–5,000 tools. Current best agents solve fewer than 30% of hard instances, revealing a large gap in compositional planning ability.
**Tags:** `agents`, `planning`, `tool-use`, `benchmark`, `long-horizon`

---

## [ACE-Brain-0.5: A Unified Embodied Foundational Model for Physical Agentic AI](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.04426)
**Authors:** (Multiple contributors, arXiv:2607.04426)
**TL;DR:** Presents ACE-Brain-0.5, a 7B-parameter model trained jointly on manipulation, navigation, and instruction-following tasks across 15 robot platforms. Achieves strong zero-shot transfer to unseen robots and outperforms specialized policies on most embodied benchmarks.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `foundation-model`, `manipulation`, `navigation`

---

## [Embodied AI Agents: Modeling the World](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.22355v1)
**Authors:** (Multiple contributors, arXiv:2506.22355)
**TL;DR:** Surveys the landscape of world-model approaches for embodied AI agents, categorizing methods by representation (latent, pixel, symbolic) and by learning paradigm (supervised, RL, self-supervised). Highlights open challenges in sim-to-real transfer and causal grounding that limit deployment outside controlled settings.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `world-models`, `survey`, `robotics`, `agents`

---

## [How Should World Models Be Evaluated for Embodied Decision-Making? A Decision-Making-Centric Position](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.15032)
**Authors:** (Multiple contributors, arXiv:2606.15032)
**TL;DR:** Challenges the prevailing pixel-prediction evaluation paradigm for world models, arguing that fidelity metrics poorly predict usefulness in downstream planning. Proposes a decision-making-centric evaluation protocol and shows it better differentiates models on real robot tasks.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `world-models`, `evaluation`, `robotics`, `decision-making`

---

## [RISE: Self-Improving Robot Policy with Compositional World Model](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.11075)
**Authors:** (Multiple contributors, arXiv:2602.11075)
**TL;DR:** Introduces RISE, a robot learning system that uses a compositional world model to generate synthetic rollouts for self-improvement without additional real-world data. On manipulation benchmarks, RISE doubles task success rate after three rounds of self-improvement, reducing human data requirements significantly.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `self-improvement`, `world-models`, `manipulation`
