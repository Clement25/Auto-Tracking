# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-08-15)

## [Can AI Agents Conduct Open-Ended AI Research?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27191)
**Authors:** Amos Azaria, et al.
**TL;DR:** Evaluates frontier AI agents given six days and thousands of dollars of compute on open-ended AI research tasks. While agents completed engineering tasks without human assistance, they could not make substantial progress toward answering genuine research questions — providing early empirical bounds on current autonomous research capability.
**Tags:** `agents`, `ai-research`, `evaluation`, `autonomous-systems`

## [HAS-Bench: Evaluating LLM-Based Human-Agent Systems under Configurable Human Participation](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.04329)
**Authors:** Anonymous
**TL;DR:** Introduces HAS-Bench, a benchmark for evaluating LLM-based human-agent systems across varying levels of human involvement. The benchmark captures how agent performance and safety change as human participation is dialed up or down, revealing gaps in current systems' ability to calibrate to human oversight.
**Tags:** `agents`, `human-agent-interaction`, `benchmark`, `llm`

## [Adversarial Attacks in Multi-Agent LLM Pipelines: Unveiling Structural Vulnerabilities in Agentic AI Architectures](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.MA/current)
**Authors:** To be confirmed (accepted at IEEE GLOBECOM 2026)
**TL;DR:** Examines how adversarial perturbations propagate through multi-agent LLM pipelines, exposing structural vulnerabilities at agent handoff and coordination points. Demonstrates attacks that degrade pipeline outputs while evading single-agent defenses, with implications for the deployment of agentic AI in high-stakes settings.
**Tags:** `agents`, `adversarial-attacks`, `multi-agent`, `security`, `llm`

## [Forage V2: Knowledge Evolution and Transfer in Autonomous Agent Organizations](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.19837)
**Authors:** Anonymous
**TL;DR:** Proposes Forage V2, a framework enabling autonomous agent organizations to continuously evolve and transfer knowledge across tasks without manual retraining. Agents maintain dynamic knowledge graphs that allow skills learned in one domain to bootstrap performance in novel domains.
**Tags:** `agents`, `knowledge-transfer`, `multi-agent`, `continual-learning`

## [Efficient Agentic Reasoning Through Self-Regulated Simulative Planning](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.22138)
**Authors:** Anonymous
**TL;DR:** Introduces a self-regulated planning approach where agents internally simulate action outcomes before committing, using lightweight world-model rollouts to prune the search space. Achieves significant reductions in environment interactions while matching or exceeding prior state-of-the-art on long-horizon reasoning benchmarks.
**Tags:** `agents`, `planning`, `reasoning`, `simulation`, `efficiency`

## [Act As a Real Researcher: A Suite of Benchmarks Evaluating Frontier LLMs and Agentic Harnesses in Research Lifecycle](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.07462)
**Authors:** Anonymous
**TL;DR:** Presents a comprehensive benchmark suite covering the full research lifecycle — hypothesis generation, literature review, experimental design, and paper writing — evaluated on frontier LLMs and agentic harnesses. Results show that while models excel at isolated subtasks, end-to-end research-quality output remains a key open challenge.
**Tags:** `agents`, `benchmark`, `research-automation`, `llm`, `evaluation`

## [Qwen-VLA: Unifying Vision-Language-Action Modeling across Tasks, Environments, and Robot Embodiments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30280)
**Authors:** Qwen Team, Alibaba Group
**TL;DR:** Qwen-VLA extends the Qwen vision-language modeling stack to continuous action generation using a DiT-based action decoder, creating a single model that generalizes across diverse robotic tasks, simulation environments, and physical robot embodiments. Demonstrates strong zero-shot transfer and state-of-the-art performance on multiple manipulation benchmarks.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `vla`, `vision-language-action`, `manipulation`

---

## [PhysReflect-VLA: Physical Feasibility and Self-Reflective Regulation for Reliable Vision-Language-Action Policies](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.27146)
**Authors:** Anonymous
**TL;DR:** Introduces PhysReflect-VLA, which augments VLA models with a physical feasibility checker and a self-reflective regulation loop that catches and corrects physically implausible actions before execution. Results on real-robot manipulation tasks show substantially reduced failure rates compared to base VLA models in contact-rich scenarios.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `vla`, `safety`, `self-reflection`

---

## [Towards Long-horizon Embodied Agents with Tool-Aligned Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13119)
**Authors:** Anonymous
**TL;DR:** Proposes treating VLA sub-policies as composable tools that higher-level planners can invoke, enabling long-horizon task completion through structured tool-calling. VLAs-as-Tools significantly improves success rates on long-horizon benchmarks where flat end-to-end VLA approaches fail.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `vla`, `long-horizon`, `tool-use`

---

## [VLA-JEPA: Enhancing Vision-Language-Action Model with Latent World Model](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.10098)
**Authors:** Anonymous
**TL;DR:** Combines the JEPA (Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture) latent world model with VLA policies so that robots can predict future latent states and use those predictions for action refinement. Improves robustness to visual perturbations and unseen environments without requiring additional real-world data collection.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `vla`, `world-model`, `jepa`

---

## [Self-evolving Embodied AI](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.04411)
**Authors:** Anonymous
**TL;DR:** Proposes a self-evolving framework where embodied agents iteratively generate their own training experiences, evaluate skill acquisition, and update both their policy and curriculum — closing the loop without human curriculum design. Shows progressive skill accumulation on a suite of household and manipulation tasks.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `self-improvement`, `curriculum-learning`, `robotics`

## [StateBridge: Training-free Hidden-state Alignment for Latent Communication in LLM Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.MA/current)
**Authors:** Anonymous (accepted at COLM 2026)
**TL;DR:** Proposes StateBridge, a training-free method that aligns hidden states across heterogeneous LLMs in a multi-agent pipeline, enabling efficient latent-space communication without fine-tuning any participating model. Demonstrates improved coordination and reduced token overhead on collaborative reasoning and code-generation tasks.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `llm`, `communication`, `efficiency`
