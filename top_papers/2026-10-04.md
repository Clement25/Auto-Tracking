## [DynamicVLA: A Vision-Language-Action Model for Dynamic Object Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22153)
**Authors:** Haozhe Xie, Beichen Wen, Jiarui Zheng, et al.
**TL;DR:** Addresses the latency-induced perception-execution mismatch in VLA models when manipulating moving objects — object states continue to evolve during inference, making actions predicted from past observations stale. DynamicVLA improves dynamic manipulation success under changing object motion, perception-heavy instructions, and unseen motion patterns. Accepted at NeurIPS 2026.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `vision-language-action`, `manipulation`

## [RPG: Reconstruct, Practice, Go Real — Guided Self-Improvement for Embodied Agents](https://arxiv.org/search/?query=RPG+embodied+agents&searchtype=all)
**Authors:** (From arXiv agents-radar digest 2026-10-03)
