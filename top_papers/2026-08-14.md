# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-08-14)

## [ComBodied Agents: a New Paradigm of Human-Centric Agentic AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10915)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2608.10915, submitted August 11, 2026)
**TL;DR:** Introduces Combodied Agents, a human-centered paradigm in which AI perceives, models, predicts, and supports individual human-state trajectories over time. The system uses software tools, wearables, sensors, robots, and human-service channels as unified action channels, bridging embodied and agentic AI under a single framework.
**Tags:** `agents`, `embodied-ai`, `human-centric`, `multi-modal`

## [Towards Embodied Agentic AI: Review and Classification of LLM- and VLM-Driven Robot Autonomy and Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05294)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2508.05294v4)
**TL;DR:** Comprehensive survey of foundation models (LLMs, VLMs, and VLAs) enabling novel approaches to robot autonomy, human-robot interfaces, and agentic architectures where AI agents serve as coordinators, planners, and generalist interfaces. Covers agentic applications where robots reason over natural language, invoke APIs, plan task sequences, and assist in operations. Reviews the state of large behavior models (LBMs) increasing dexterity and capabilities in robotic systems.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `VLM`, `LLM`, `survey`, `human-robot-interaction`

## [StateBridge: Training-free Hidden-state Alignment for Latent Communication in LLM Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/recent)
**Authors:** Yanwen Peng, Delvin Ce Zhang, Xi Wang, Nikolaos Aletras
**TL;DR:** Proposes a training-free method for aligning hidden states across LLMs to enable latent-space communication between agents, bypassing costly token-by-token message passing. Analyzes latent communication along three axes: what information is shared (embeddings, hidden states, KV-caches), which alignment strategy is used, and how communicated information is fused into the receiver. Accepted to COLM 2026.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `latent-communication`, `LLM`, `efficiency`

## [Beyond Tokens: A Unified Framework for Latent Communication in LLM-based Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05711)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2606.05711)
**TL;DR:** Proposes a unified framework for enabling LLM-based agents to communicate through continuous latent representations rather than discrete tokens, addressing token inefficiency in multi-agent pipelines. Explores channels, alignment mechanisms, and the fundamental limits of text-based inter-agent communication compared to latent alternatives.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `latent-communication`, `LLM`

## [HAS-Bench: Evaluating LLM-Based Human-Agent Systems under Configurable Human Participation](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.04329)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2607.04329)
**TL;DR:** Introduces HAS-Bench, a benchmark for systematically evaluating how LLM-powered agents perform when humans participate at configurable levels of engagement—from fully automated to human-in-the-loop. Surfaces how agent performance degrades or improves as human involvement changes, providing reproducible baselines for hybrid agent systems.
**Tags:** `agents`, `evaluation`, `benchmark`, `human-in-the-loop`, `LLM`

## [AgentLongBench: A Controllable Long Benchmark For Long-Contexts Agents via Environment Rollouts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20730)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2601.20730)
**TL;DR:** Proposes a controllable benchmark that evaluates agents on long-horizon tasks through simulated environment rollouts based on Lateral Thinking Puzzles, generating rigorous interaction trajectories for context windows from 32K to 4M tokens. Experiments on state-of-the-art models expose a critical weakness: agents excel at static retrieval but struggle with dynamic information synthesis essential for multi-step workflows.
**Tags:** `agents`, `benchmark`, `long-context`, `evaluation`, `LLM`

## [NeuroFilter: Privacy Guardrails for Conversational LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/recent)
**Authors:** (recent arXiv submission)
**TL;DR:** Introduces activation-space guardrails that detect privacy-violating intent within LLM agents by exploiting linear separability of internal representations—without modifying model weights. Includes drift detection across multi-turn conversations to catch latent privacy breaches that develop incrementally across dialogue turns.
**Tags:** `agents`, `privacy`, `safety`, `LLM`, `guardrails`

## [Embodied AI Agents: Modeling the World](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.22355)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2506.22355)
**TL;DR:** Examines how embodied AI agents—systems instantiated in visual, virtual, or physical form—learn to model and interact with their environment through world models. Bridges the gap between passive world modeling and active embodied agents that must act under partial observability and physical constraints.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `world-models`, `agents`, `robotics`

## [A Survey: Learning Embodied Intelligence from Physical Simulators and World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00917)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2507.00917)
**TL;DR:** Surveys the landscape of embodied intelligence learning by categorizing approaches that leverage physics-based simulators and learned world models to train agents for real-world deployment. Covers sim-to-real transfer, differentiable simulation, and the integration of generative world models as training environments for robotic policies.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `world-models`, `simulation`, `survey`

## [Multi-agent Collaboration with State Management](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20563)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2605.20563)
**TL;DR:** Addresses the challenge of maintaining consistent shared state across asynchronous LLM-based multi-agent systems, proposing a principled state-management layer that reduces coordination failures and conflicting updates. Demonstrates measurable gains on collaborative task benchmarks where agents must read and write shared context under concurrency constraints.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `coordination`, `state-management`, `LLM`

## [InternAgent-1.5: A Unified Agentic Framework for Long-Horizon Autonomous Scientific Discovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.08990)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2602.08990, InternScience)
**TL;DR:** Presents InternAgent-1.5, a unified framework for autonomous scientific discovery that closes the hypothesis-to-verification loop without human intervention. Integrates tool use, experiment design, data analysis, and iterative refinement into a single long-horizon agent pipeline benchmarked on real scientific tasks spanning chemistry, biology, and materials science.
**Tags:** `agents`, `scientific-discovery`, `autonomous`, `long-horizon`, `tool-use`

## [Embodied AI in Action: Insights from SAE World Congress 2026 on Safety, Trust, Robotics, and Real-World Deployment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10653)
**Authors:** (arXiv:2605.10653)
**TL;DR:** Documents expert consensus from a cross-industry panel (automotive, robotics, AI) on the safety and trust challenges blocking real-world deployment of embodied AI systems. Highlights divergent regulatory expectations between the EU and US, proposes minimum viable safety standards for physical AI agents, and identifies human-trust calibration as the central unsolved deployment problem.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `safety`, `deployment`, `policy`
