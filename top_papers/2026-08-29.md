# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-08-29)

## [From LLM Reasoning to Autonomous AI Agents: A Comprehensive Review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.19678)
**Authors:** Multiple authors (comprehensive survey team)
**TL;DR:** A sweeping review comparing benchmarks developed between 2019 and 2025 for autonomous AI agents. Covers real-world applications across materials science, biomedical research, software engineering, finance, and more, and traces the evolution of diverse evaluation frameworks and collaboration protocols.
**Tags:** `agents`, `llm`, `survey`, `benchmarks`, `autonomous-agents`

## [Deep Research Agents: A Systematic Examination And Roadmap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.18096)
**Authors:** Yuxuan Huang, Yihang Chen, Haozheng Zhang, Kang Li, Meng Fang, Linyi Yang, Xiaoguang Li, Lifeng Shang, Songcen Xu, Jianye Hao, Kun Shao, Jun Wang
**TL;DR:** Introduces and systematically examines "Deep Research" (DR) agents — a new class of autonomous LLM systems designed for complex, multi-turn informational research via dynamic reasoning, adaptive long-horizon planning, and multi-hop retrieval. Reviews information acquisition strategies (API-based vs. browser-based) and modular tool-use frameworks including MCP integration.
**Tags:** `agents`, `deep-research`, `llm`, `information-retrieval`, `long-horizon`

---

## [Safe Embodied AI for Long-horizon Tasks: A Cross-layer Analysis of Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05660)
**Authors:** Dabin Kim, Daemin Park, Sangyub Lee, Jinsik Kim, Yeongtak Oh, Jongho Shin, Sungroh Yoon
**TL;DR:** A 63-page survey addressing safety in long-horizon robotic manipulation. Organizes the literature by intervention locus — planning-time, policy-time, and execution-time safety — and analyzes evidence quality across each line of work. Particularly timely as embodied AI systems move into real physical workplaces.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `safety`, `manipulation`, `long-horizon`

---

## [Embodied AI Agents: Modeling the World](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.22355v1)
**Authors:** Meta AI Research team
**TL;DR:** Provides an overview of embodied AI agents — AI systems instantiated in visual, virtual, or physical form that learn and interact with users and surroundings. Covers how agents model their environment for perception, planning, and action. A framework paper for understanding the current embodied AI landscape.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `agents`, `world-models`, `perception`

---

## [A Survey: Learning Embodied Intelligence from Physical Simulators and World Models](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.00917)
**Authors:** Survey team (multiple authors)
**TL;DR:** Surveys how physical simulators and world models are used to train embodied intelligence. Covers sim-to-real transfer, procedural environment generation, and how world models enable offline learning before physical deployment. Highlights open challenges in grounding and generalization.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `world-models`, `simulation`, `sim-to-real`, `robotics`

## [Embodied Robot Manipulation in the Era of Foundation Models: Planning and Learning Perspectives](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.22983)
**Authors:** Shuanghao Bai et al. (updated August 15, 2026)
**TL;DR:** Examines how foundation models (vision-language models, large language models) are being applied to robot manipulation tasks. Covers both planning-centric and learning-centric integration strategies and identifies gaps in generalizing manipulation skills to open-world scenarios.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `manipulation`, `foundation-models`, `planning`

## [Harnessing the Collective Intelligence of AI Agents in the Wild for New Discoveries](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.10402)
**Authors:** (Multiple authors)
**TL;DR:** Proposes a framework for using networks of AI agents working collectively to make scientific discoveries. Explores how agents with diverse reasoning strategies and tool access can pool findings beyond what any single agent achieves. Demonstrates applications in hypothesis generation and experimental design.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `scientific-discovery`, `collective-intelligence`

## [Adversarial Attacks in Multi-Agent LLM Pipelines: Unveiling Structural Vulnerabilities in Agentic AI Architectures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.17332)
**Authors:** (Accepted at IEEE GLOBECOM 2026)
**TL;DR:** Identifies and demonstrates structural vulnerabilities in multi-agent LLM pipelines susceptible to adversarial attacks. Shows how prompt injection and message-passing exploits can compromise coordinated agent systems, and proposes defense mechanisms at the architecture level. Directly relevant to real-world incidents of AI agent misbehavior.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `security`, `adversarial`, `llm`

## [Fundamentals of Building Autonomous LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.09244v1)
**Authors:** (TUM seminar report)
**TL;DR:** A structured technical reference covering the architecture and implementation of LLM-powered agents. Focuses on agents' perception, memory, reasoning, planning, and execution subsystems. Based on the TUM course "Trends in Autonomous Agents: Advances in Architecture and Practice."
**Tags:** `agents`, `llm`, `architecture`, `planning`, `memory`

## [PALM: Progress-Aware Policy Learning via Affordance Reasoning for Long-Horizon Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.07060)
**Authors:** (Multiple authors)
**TL;DR:** Introduces PALM, a policy learning approach that integrates affordance reasoning to track task progress during long-horizon manipulation. The model selects sub-goals based on object affordances and current state, leading to significantly more robust multi-step task completion. Benchmarked on challenging tabletop manipulation tasks.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `manipulation`, `affordance`, `long-horizon`, `policy-learning`

---

## [Towards Long-horizon Embodied Agents with Tool-Aligned Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.13119)
**Authors:** (Multiple authors)
**TL;DR:** Proposes aligning Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models with external tool usage to extend embodied agent capabilities over long-horizon tasks. Demonstrates that tool-aligned VLAs can delegate sub-tasks to specialized modules (perception tools, grasping planners), dramatically improving success on multi-step household and industrial manipulation benchmarks.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `vla`, `vision-language-action`, `tools`, `long-horizon`, `agents`
