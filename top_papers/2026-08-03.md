# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-08-03)

## [Qwen-UI-Agent Technical Report: Toward Next-Generation Real-World Centric Foundation GUI Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28227)
**Authors:** Tongyi-Mai Team (Alibaba/Qwen)
**TL;DR:** Qwen-UI-Agent is a foundation GUI agent spanning mobile, computer-use, web, and DeepSearch environments with a unified action space that interleaves GUI operations with CLI execution and supports online RL over 100-turn trajectories. It achieves SOTA on mobile-use benchmarks (97.5% on AndroidDaily, 82.1% on MobileWorld) while competing with GPT-5.6 Sol and Claude Opus 4.8 on computer-use tasks.
**Tags:** `agents`, `gui-agents`, `mobile-agents`, `computer-use`, `vision-language-models`, `rl`

## [Metis: Memory Foundation Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26760)
**Authors:** MemTensor / Metis Group
**TL;DR:** Metis introduces the first prototype of a memory foundation model that gives LLMs native memory capabilities via a persistent, dynamically evolving memory state within the backbone—replacing external retrieval modules. Native memory is formalized through Fast Weight Programming-inspired architecture, enabling gradient-free online memory maintenance with only a forward pass, and outperforms external memory baselines on complex memory-based QA tasks.
**Tags:** `agents`, `memory`, `foundation-models`, `llm`, `architecture`

## [TurboVLA: Real-Time Vision-Language-Action Model at 32 Hz on an RTX 4090 with <1 GB VRAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27205)
**Authors:** (Submitted July 29, 2026)
**TL;DR:** TurboVLA introduces an efficient VLA architecture that independently encodes visual observations and language instructions, then connects them via lightweight bidirectional vision-language interaction and a compact action-chunk decoder—achieving 97.7% success on LIBERO with only 0.2B parameters, 31.2 ms latency, and under 1 GB VRAM on consumer hardware. This challenges the prevailing LLM-centric VLA paradigm by showing that compact architectures can match or exceed large-model performance at a fraction of the compute cost.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `vision-language-action`, `vla`, `efficiency`, `manipulation`

## [A Comprehensive Survey of Self-Evolving AI Agents: A New Paradigm Bridging Foundation Models and Lifelong Agentic Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07407)
**Authors:** Jinyuan Fang, Yanwen Peng, Xi Zhang, Yingxue Wang, Xinhao Yi, Guibin Zhang, Yi Xu, Bin Wu, Siwei Liu, Zihao Li, Zhaochun Ren, Nikos Aletras, Xi Wang, Han Zhou, Zaiqiao Meng
**TL;DR:** This survey introduces a unified conceptual framework abstracting the feedback loop underlying self-evolving agentic systems—agents that automatically enhance themselves via interaction data and environmental feedback rather than relying on static, manually crafted configurations. It surveys the landscape of agent evolution techniques, covering memory, planning, tool use, and self-modification, and discusses open challenges for lifelong agentic systems built on foundation models.
**Tags:** `agents`, `self-evolving`, `survey`, `llm`, `lifelong-learning`, `memory`

## [LLM Collaboration With Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04652)
**Authors:** Shuo Liu, Tianle Chen, Zeyu Liang, Xueguang Lyu, Christopher Amato
**TL;DR:** This paper models LLM collaboration as a cooperative Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL) problem and proposes Multi-Agent Group Relative Policy Optimization (MAGRPO), a multi-agent multi-turn RL algorithm that incentivizes coordination through team-level rewards rather than per-agent reward design. Experiments on writing and coding collaboration tasks show MAGRPO enables higher-quality responses than independently fine-tuned baselines.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `reinforcement-learning`, `marl`, `llm`, `fine-tuning`

## [Embodied-R1.5: Evolving Physical Intelligence via Embodied Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11324)
**Authors:** Embodied-R Team
**TL;DR:** Embodied-R1.5 is a unified Embodied Foundation Model (EFM) integrating embodied cognition, task planning, correction, and visual grounding within a single 8B-parameter architecture, trained with 15B+ tokens via automated pipelines and a multi-task balanced RL recipe. It achieves SOTA on 16/24 embodied VLM benchmarks (surpassing Gemini-Robotics-ER-1.5 and GPT-5.4) and can be fine-tuned into a VLA that outperforms π₀.₅ across manipulation suites via a Planner-Grounder-Corrector closed-loop framework.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `foundation-models`, `vla`, `reinforcement-learning`, `manipulation`

## [Qwen-AgentWorld: Language World Models for General Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24597)
**Authors:** Qwen Team (Alibaba)
**TL;DR:** Qwen-AgentWorld introduces a unified language world model that enables general-purpose agents to simulate environment dynamics in natural language, allowing agents to plan and explore without executing every candidate action in the real environment. The approach improves agent performance on long-horizon decision-making and reduces the number of costly real-world interactions needed.
**Tags:** `agents`, `world-models`, `planning`, `llm`, `decision-making`

## [Safe Embodied AI for Long-horizon Tasks: A Cross-layer Analysis of Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05660)
**Authors:** (Submitted June 2026)
**TL;DR:** This paper provides a cross-layer analysis of safety in embodied AI systems executing long-horizon robotic manipulation tasks, examining failure modes across perception, planning, and actuation layers. It proposes mitigation strategies at each layer and demonstrates that layered safety interventions significantly reduce error propagation during complex manipulation sequences.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `safety`, `robotics`, `manipulation`, `long-horizon`

## [Outplaying Elite Table Tennis Players with an Autonomous Robot (Project Ace)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10338-5)
**Authors:** Sony AI Research Team
**TL;DR:** Sony AI's Project Ace presents the first autonomous system to compete with and beat elite and professional table tennis players under official rules, using an 8-joint robot arm, a 200 Hz multi-camera event-sensor perception rig, and a model-free RL policy that selects strokes in real time with 10.2 ms average latency. In March 2026 matches, Ace defeated all three professional opponents at least once—marking the first known real-world AI competitive with professional humans in a fast-paced physical sport.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `reinforcement-learning`, `physical-ai`, `sports-robotics`

## [Adaptive Orchestration: Scalable Self-Evolving Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09742)
**Authors:** (Submitted January 2026)
**TL;DR:** This paper proposes an adaptive orchestration framework for multi-agent systems that dynamically expands agent pools by generating familiarization tasks and building routing memory, enabling safe monotonic improvement without degrading existing capabilities. The approach addresses the challenge of scaling MAS without catastrophic forgetting or coordination collapse as new agents are added.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `orchestration`, `self-evolving`, `scalability`

## [HAS-Bench: Evaluating LLM-Based Human-Agent Systems under Configurable Human Participation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04329)
**Authors:** (Submitted July 2026)
**TL;DR:** HAS-Bench introduces a benchmark for evaluating human-agent collaboration systems by parameterizing the degree of human participation across tasks—enabling controlled measurement of how LLM-based agents perform as human oversight increases or decreases. Results reveal a nuanced trade-off: higher human participation improves task accuracy but reduces throughput, with current agents struggling to optimally adapt to varying collaboration modes.
**Tags:** `agents`, `human-agent-interaction`, `benchmark`, `evaluation`, `llm`
