# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-08-31)

## [Security of Foundation-Model-Powered Embodied Agents: Attack Surfaces, Attacks, Defenses, and Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16843v1)
**Authors:** Jiawei Liu, Jiacheng Guo, Tian Zhang, Yiwei Xu, Juan Wang, Jinlin Fan, Bowen Xiao (Wuhan University)
**TL;DR:** A systematic security survey of embodied agents powered by foundation models, organizing risks across five system layers and twelve attack surfaces spanning the full pipeline from model supply chain to execution control. Based on 58 attack records and 61 defense records collected through August 15, 2026, the paper analyzes cross-layer attack propagation and defense placement, providing the first comprehensive evaluation framework for this threat surface.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `security`, `adversarial`, `agents`, `foundation-models`

## [Towards the Harness of Embodied Agents (Thea)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11246)
**Authors:** EIT-HAI Research Group
**TL;DR:** Thea is an agentic harness that wraps robot capabilities as callable tools orchestrated by an LLM loop, asking whether the coding-agent infrastructure paradigm extends to embodied systems. It introduces Scene Graph as Context (a persistent symbolic world representation) and Evaluation as Exit Codes (to detect action completion and diagnose failures), enabling long-horizon task completion in real physical environments through tool composition.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `agents`, `harness`, `scene-graph`

## [Self-Evolving Embodied Agents via Skill-Harness Evolution (SHAPER)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11350)
**Authors:** Peidong Wang, Zhiming Ma, Ying Chang, Xufang Luo, Xiaocui Yang, Shi Feng, Yuqing Yang, Dongsheng Li (Microsoft Research, Northeastern University)
**TL;DR:** SHAPER is a train-free embodied adaptation framework that keeps model parameters frozen while evolving reusable skills and a context-code harness through environment rollouts. The same frozen foundation model serves as both planner and optimizer, improving the non-parametric agent system incrementally without any gradient updates—a significant step toward zero-shot embodied generalization.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `agents`, `skill-learning`, `self-evolving`, `robotics`

## [OneDayAgent: Towards a Long-Horizon Harness for Autonomous Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05013)
**Authors:** Jingsheng Zheng, Xinyuan Fang, et al.
**TL;DR:** OneDayAgent tackles long-horizon, cross-environment, multimodal tasks by decomposing open-ended requests into bounded subtasks, maintaining execution memory under context pressure, and verifying/repairing final deliverables. Evaluated on the AgentIF-OneDay benchmark (104 tasks), it achieves a new state-of-the-art score of 0.821 with the GLM-5.2 backend and generalizes across five LLM families without task-specific tuning.
**Tags:** `agents`, `long-horizon`, `harness`, `multimodal`, `autonomous`

## [SKILL.state: Scalable Long-Horizon Agent Skills](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26263)
**Authors:** Sanket Badhe, Priyanka Tiwari, Jonghyun Chung
**TL;DR:** SKILL.state replaces append-only conversational history with a mutable structured execution state, providing the model only the immutable skill spec, current state, and latest observation at each step—discarding intermediate reasoning immediately after use. This yields O(T) cumulative token cost, a 16.2× token reduction on synthetic long-horizon benchmarks, and double-digit accuracy gains on real tasks including Linux CTF exploitation and customer-service workflows. Accepted at EMNLP.
**Tags:** `agents`, `long-horizon`, `memory`, `efficiency`, `skill-learning`

## [Breaking Planner Integrity Boundary: Environment State-Text Injection Attack on LLM-Driven Embodied Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16806)
**Authors:** (August 2026 submission)
**TL;DR:** This paper identifies a novel attack surface in LLM-driven embodied agents: malicious text injected into environment state observations can hijack the planner's reasoning and redirect agent actions. The work demonstrates end-to-end exploits in simulated embodied environments and proposes defense mechanisms based on state sanitization and planner integrity checking.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `security`, `adversarial`, `prompt-injection`, `agents`

## [Towards Embodied Agentic AI: Review and Classification of LLM- and VLM-Driven Robot Autonomy and Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05294)
**Authors:** (August 2026 survey)
**TL;DR:** A comprehensive review of how foundation models—LLMs, VLMs, and vision-language-action (VLA) models—have reshaped robot autonomy, classifying approaches by perception, reasoning, and action modalities. The paper surveys large behavior models and emerging paradigms like multimodal teleoperation, physical simulation transfer, and embodied world models, providing a taxonomy for the rapidly expanding field.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `survey`, `robotics`, `VLA`, `foundation-models`

## [A Two-Tier Perspective on Inference-Time Parallelism in Multi-Agent LLM Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05791)
**Authors:** Zihan Xu, Haolin Tian, Hai Jiang
**TL;DR:** This work (accepted at ICML 2026) formalizes inference-time parallelism in multi-agent LLM pipelines at two levels: within-agent (parallel tool calls, parallel reasoning chains) and across-agent (concurrent agent execution). Theoretical analysis and empirical results show how parallelism interacts with task dependencies, providing guidelines for maximizing throughput in agentic workflows without sacrificing correctness.
**Tags:** `multi-agent`, `inference`, `parallelism`, `LLM`, `efficiency`

## [OpenLife: Toward Open-World Artificial Life with Autonomous LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.31046v1)
**Authors:** (2026)
**TL;DR:** OpenLife runs six LLM agents in an open world for approximately twelve weeks, observing emergent behaviors including a shift from reactive to spontaneous activity, formation of social structures, and self-directed goal-setting without explicit reward signals. The paper argues that open-world artificial life (ALIFE) is now a viable experimental paradigm for studying emergent intelligence, social dynamics, and long-horizon agency.
**Tags:** `agents`, `artificial-life`, `emergent-behavior`, `multi-agent`, `open-world`

## [Adversarial Attacks in Multi-Agent LLM Pipelines: Unveiling Structural Vulnerabilities in Agentic AI Architectures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16843)
**Authors:** (Accepted at IEEE GLOBECOM 2026)
**TL;DR:** An investigation of how structural properties of multi-agent LLM pipelines—agent topology, trust chains, and message routing—create new attack surfaces beyond single-agent prompt injection. The paper demonstrates attacks that propagate adversarial instructions across agent boundaries and evaluates architectural defenses including message signing, role-locked agents, and sandboxed tool execution.
**Tags:** `multi-agent`, `security`, `adversarial`, `LLM`, `agents`

## [Harnessing the Collective Intelligence of AI Agents in the Wild for New Discoveries](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.10402)
**Authors:** (2026)
**TL;DR:** This paper studies how collectives of AI agents—each with partial information and limited individual capability—can coordinate to make scientific discoveries beyond the reach of any single agent. Field experiments across chemistry, biology, and materials science tasks show that structured agent collectives with memory sharing and debate protocols outperform both single large models and unstructured ensembles.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `scientific-discovery`, `collective-intelligence`, `LLM`
