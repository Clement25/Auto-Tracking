# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-10-26)

## [EpiSEM-Bench: Evaluating Safety and Evidence Recovery in LLM Agents under Missing Evidence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31650)
**Authors:** Qijin Zhang, Hanzhen Wang, Yuehan Zhao, et al.
**TL;DR:** A new benchmark that tests whether tool-using LLM agents correctly avoid prohibited actions when tool outputs omit safety-relevant facts. It exposes a critical failure mode where agents act on incomplete evidence, raising concerns about deploying LLM agents in high-stakes workflows.
**Tags:** `agents`, `safety`, `benchmarks`, `tool-use`, `llm`

---

## [Same Luck, Different Choice: Twin-World Credit Assignment for Agentic Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31544)
**Authors:** Zeropy Surio, Tobias Engel, Mei-Lin Chou, et al.
**TL;DR:** Proposes a counterfactual credit assignment method for agentic RL that shares sampling noise across decision branches, reducing biased credit estimates. The twin-world construction yields more reliable policy gradient estimates for long-horizon agent tasks.
**Tags:** `agents`, `reinforcement-learning`, `credit-assignment`, `agentic-rl`

---

## [METTA: Memory-Enhanced Test-Time Adaptation for Meta-Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31656)
**Authors:** Ali Najar, Hosein Hasani, Amir Kooshan Fattah, et al.
**TL;DR:** A memory-based framework that allows RL agents to adapt faster to unseen tasks from limited interactions at test time. METTA decouples task representation from policy execution, enabling rapid in-context adaptation without retraining.
**Tags:** `agents`, `reinforcement-learning`, `meta-learning`, `memory`, `adaptation`

---

## [DuoCook: An Overcooked-Inspired Benchmark for Visual Planning and Coordination](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31511)
**Authors:** Ziyi Jiao, Heng Zhang, Drishti Regmi, et al.
**TL;DR:** A new Unreal Engine benchmark testing vision-language agents on dual-player cooking coordination under time limits. DuoCook requires agents to jointly plan and communicate, exposing bottlenecks in multimodal multi-agent coordination.
**Tags:** `agents`, `multi-agent`, `vision-language`, `benchmarks`, `embodied-ai`

## [Sensorimotor Alignment: A Platonic Proxy for Embodied Policy Success](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31670)
**Authors:** Yunfei Xie, Howard H. Qian, Shiyi Lan, et al.
**TL;DR:** Proposes an offline proxy metric based on vision-language sensorimotor alignment that predicts whether a robotic foundation model will succeed in closed-loop deployment — without requiring expensive real-world rollouts. The metric correlates strongly with actual policy performance across diverse manipulation tasks.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `evaluation`, `vision-language`, `foundation-models`
