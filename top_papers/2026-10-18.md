# Top Papers — AI Agents & Embodied AI (2026-10-18)

## [Harness²: Recursive Refinement of Agent Execution Harnesses](https://huggingface.co/papers/trending)
**Authors:** Google DeepMind Team
**TL;DR:** Proposes a two-level learning framework where an LLM agent refines its own execution harness at both the task level (recursive refinement) and harness level (continual learning). Achieves significant gains in agentic task completion by letting the system self-improve the scaffolding that drives it.
**Tags:** `agents`, `self-improvement`, `execution-harness`, `llm`

---

## [Agent-Reflex: Training Language Agents to Recover from Noisy Tool Feedback](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.MA/current)
**Authors:** RUC-NLPIR Lab
**TL;DR:** Introduces a training methodology that teaches LLM-based agents to detect and recover from unreliable or noisy feedback from external tools. Demonstrates robustness improvements across multi-step tool-use benchmarks where prior agents would silently fail.
**Tags:** `agents`, `tool-use`, `robustness`, `training`

---

## [OpenGCUA: An Open Generalist Computer-Use Agent](https://www.alphaxiv.org/)
**Authors:** Tongyi-MAI (Alibaba Research)
**TL;DR:** Presents an open-source generalist agent capable of operating software interfaces across diverse desktop and web environments. Benchmarks show competitive performance with closed proprietary agents on GUI navigation and multi-step computer tasks.
**Tags:** `agents`, `computer-use`, `gui-navigation`, `open-source`

---

## [AutoSFT: An Agentic System for Automated Supervised Fine-Tuning Data Research](https://www.alphaxiv.org/)
**Authors:** AutoSFT Team
**TL;DR:** Builds a fully automated pipeline where an LLM agent designs, generates, and filters SFT datasets without human curation. Shows that agent-sourced SFT data can match or exceed manually curated datasets on downstream task benchmarks.
**Tags:** `agents`, `sft`, `data-curation`, `llm-training`

---

## [Terminal-RSI: Recursive Self-Improving Terminal Agents](https://www.alphaxiv.org/)
**Authors:** Independent researchers
**TL;DR:** Explores a framework for terminal-environment agents that iteratively rewrite their own action policies to improve task performance. Raises important safety considerations around containment and goal-drift in self-modifying agentic systems.
**Tags:** `agents`, `self-improvement`, `safety`, `recursive`

---

## [Forms of LLM-Integrated Applications from LLM-Chats to Autonomous AI Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11899)
**Authors:** Survey authors (arXiv 2610.11899)
**TL;DR:** A systematic taxonomy that maps the design space from simple LLM chatbots to fully autonomous multi-agent systems, categorizing integration patterns, control flows, and autonomy levels. Serves as a unifying framework for understanding the growing diversity of LLM-powered application architectures.
**Tags:** `agents`, `survey`, `taxonomy`, `llm-applications`

---

## [Security Analysis of LLM-Orchestrator Tool Use: 253 Bugs Across 118 MCP Servers](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.MA/current)
**Authors:** Security research team
**TL;DR:** Evaluates 273 open-source MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers encompassing 3,405 tools and uncovers 253 verified semantic security vulnerabilities in 118 servers, including trust boundary violations between orchestrators and tools. Provides concrete attack taxonomies and calls for standardized security auditing in agentic pipelines.
**Tags:** `agents`, `security`, `mcp`, `tool-use`, `vulnerabilities`

---

## [Thinker-WM: A Unified World Model with Shared Multimodal Representations for Embodied Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current)
**Authors:** Thinker-WM Team
**TL;DR:** Proposes a world model that unifies perception, memory, and planning through shared multimodal representations, enabling embodied agents to reason about physical environments more coherently. Evaluated on embodied navigation and manipulation benchmarks with state-of-the-art performance on several tasks.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `world-models`, `multimodal`, `robotics`

---

## [LIVIN: Benchmarking Spatial and Embodied Intelligence in Digital Twins of Lived-In Homes](https://github.com/Ponkux/DailyArXiv-cp/issues/587)
**Authors:** LIVIN Benchmark Authors
**TL;DR:** Introduces a benchmark built from high-fidelity digital twins of real homes, testing agents on spatial reasoning, object permanence, and multi-room navigation in everyday lived-in environments. Reveals significant gaps between current VLMs and human-level spatial intelligence.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `benchmark`, `spatial-reasoning`, `digital-twin`

---

## [OmniDex: Scaling Dexterous Hand Grasping to Diverse Cluttered Scenes](https://github.com/Ponkux/DailyArXiv-cp/issues/586)
**Authors:** OmniDex Team
**TL;DR:** Presents a framework for training dexterous robotic hands to grasp objects reliably across highly cluttered and varied real-world scenes. Leverages large-scale simulation data and domain randomization to achieve generalizable dexterous manipulation policies.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `dexterous-manipulation`, `grasping`

---

## [JitterVLA: A Replay Benchmark for Motion Quality in VLA-Driven Human-Robot Manipulation](https://github.com/Ponkux/DailyArXiv-cp/issues/592)
**Authors:** JitterVLA Team
**TL;DR:** Identifies and quantifies motion jitter as a critical but under-studied failure mode in vision-language-action (VLA) models driving robotic manipulation. Proposes a replay-based evaluation protocol and smoothing techniques that substantially reduce jitter without degrading task success.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `robotics`, `vla`, `manipulation`, `motion-quality`

---

## [Embodied AI in Action: Insights from SAE World Congress 2026 on Safety, Trust, Robotics, and Real-World Deployment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10653)
**Authors:** SAE World Congress 2026 contributors
**TL;DR:** Synthesizes findings from the SAE World Congress 2026 on critical challenges in deploying embodied AI in real-world settings, covering safety frameworks, trust calibration, and the gap between laboratory robotics and real deployment conditions. Highlights regulatory and engineering open problems as the field moves toward commercial embodied AI.
**Tags:** `embodied-ai`, `safety`, `deployment`, `robotics`, `survey`
