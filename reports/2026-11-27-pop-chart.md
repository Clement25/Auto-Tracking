## Highlights

### US
Ella Langley's country-pop crossover "Choosin' Texas" continues its extraordinary run, logging a 29th consecutive week at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 — one of the longest streaks in recent chart history. The holiday season is officially beginning its annual takeover, with Mariah Carey's perennial "All I Want for Christmas Is You" climbing back into the top five. Tame Impala and JENNIE's dark-pop collaboration "Dracula" represents one of the most commercially successful K-pop crossover efforts of the year, while Olivia Rodrigo's self-deprecating "Stupid Song" continues to resonate with Gen Z audiences. Tate McRae ("Housewife"), Karol G ("BbY WOW"), and ADELA ("Nicole Kidman") round out a diverse, genre-blending top ten.

### EU
The UK chart is dominated by emotional pop ballads this week. Jamie Miller's "Babydoll," a swooping, heartbreak-driven track, has now spent 13 weeks on the chart and maintains its hold at No. 1 through the week ending November 26. Ashe's anthemic "Stop The Wedding!" holds strong at No. 2, with Spotify UK daily plays reaching 355,566. Alternative-pop act sombr breaks into the top three with "My Body Isn't Ready," while Tate McRae's "Housewife" — also charting in the US — demonstrates strong transatlantic appeal. Christmas songs, led by Wham!'s "Last Christmas," are beginning to make their seasonal appearance in the lower reaches of the top 40.

### CN
China's charts reflect a mix of nostalgia, OST tie-ins, and K-pop crossover influence this week. BESTie's re-released 2013 single "Love Options" surged to No. 1 on QQ Music Hot Songs on November 27, riding a wave of K-pop retro enthusiasm and also charting on the Circle and Melon charts. Xu Song's "Tian Long Ba Bu Zhi Su Di," linked to the popular drama adaptation of Jin Yong's classic novel, was one of only two tracks to simultaneously chart on six major Chinese streaming platforms — a feat that underscores the enduring power of drama OSTs in mainland China. Stefanie Sun continues to hold ground on NetEase Cloud Music, while Taiwanese singer DIOR 大颖 leads the broader Taiwan & China Cpop aggregated chart with her introspective ballad "欲落未落."
