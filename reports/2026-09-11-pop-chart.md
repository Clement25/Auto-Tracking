## Highlights

### US

Ella Langley's "Choosin' Texas" continues its extraordinary reign atop the Billboard Hot 100, reaching its 21st week at #1 and placing it within one week of tying the all-time record for longest chart-topper in history. The country-pop crossover anthem has cemented itself as the undisputed Song of the Summer 2026. Stella Lefty's debut single "Boston" holds strong at #2 for a second consecutive week, marking one of the most impressive new artist entrances of the year. Taylor Swift's "I Knew It, I Knew You" — the lead single from the Toy Story 5 soundtrack — locked in at #3 after debuting at #1 in late August, while Ariana Grande's album cut "Petal" (from her critically praised LP of the same name, released July 31) climbed to #4, with the full album charting all 12 tracks simultaneously on the Hot 100. Morgan Wallen rounds out the top 5 with the country smash "Been By Now."

### EU

The UK chart for the week ending September 10, 2026 saw Ella Langley's "Choosin' Texas" cross the Atlantic and claim the top spot in Britain as well, making it one of the rare country crossovers to top both US and UK charts simultaneously. Newcomer Sienna Spiro surged to #2 with the indie-pop anthem "Great Expectation," becoming the UK's breakout story of early autumn. The house-infused "Movin' To The Sun" by Hugel featuring Imael Angel and Ultra Naté continued its summer dance run at #3, while Stella Lefty's "Boston" mirrored its US success at #4. In a nostalgic twist, Dolly Parton's "9 To 5" returned to the top 10 at #5 following a viral moment on social media.

### CN

The QQ Music 热歌榜 (Hot Songs Chart) for the week of September 8, 2026 was dominated by 马嘉祺 (Ma Jiaqi), the popular C-pop idol and TFBoys member, who swept the top 3 spots following the release of a new multi-single project. "冰箱没有鸡蛋" (Fridge Has No Eggs) leads the chart with a playful, emotional style resonating with younger Chinese audiences, while "穿帮" and "潮汐锁定" (Tidal Locking) further showcase his versatility in R&B and synth-pop respectively. Singapore-born Mandopop veteran 孙燕姿 (Stephanie Sun) appeared at #4 with the mid-tempo ballad "我不难过" (I'm Not Sad), reaffirming her enduring cross-generational appeal. Taiwanese vocalist 李佳薇 (Jess Lee) closes the top 5 with the relatable breakup anthem "甲乙丙丁 (你我怎么两清)."
