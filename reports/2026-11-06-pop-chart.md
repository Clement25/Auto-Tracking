## Highlights

### US
Ella Langley's "Choosin' Texas" continues its extraordinary run at No. 1, now entering its 26th consecutive week atop the Billboard Hot 100 — a record-extending milestone for 2026. Country-pop crossover dominates the top tier, with Morgan Wallen's "Been By Now" at No. 4 reinforcing the genre's commercial power. A standout mover is "Dracula" by indie rock legend Tame Impala in collaboration with BLACKPINK's JENNIE, surging to No. 5 on a post-Halloween streaming bump — an unexpected pairing that blends psychedelic pop with K-pop gloss. Tate McRae's "Housewife" holds strong at No. 2, cementing her as one of 2026's most consistent chart performers.

### EU
The UK chart this week is defined by a mix of breakout UK talent and globally touring acts. Jamie Miller claims No. 1 with "Babydoll," his biggest chart achievement to date, riding a wave of TikTok virality and UK radio support. Tate McRae crosses over to both sides of the Atlantic at No. 2. Ashe's "Stop The Wedding!" at No. 3 continues her strong European rollout — the indie-pop breakup anthem has been embraced particularly in Nordic markets. The collaborative "Expectations" between Olivia Rodrigo and Chappell Roan is a critical darling and commercial hit, reflecting the ongoing alt-pop boom driven by female artists in 2026.

### CN
China's TME UNI chart reflects strong domestic pop and C-pop trends. 梓渝 (Ziyu)'s emotional ballad "致亲爱的自己" (Dear Myself) leads as a deeply resonant introspective song embraced across social streaming platforms. 周深 (Zhou Shen) maintains consistent chart presence with "大梦归," his signature atmospheric vocal style connecting with broad Chinese audiences. 刘宇宁 (Liu Yuning) dominates the lower half of the top 5 with two entries — "她" and "折月亮" — demonstrating his remarkable popularity in the C-pop ballad space. TF家族 member 张函瑞 (Zhang Hanrui) brings a younger, idol-pop energy with "Baby's Horizon," appealing to the fan-driven streaming audience on platforms like QQ Music and Kugou.
