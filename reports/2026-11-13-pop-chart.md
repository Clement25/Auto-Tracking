## Highlights

### US
Ella Langley's country-pop crossover hit "Choosin' Texas" continues its extraordinary reign atop the Billboard Hot 100, now in its 27th week at number one — cementing its status as the longest-running No. 1 in chart history, surpassing Mariah Carey's holiday perennial. The song, the lead single from her album *Dandelion*, first went viral on TikTok in late 2025. A surprise entry this week: Michael Jackson's classic "Thriller" jumped 18 positions to reach a new all-time peak at No. 2, likely boosted by Halloween season residual streams. Meanwhile, the Tame Impala and JENNIE collaboration "Dracula" continues to climb with a new peak at No. 3, showcasing the global appeal of K-pop crossover projects.

### EU
The UK chart this week is dominated by home-grown talent. Sam Fender and Olivia Dean's indie-folk duet "Rein Me In" is enjoying its 12th non-consecutive week at No. 1, a remarkable run for a British act that speaks to its sustained streaming and radio performance. Drake's "Janice STFU" enters at No. 2, proving his transatlantic pull despite being primarily a US artist. On Spotify UK, Olivia Dean also leads midweek streaming charts independently with "Man I Need," making her one of the most dominant presences on the UK chart this period. Taylor Swift's "The Fate of Ophelia" leads UK radio airplay, and RAYE's characteristically raw and confessional "WHERE IS MY HUSBAND!" remains a fixture in both streams and airplay.

### CN
The Chinese market is seeing a blend of nationalist folk-pop and emotionally resonant ballads dominate the charts. Lóng Yǒulín's "龙耀华夏" (Glory of the Dragon Nation), a sweeping orchestral patriotic anthem, holds the top position on the national 全网热歌榜 aggregate chart. Beloved vocalist Zhou Shen (周深) appears twice — with the melodic dream-ballad "大梦归" and the inspirational "自己就是自己的光" — reflecting his sustained popularity across demographics. Xiao Zhan (肖战), one of China's biggest idol-actors, charts with the upbeat "异想天开." On the Sina 华语音乐传媒榜, the tandem collaboration "天地志" by Tan Wei Wei and Tan Jian Ci leads the mainland category, blending traditional Chinese lyrical aesthetics with modern production.
