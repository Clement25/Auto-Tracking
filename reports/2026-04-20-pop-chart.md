## Highlights

### US
Country-influenced pop continues to dominate the Hot 100 in mid-April 2026, with Ella Langley's "Choosin' Texas" holding the #1 spot for its sixth non-consecutive week — making it the year's longest-running number one. Olivia Dean is having a breakthrough crossover moment, landing at #2 with "Man I Need." Bruno Mars' "I Just Might" marks another top-3 debut for the perennial chart favorite, while BTS' "Swim" — the lead single from their comeback album — became the group's seventh song ever to debut at #1, now settled at #5.

### EU
The UK chart this week showcases a strong indie-alternative tilt. The Sam Fender and Olivia Dean collaboration "Rein Me In" claims the top spot, reflecting Fender's growing mainstream pull. Tame Impala's "Dracula" at #2 signals the Australian psych-rock project's continued grip on European audiences. Alex Warren appears in both the US and EU charts with different tracks ("Ordinary" stateside, "Fever Dream" in the UK), suggesting a genuine transatlantic breakthrough. Dominic Fike places two songs in the top 7, underscoring his post-hiatus commercial resurgence.

### CN
Chinese streaming and short-video platforms are driving a diverse mix of homegrown and K-pop crossover hits in April 2026. 潮汐老李's "孟婆嫌我烦 (奈何桥头又碰面)" — a melodic Mandopop track referencing underworld mythology — is leading both iTunes China and Douyin virality. Zhou Shen (周深), known for his remarkable countertenor range, continues to chart with "和光同尘," a sweeping ballad. Jay Chou remains a constant presence with "那天下雨了." Meanwhile, K-pop act Choi Ye-na's "Catch Catch" has amassed over 250 million Douyin challenge views, reflecting the deep K-pop/C-pop crossover ecosystem driven by short-form video.
