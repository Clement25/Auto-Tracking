## Highlights

### US
Ella Langley continues her historic dominance of the Billboard Hot 100, occupying three spots in the top five simultaneously — a feat rarely seen in the modern streaming era. "Choosin' Texas" claims its 13th week at #1, cementing Langley as the breakout artist of 2026. Taylor Swift holds steady at #2 with "I Knew It, I Knew You," while the genre-bending collaboration "Dracula" by Tame Impala and JENNIE (of BLACKPINK) continues to climb, reflecting growing crossover appeal between indie psychedelic pop and K-pop fan bases.

### EU
The UK chart makes history this week: Sam Fender & Olivia Dean's "Rein Me In" hits its 19th week at #1, breaking a 73-year-old record to become the longest-running Number 1 single in UK chart history. The emotionally resonant indie-folk duet has captured British listeners through the summer, defying typical chart turnover. Meanwhile, Shakira's afrobeats collaboration with Burna Boy, "Dai Dai," surged to #2 mid-week and is poised to challenge for the top spot in coming weeks. The transatlantic popularity of Ella Langley's "Choosin' Texas" crossing into the UK top 5 underscores the growing influence of US country-pop globally.

### CN
BLACKPINK's Jennie launched "Less than a Lover" on July 24 and immediately swept to #1 on all three of China's largest music platforms — QQ Music, NetEase Cloud Music, and KuGou Music — within hours of release, also earning a Gold certification on QQ Music. The song charted #1 in 27 regions globally, marking the strongest K-pop solo debut of 2026. Domestic Mandopop continues strong with emotional ballads dominating: 马也_Crabbit's "海屿你" captured NetEase listeners with its melancholic ocean imagery, and 郑润泽's "于是" has sustained remarkable longevity on streaming charts thanks to its resonance with younger urban audiences.
