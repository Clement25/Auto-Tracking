## Highlights

### US
Ella Langley's country-pop crossover "Choosin' Texas" remains an unstoppable force, entering its 17th consecutive week at #1 on the Billboard Hot 100 — one of the longest chart reigns of 2026. Ariana Grande makes a strong double appearance this week, with "Hate That I Made You Love Me" holding firm at #2 and her new single "Petal" debuting impressively at #4. Morgan Wallen maintains his country-pop dominance at #3 with "Been By Now," while Taylor Swift's emotional indie-pop track "I Knew It, I Knew You" continues to build momentum at #5.

### EU
The UK chart sees a new #1 this week as the indie-folk duet "Rein Me In" by Sam Fender & Olivia Dean climbs to the top spot, reflecting a broader European appetite for introspective, guitar-driven pop. Dance-pop collaboration "Movin' to the Sun" by Hugel, Imael Angel, and Ultra Naté holds at #2, driven by summer festival plays across Continental Europe. American crossover hit "Choosin' Texas" by Ella Langley continues to chart strongly in the EU at #3, while Olivia Dean scores a second entry with the breezy "So Easy (To Fall In Love)" at #4. Tame Impala and JENNIE's hypnotic psych-pop collab "Dracula" rounds out the top 5, fueled by massive Spotify streams across Europe and K-pop fandoms.

### CN
Li Jiawei leads the Chinese charts this week with "甲乙丙丁 (Jia Yi Bing Ding)," a melodic Mandopop ballad resonating widely across QQ Music and Douyin short-video platforms. A heartfelt duet "全世界的雨 (All the Rain in the World)" by veteran singers Zhou Shen and Ella Chen continues its high charting at #2, blending soft vocals with atmospheric production. Cross-regional collaboration "过海 (Crossing the Sea)" by Wang Heye and Huang Ling brings an emotional narrative style popular among younger listeners at #3. Huang Qibin's sweeping ballad "绵绵无绝期 (Endless Longing)" climbs the NetEase charts at #4, while Liu Yuning's easy-listening "来我的岛 (Come to My Island)" rounds out the top 5 with strong summer playlist placements.
