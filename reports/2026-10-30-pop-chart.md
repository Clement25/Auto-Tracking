## Highlights

### US
The Billboard Hot 100 chart dated October 31, 2026 sees Ella Langley's country crossover smash "Choosin' Texas" reach a record-breaking 25th week at No. 1, surpassing Mariah Carey's legendary 22-week record set by "All I Want for Christmas Is You." Three major debuts join the top five: Tate McRae's empowering pop anthem "Housewife" (released October 15, debuting at her career-high No. 2), the Olivia Rodrigo and Chappell Roan powerhouse collab "Expectations" soaring 60 places to No. 3, and Zach Bryan and Lana Del Rey's atmospheric country-pop ballad "Lies" landing at No. 4. Taylor Swift's "Patient Zero" falls to No. 5 after three weeks atop the chart, keeping the top five dominated by female-led artists and country-adjacent sounds.

### EU
The UK Official Singles Chart for the week of October 23–29 is led by Jamie Miller's moody pop track "Babydoll," which has been climbing steadily after viral TikTok momentum. Tate McRae's "Housewife" makes a simultaneous high-charting debut on both sides of the Atlantic, confirming the Canadian singer-songwriter's global reach. The trans-Atlantic collab "Expectations" by Olivia Rodrigo and Chappell Roan stormed the UK chart from No. 35 to No. 3, riding heavy streaming gains. British indie-pop duo Sam Fender & Olivia Dean's tender duet "Rein Me In" holds firm in the top five with approximately 2.4 million Spotify UK streams, reflecting strong domestic appetite for homegrown talent.

### CN
China's charts this week blend streaming nostalgia with viral short-video trends. Sun Yanzi's (孙燕姿) "我不难过" tops Apple Music China, continuing her enduring popularity in Mandopop. Jay Chou (周杰伦) classics "七里香" and "晴天" remain perennial favourites in the top streaming spots — a testament to his timeless catalog regularly trending on QQ Music and NetEase. On Douyin, the new generation is making waves: indie singer-songwriter duo Cole先生 & 马也_Crabbit's "海屿你" is a standout hit from the Malaysian-Chinese indie scene that crossed into mainland charts, while a remix of traditional opera-inspired "赤伶" continues its viral lifecycle as one of the most-used sounds on the short-video platform.
