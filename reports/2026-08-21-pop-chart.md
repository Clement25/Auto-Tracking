## Highlights

### US
Country-pop crossover continues to dominate the US charts: Ella Langley's "Choosin' Texas" sits at its 17th consecutive week at #1 on the Billboard Hot 100, an extraordinary run that reflects the song's broad appeal and heavy radio rotation. Ariana Grande makes a striking double entry this week with "Hate That I Made You Love Me" at #2 and "Petal" at #4 — both tracks from her latest album are driving major streaming numbers, signaling one of the strongest multi-song chart weeks she's ever had. Morgan Wallen and Taylor Swift round out the top 5, confirming country-leaning and crossover pop as the dominant sounds of summer 2026.

### EU
The UK chart this week reflects a rich diversity of styles. British indie-folk duo Sam Fender & Olivia Dean score the top spot with "Rein Me In," a heartfelt collaboration that's resonated deeply with UK audiences since its release. Global superstar Shakira teams with Afrobeats giant Burna Boy on "Dai Dai" at #2, continuing Europe's embrace of Afropop fusions. Ella Langley's US #1 "Choosin' Texas" also crosses the Atlantic strongly at #3, while Hugel's dance banger "Movin' to the Sun" and Olivia Rodrigo's "Stupid Song" represent the pop/dance contingent. The presence of Madonna & Kylie Minogue's "Relove Sensation" at the top of the singles sales chart (physical/download) shows that legacy pop queens still move units.

### CN
Chinese pop this week is marked by emotive, cinematic ballads dominating QQ Music. Zhou Shen and Taiwanese singer Ella Chen's cross-strait duet "全世界的雨" (All the Rain in the World) leads the QQ Music global chart, blending crystalline falsetto with lush orchestration in a style that typifies mainstream Mandopop in 2026. Wang Heye and Huang Ling's "过海" (Crossing the Sea) offers a more dramatic narrative ballad in the #2 slot. On Douyin (China's TikTok), shorter and hookier material leads: Li Jiawei's "甲乙丙丁" went viral thanks to a catchy earworm chorus, while Huang Qibin's "绵绵无绝期" builds on emotional resonance that drives repeat listens and video creation alike. The overall CN trend points to romantic and emotional themes as the consistent chart drivers.
