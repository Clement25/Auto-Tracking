## Highlights

### US
Ella Langley's "Choosin' Texas" continues its extraordinary run at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100, now holding the top spot for a remarkable 28 weeks — cementing its place as one of the longest-charting country-pop crossover hits of the decade. Stella Lefty's "Boston" rebounds to No. 2 while Tame Impala & JENNIE's "Dracula" benefited from a Halloween boost to reach No. 3, its peak position. Tate McRae's "Housewife" holds strong at No. 4, and Olivia Rodrigo's "Stupid Song" continues its upward climb to No. 5. With holiday season approaching, Mariah Carey's "All I Want For Christmas Is You" has already re-entered the chart at No. 18.

### EU
The UK chart this week sees Morrissey's anthemic "without music the world dies" hold on for a third consecutive week at No. 1, a surprising resurgence for the legendary indie icon. Michael Kiwanuka's soulful "sing" sits close behind at No. 2. Meanwhile, Olivia Rodrigo dominates alternative charts with "Take Me Home (4 Christmas)" hitting No. 1 on both physical sales and the Official Trending Chart, signalling early holiday season momentum. On the Spotify Global chart (week of Nov 6–12), Latin pop ruled with KAROL G, Judeline and rusowsky's "BbY WOW" pulling over 33 million streams for the top spot. Jamie Miller's "Babydoll" also features prominently across both UK and streaming charts.

### CN
The Weeknd's "Hong Kong" — a track that blends English and Cantonese — topped both QQ Music and NetEase Cloud Music this week, showing remarkable crossover appeal for a Western artist in China's domestic market. Stefanie Sun's comeback single "Wo Bu Nan Guo" is performing strongly, reaching No. 2 on NetEase Hot Songs and mirroring strong Apple Music placement, driven by nostalgic Mandopop listeners. Rainie Yang's "Yu Ai" rides Douyin virality to chart across multiple platforms. Xu Song's martial-arts drama tie-in "Tian Long Ba Bu Zhi Su Di" charted on six platforms simultaneously — a cross-platform feat recorded by just two songs in the same tracking week. Regional novelty hit "慌啥子嘛" by 村口吴老二 is a rising Douyin darling, climbing quickly across streaming platforms.
