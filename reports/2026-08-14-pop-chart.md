## Highlights

### US

Country music continues its stranglehold on the Billboard Hot 100 this week. Ella Langley's "Choosin' Texas" notches its 16th consecutive week at #1, surpassing Mariah Carey & Boyz II Men's "One Sweet Day" to become one of the longest-leading Hot 100 #1s in history. Morgan Wallen's "Been By Now" debuts strongly at #2, while Ella Langley and Wallen's romantic duet "I Can't Love You Anymore" holds at #4 — meaning the pair occupy two of the top four spots simultaneously. Taylor Swift's Toy Story 5 soundtrack entry "I Knew It, I Knew You" (a former #1) stays put at #3, and breakout indie artist Stella Lefty's viral folk-pop hit "Boston" rounds out the top 5 after peaking at #3 the prior week.

### EU

The UK chart is in record-breaking territory. Sam Fender & Olivia Dean's "Rein Me In" secures its 19th week at #1, smashing Frankie Laine's 73-year-old record of 18 weeks for the longest total run at the top of the UK Singles Chart. The song originated as a TikTok phenomenon and won the fan-voted Song of the Year at the 2026 BRIT Awards. Shakira & Burna Boy's FIFA World Cup 2026 anthem "Dai Dai" holds at #2, riding lingering tournament excitement, while US country crossover "Choosin' Texas" continues its transatlantic run at #3. Dance track "Movin' to the Sun" and Olivia Rodrigo's "Stupid Song" fill out the top 5.

### CN

Animated film themes and sentimental ballads dominate the Chinese charts this week. "全世界的雨" (Rain All Over the World), the theme song from the animated film 《八仙！》 performed by Zhou Shen & Taiwanese singer Ella Chen, reigns at #1 on QQ Music's hot songs chart. Zhou Shen's signature falsetto continues to be a commercial force in Mandopop. Wang Heye & Huang Ling's duet "过海" (Across the Sea) holds at #2 with its signature folk-meets-R&B blend, and Liu Yuning's laid-back seaside anthem "来我的岛" (Come to My Island) stays at #3. Douyin (Chinese TikTok) continues to be the dominant discovery engine, with several chart entries having gone viral as short-video soundtracks before charting on streaming platforms.
