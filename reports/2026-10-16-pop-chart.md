## Highlights

### US
Taylor Swift's brand-new track "Patient Zero" scored its second consecutive week at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100, marking one of the fastest No. 1 holds of 2026 with massive streaming numbers upon release. Country crossover breakout Ella Langley's "Choosin' Texas" finally fell from its record-setting 24-week reign at the top to No. 2 — still a remarkable show of longevity. Drake and Don Toliver made a splashy debut at No. 5 with "Solar Eclipse," signaling continued hip-hop competition for pop chart supremacy.

### EU
The UK chart mirrors the US with Taylor Swift's "Patient Zero" holding the top spot for three weeks running, reflecting her global pull. Emerging UK pop act Sienna Spiro's "Great Expectation" continues to climb at No. 2, generating buzz as one of the most-watched British new artists of autumn 2026. Jamie Miller's "Babydoll" — now 8 weeks on the chart and having peaked at No. 2 in the midweek update — demonstrates impressive staying power. The chart shows a blend of American pop dominance alongside rising homegrown UK talent.

### CN
China's chart scene in mid-October 2026 is heavily driven by Douyin (TikTok China) viral trends, with short emotional Mandopop ballads dominating. 《此去半生》(This Half-life Onward / Cǐ qù bànshēng) is the most widely circulated track across short-video platforms, resonating deeply with younger listeners through its melancholic storytelling and nostalgic folk-pop arrangement. Jay Chou and Jolin Tsai maintain their perennial presence on TME platforms (QQ Music, Kugou, Kuwo), reflecting enduring loyalty to established Mandopop icons. The CN market continues to show a strong preference for emotionally resonant Mandopop ballads contrasting sharply with Western pop/country crossover trends dominating the US and EU.
