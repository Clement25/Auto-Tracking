## Highlights

### US
Ella Langley's country-pop anthem **"Choosin' Texas"** continues its historic run at #1, now at 23 consecutive weeks — an all-time Billboard Hot 100 record, surpassing Mariah Carey and the previous record tied the week prior. Stella Lefty's breakout indie-pop track **"Boston"** climbs to #2, making her one of the fastest-rising new artists of 2026. Ariana Grande's sultry R&B-pop cut **"Hate That I Made You Love Me"** holds firm at #4, underscoring her consistent commercial presence this year, while Morgan Wallen's crossover country banger **"Been By Now"** stays anchored at #3.

### EU
The UK chart tells a cross-Atlantic story: both **"Choosin' Texas"** (Ella Langley, #2) and **"Boston"** (Stella Lefty, #4) have made huge waves internationally, confirming US chart dominance crossing over. UK newcomer **Sienna Spiro** owns #1 with the dreamy alt-pop track **"Great Expectation"**, which has now topped the UK chart for multiple consecutive weeks. Electronic duo effort **"Movin' to the Sun"** (Hugel, Imael Angel & Ultra Naté) is a dancefloor favourite climbing the European streaming charts, while **"Rein Me In"** pairs indie icon Sam Fender with the soulful Olivia Dean for a stirring folk-pop collaboration.

### CN
China's QQ Music chart reflects a blend of emotional Mandopop and viral streaming hits. **"全世界的雨" (Rain All Over the World)** by Zhou Shen and Ella Chen is topping the chart with its soaring cinematic ballad style, driven by soundtrack tie-ins and short-video virality on Douyin. Duet track **"过海" (Crossing the Sea)** by Wang Heye and Huang Ling continues its upward momentum with raw vocal chemistry and heartfelt lyrics. Liu Yuning's island-themed romantic pop number **"来我的岛" (Come to My Island)** rounds out the top 5, showcasing the enduring popularity of warm-voiced male soloists in the mainland market. Notably, Japanese indie act 忘れらんねえよ's viral track **"なんもねえ"** charted at #3, illustrating the growing cross-cultural streaming traffic between Japan and China.
