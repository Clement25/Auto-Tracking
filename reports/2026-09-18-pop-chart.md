## Highlights

### US

Ella Langley is making chart history this week. "Choosin' Texas" logged its **22nd consecutive week at #1** on the Billboard Hot 100, tying Mariah Carey and Boyz II Men's record for the longest-running #1 in chart history, and was named Billboard's Song of the Summer 2026. Country-pop's grip on mainstream America is total: Langley also holds #4 alongside Morgan Wallen with their duet "I Can't Love You Anymore," which recently topped Country Airplay and made history as the first core-country duet to debut in the Hot 100 top 10. The previous week briefly saw all five top-five slots occupied by country acts — the first time in the chart's 68-year history. Stella Lefty's "Boston," a viral TikTok-origin country-indie crossover interpolating Noah Kahan's "Stick Season," holds at #2, while Taylor Swift's orchestral-pop Toy Story 5 contribution sits at #3. Ariana Grande's "Hate That I Made You Love Me" — her milestone 10th chart-topper in June — rounds out the top five as it gradually slides from its summer peak.

### EU

Sienna Spiro's "Great Expectation" holds the UK #1 for a second consecutive week. The London-born pop newcomer's debut single from her LP *Visitor* made her the youngest British female artist to claim a chart-topper in over a decade. US country-pop crossover continues unabated: Ella Langley's "Choosin' Texas" sits at #2, and Stella Lefty's "Boston" at #4 — three of the top five are American acts. Dance-house collaboration "Movin' to the Sun" by Hugel, Imael Angel & Ultra Naté holds at #3, a sun-soaked Eurodance revival that has been a summer radio staple across European markets. Sam Fender & Olivia Dean's indie-folk duet "Rein Me In" anchors at #5, representing the UK's homegrown talent amid heavy American crossover dominance.

### CN

Jay Chou (周杰伦) dominates the China chart in breathtaking fashion, placing three classic catalog tracks in the top five simultaneously — a direct halo effect from his 16th studio album *Children of the Sun* (太陽之子), released March 2026, which reportedly exceeded 100 million yuan in revenue and whose ongoing Carnival II World Tour is driving listeners back to his earlier work. "七里香" (Qilixiang, 2004) and "晴天" (Sunny Day, 2003) and "搁浅" (Stranded, 2003) all charted this week, with Jay placing 32 songs total in the Apple Music China top 100. Stefanie Sun (孙燕姿) bookends the top five at #1 and #5: her emotional ballad "我不难过" (I'm Not Sad) holds the top spot for a second week, riding the 2026 trend of nostalgia-driven ballads going viral via Douyin short-video snippets. The chart reflects China's strongly insular listening culture — not a single Western crossover track appears, while catalog music's dominance over new releases underlines the long-tail streaming behavior typical of Chinese platforms.
