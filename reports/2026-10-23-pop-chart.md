## Highlights

### US
Taylor Swift continues her dominance of the Billboard Hot 100, holding the #1 spot for a third consecutive week with "Patient Zero," a track from her latest album cycle that has resonated strongly with both casual listeners and long-time fans. Country crossover artist Ella Langley's "Choosin' Texas" remains a remarkable commercial force at #2 after 52+ weeks on the chart — one of the longest chart runs of 2026. The week also sees genre diversity in the top 5, with the psychedelic indie-pop collaboration "Dracula" by Tame Impala & K-pop star JENNIE climbing to a new peak at #5, reflecting the growing global crossover appeal between Western alternative acts and K-pop artists.

### EU
The UK chart mirrors US trends at the top, with Taylor Swift's "Patient Zero" ruling for a third week, topping both the streaming and sales charts simultaneously. Jamie Miller's "Babydoll" locks in at #2 after an earlier #1 peak, maintaining strong momentum. Rising newcomer Sienna Spiro consolidates her UK breakthrough with "Great Expectation" holding in the top 3, while ADÉLA's "Nicole Kidman" surges from #8 to #5, suggesting growing European enthusiasm for the Austrian singer-songwriter who first broke through in 2025.

### CN
The Chinese market this week is dominated by established C-pop idols and drama OSTs. Xiao Zhan's "异想天开" leads the Tencent Music 由你榜 (UNI Chart), riding the wave of its parent drama's premiere and a devoted fan base that drove massive streaming numbers in early October. Veteran vocalist Zhou Shen's "我们在场" continues to chart strongly, characteristic of his consistent presence on Chinese streaming platforms. Notably, Tan Jianci's English-titled "Trouble In My DMs" marks an interesting trend of C-pop artists experimenting with English-language releases to expand their reach.
