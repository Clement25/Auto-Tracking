## Highlights

### US
Ella Langley is having an extraordinary run — "Choosin' Texas" sits at #1 for a record-tying 14th consecutive week, matching Whitney Houston and Mariah Carey for the most weeks at #1 among solo women in Hot 100 history. She simultaneously holds #3 with "Be Her," a rare double-chart feat. Taylor Swift's "I Knew It, I Knew You," from the Toy Story 5 soundtrack, debuted at #1 on its release week before settling to #2, driven by Disney/Pixar's massive promotional campaign. Ariana Grande is building anticipation for her upcoming album *Petal* (due July 31) with "hate that i made you love me," her 10th chart-topper. The Tame Impala/Jennie (BLACKPINK) collab "Dracula" rounds out the top 5, marking Jennie's first solo US top 5 hit after a Jennie remix injected new life into the track earlier this year.

### EU
The UK chart is witnessing history: Sam Fender and Olivia Dean's "Rein Me In" has now spent 16 non-consecutive weeks at #1, officially becoming the longest-running #1 by a British act in UK chart history, surpassing Wet Wet Wet's 1994 record. The first dance/electronic track of 2026 to break into the UK top 5, ANOTR's "Talk to You" went viral after England footballer John Stones was filmed dancing to it in a post-match changing room celebration. American country crossover hit "Choosin' Texas" has translated across the Atlantic, confirming US country-pop's growing global reach. Harry Styles' breezy Americana-pop single "American Girls" continues a steady climb, and Ariana Grande's pre-album single mirrors its strong US performance.

### CN
Chinese charts this week reflect the enduring dominance of 古风 (gǔ fēng — ancient/classical style) ballads, particularly those tied to streaming drama OSTs. Ziyu's "凌绝顶" leads as a resolute, emotionally stirring breakout, while Zhou Shen's wuxia-themed "孤剑吟" showcases his signature high-register vocals in a period-drama soundscape. Zhang Guiyuan's "普通的歌" ("An Ordinary Song") resonates for its relatable emotionality, and Liu Yuning continues his consistent presence across Tencent platforms. K-pop crossover remains strong: aespa's "ATTITUDE" holds a top-5 position, reflecting the Korean wave's sustained grip on Chinese streaming audiences.
