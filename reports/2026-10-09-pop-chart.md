## Highlights

### US

Taylor Swift dominates the Billboard Hot 100 this week like no artist has in recent memory. Her deluxe release *The Life of a Showgirl: The Encore* (September 25) detonated across the chart, with "Patient Zero" debuting at #1 — her 16th Hot 100 number one, placing her third all-time behind The Beatles and Mariah Carey — while three other new tracks ("Cleveland!", "Babylon", "Pink Clouding") simultaneously entered at #3, #4, and #5. This ended the extraordinary 24-week reign of Ella Langley's country-pop crossover "Choosin' Texas," which had been one of the longest runs at #1 in years. Olivia Rodrigo's "Stupid Song" also re-entered the top 10, alongside Ariana Grande and Morgan Wallen holding steady.

### EU

The UK chart mirrors the US in one key way: Taylor Swift's "Patient Zero" debuted straight to #1, a transatlantic simultaneous chart-topper. But underneath Swift, British talent is thriving. Sienna Spiro, 20, holds #2 with "Great Expectation" from her debut album *Visitor* — she is the youngest British female solo act to top the UK chart since Becky Hill in 2014, having already claimed #1 earlier this run before being displaced by Swift. American singer-songwriter Ashe lands a career-best #3 with "Stop the Wedding!", released August 7 on Atlantic Records. Latin pop powerhouse KAROL G's "BbY WOW" (with Spanish artists Judeline and rusowsky) remains in the top tier after spending multiple weeks at #1 on the Billboard Global 200 and Spotify Global Daily chart in late August/September.

### CN

China's multi-platform chart (aggregating QQ Music, NetEase Cloud Music, Douyin, and Kuaishou) continues to be led by 龙友林's 《龙耀华夏》 ("Dragon Glory"), an epic patriotic anthem that accumulated 150 billion+ cross-platform plays and has dominated the 热歌榜 for months — a testament to Douyin's power in propelling nationalistic and inspirational content. 周深's (Zhou Shen) 《奔腾》 ("Gallop"), the officially commissioned People's Daily New Year theme track (45B+ plays), holds steady at #2, blending orchestral pop with patriotic resonance. Jay Chou remains a perennial force with new single 《那天下雨了》 cracking the multi-platform top 5. Short-form video platform Douyin continues to act as the primary hit-maker: 《玻璃》 ("Glass") trended across Douyin in mid-2026 and has maintained streaming momentum into autumn.
