## Highlights

### US
Ella Langley's country-pop anthem **"Choosin' Texas"** remains immovable at #1 on the Billboard Hot 100 for a record-tying 24th week, a remarkable display of longevity rarely seen in the streaming era. The chart also reflects genre diversity: Morgan Wallen represents country's continued mainstream dominance at #3, while the experimental collaboration **"Dracula"** between Tame Impala and K-pop star JENNIE at #5 signals a growing appetite for cross-genre, cross-cultural projects. Ariana Grande's brooding **"hate that i made you love me"** at #4 continues her post-hiatus resurgence.

### EU
The UK chart is topped this week by **Sienna Spiro's "Great Expectation"**, a British newcomer making waves with her indie-pop sound, signalling continued appetite for home-grown talent. US crossover hits are also prominent — Ella Langley (#2) and Stella Lefty (#3) both holding strong positions. Czech-Australian artist **E Adéla's "Nicole Kidman"** at #4 reflects Europe's diverse taste in quirky, viral pop. Dua Lipa's evergreen **"Training Season"** maintains a steady #5 presence, confirming her enduring European fanbase.

### CN
China's charts this week are dominated by **Yan Haoxiang (严浩翔)**, member of idol group Time Boys (时代少年团), whose emotional track **"Hope to Remain" (希望还在)** has spent 5 weeks on the China TME UNI Chart with a peak at #1 — boosted significantly by fan streams on QQ Music and Kugou. The **说唱巅峰对决2026 (Rap Summit Showdown 2026)** TV competition is also driving chart activity, with hip-hop and R&B flavours penetrating the mainstream pop charts. Douyin (Chinese TikTok) continues to serve as a powerful launchpad for viral tracks: DJ remixes and short-video dance challenges are pushing songs by artists like Ryan.B and JiNiubupi into the national conversation.
